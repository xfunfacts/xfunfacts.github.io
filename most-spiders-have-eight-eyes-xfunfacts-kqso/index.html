Most Spiders Have Eight Eyes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Most spiders have eight eyes, though their vision and eye arrangement vary widely between species.

Most spiders have eight eyes, although the number and arrangement vary among different species. Some spiders rely heavily on vision to hunt, while others depend more on vibrations, touch, or chemical signals. Jumping spiders possess especially sharp eyesight, allowing them to accurately judge distances before leaping onto prey. Other species have much poorer vision but compensate with highly sensitive hairs that detect movement. The wide variety of eye arrangements reflects the diverse lifestyles found throughout the spider world.

Did you know?

Jumping spiders have some of the sharpest vision among spiders, using large forward-facing eyes to judge distance and track prey accurately.
