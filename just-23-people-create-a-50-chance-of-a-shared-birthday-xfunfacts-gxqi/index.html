Just 23 People Create a 50% Chance of a Shared Birthday xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A group of just 23 people has about a 50% chance that two share the same birthday, a famous probability result.

The birthday paradox is a famous probability puzzle showing that a surprisingly small group of people can share the same birthday. With just 23 individuals, the chance that at least two people have matching birthdays is already about 50 percent. Many people expect the probability to be much lower because they compare one person with everyone else instead of considering every possible pair in the group. The example is widely used in mathematics and computer science to demonstrate how human intuition often struggles with probability.

Did you know?

With 23 people, the probability of at least one shared birthday is about 50.7%, assuming 365 equally likely birthdays and ignoring leap day.
