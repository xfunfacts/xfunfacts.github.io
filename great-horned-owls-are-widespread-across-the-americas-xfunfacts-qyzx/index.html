Great Horned Owls Are Widespread Across the Americas xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Great horned owls are among the most widespread birds of prey, living across much of North and South America.

The great horned owl is one of the most widespread owl species in North and South America, living in forests, deserts, mountains, wetlands, and even suburban areas. Easily recognized by its prominent feather tufts that resemble horns, this powerful predator hunts a wide variety of prey, including rabbits, rodents, birds, and reptiles. Excellent hearing, sharp vision, and silent flight allow it to hunt effectively in darkness. Its adaptability has enabled the species to thrive across an enormous geographic range. The great horned owl is considered one of the Americas' most successful birds of prey.

Did you know?

Great horned owls can adapt to forests, deserts, suburbs, and even city parks, helping explain their unusually wide distribution across the Americas.
