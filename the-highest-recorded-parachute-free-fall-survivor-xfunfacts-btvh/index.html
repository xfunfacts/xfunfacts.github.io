The Highest Recorded Parachute-Free Fall Survivor xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Vesna Vulovi? survived a 33,333-foot fall without a parachute, making it one of the most extraordinary survival stories ever recorded.

The story of Vesna Vulovi? remains one of the most extraordinary survival cases ever recorded in aviation history. In 1972, the Serbian flight attendant survived a fall of approximately 33,333 feet after an explosion caused her aircraft to break apart in midair. Trapped inside part of the plane, she crashed onto a snow-covered mountainside, where the impact was partially cushioned by trees and the terrain. Although she suffered numerous life-threatening injuries, including multiple fractures and temporary paralysis, she eventually recovered after spending sixteen months in the hospital. Her survival was later recognized by Guinness World Records as the highest fall survived without a parachute. Scientists continue to study the combination of circumstances that made the event possible, including aircraft structure, landing conditions, and pure chance. Vesna Vulovi?'s remarkable recovery has become an enduring symbol of human resilience and one of the most astonishing survival stories ever documented.

Did you know?

Vesna Vulovi? survived the 1972 JAT Flight 367 disaster after falling from extreme altitude, although investigators believe she remained trapped inside part of the aircraft during the descent.
