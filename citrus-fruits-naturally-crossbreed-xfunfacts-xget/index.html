Citrus Fruits Naturally Crossbreed xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many citrus fruits naturally hybridize, producing familiar varieties such as lemons, grapefruits, and numerous modern oranges.

Many familiar citrus fruits are natural hybrids created through crossbreeding over thousands of years. Grapefruits, lemons, and many oranges all originated from combinations of a small number of ancestral citrus species, including mandarins, pomelos, and citrons. Because citrus plants hybridize so easily, growers have developed hundreds of varieties with unique flavors, colors, and growing characteristics. This remarkable genetic flexibility continues to shape the fruits found in markets around the world.

Did you know?

Modern citrus fruits have a complicated hybrid ancestry involving a small number of ancestral species, including citron, mandarin, and pomelo.
