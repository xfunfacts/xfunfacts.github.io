Most Life on Earth Lives in the Ocean xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Most living organisms on Earth are found in the oceans, from microscopic plankton to giant whales living beneath the waves.

Scientists estimate that between 50 and 80 percent of all life on Earth lives in the oceans. Marine ecosystems contain an extraordinary variety of organisms, from microscopic plankton to giant whales, many of which remain undiscovered. The oceans also produce much of the oxygen we breathe through tiny photosynthetic organisms such as phytoplankton. Despite covering more than 70 percent of Earth's surface, large portions of the deep ocean remain unexplored. Every new expedition continues to reveal previously unknown species and ecosystems hidden beneath the waves.

Did you know?

The claim that most of Earth's life lives in the ocean depends on whether life is measured by species, biomass, abundance, or habitat volume, so no single percentage is universally valid.
