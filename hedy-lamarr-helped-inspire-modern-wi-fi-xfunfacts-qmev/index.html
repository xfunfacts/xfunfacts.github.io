Hedy Lamarr Helped Inspire Modern Wi-Fi xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Actress Hedy Lamarr co-invented technology that helped lay the foundation for modern Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and other wireless systems.

Hollywood actress Hedy Lamarr was also an accomplished inventor who co-developed frequency-hopping technology during World War II with composer George Antheil. Their system was designed to prevent radio-controlled torpedoes from being easily jammed by enemy forces. Although the invention was not fully appreciated at the time, its underlying principles later contributed to wireless technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and GPS. Lamarr's work is now recognized as an important milestone in both engineering and communications history.

Did you know?

Hedy Lamarr and composer George Antheil patented a frequency-hopping communication system in 1942 to resist radio interference, an early contribution to spread-spectrum technology rather than the direct invention of modern Wi-Fi.
