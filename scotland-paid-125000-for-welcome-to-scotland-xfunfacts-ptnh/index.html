Scotland Paid £125,000 for "Welcome to Scotland" xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Scotland once spent £125,000 searching for the perfect tourism slogan. The winning idea? Just two simple words.

Scotland's £125,000 tourism slogan became famous not because it was clever or controversial, but because it appeared remarkably simple. In 2007, the Scottish government invested approximately £125,000 in developing a new national branding campaign designed to strengthen the country's international image and attract more visitors. After extensive research, marketing analysis, and consultation, the winning slogan turned out to be just two words: "Welcome to Scotland." The announcement surprised many people, who questioned whether such a straightforward phrase justified the significant cost. Supporters argued that the expense covered far more than the slogan itself, including branding research, market testing, and long-term tourism strategy rather than simply paying for two words. The debate quickly became an example often cited in discussions about government spending, branding, and public relations. Whether viewed as a smart marketing investment or an expensive lesson in simplicity, the campaign demonstrates how branding involves much more than creating a memorable phrase. Sometimes the most recognizable slogans result from months of research, audience testing, and strategic planning rather than a sudden burst of creativity.

Did you know?

A widely repeated story claims Scotland spent £125,000 developing a tourism campaign associated with “Welcome to Scotland,” but the expenditure, wording, and circumstances require verification against contemporary records.
