The First Speeding Ticket Was for 8 MPH xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The first recorded speeding ticket was issued to a driver traveling just 8 miles per hour during the early days of automobiles.

The world's first recorded speeding ticket was issued in England in 1896 to a driver traveling at just 8 miles per hour in a zone with a 2-mile-per-hour speed limit. At the time, automobiles were still a new invention, and speed regulations reflected concerns about pedestrian safety and horse-drawn traffic. Modern vehicles travel much faster, but traffic laws continue serving the same purpose of protecting road users. This historic ticket marked the beginning of traffic enforcement as we know it today.

Did you know?

Early motoring laws were so restrictive that some places required cars to travel at walking speed, making an 8 mph speeding offense possible.
