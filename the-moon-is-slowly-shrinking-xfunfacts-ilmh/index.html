The Moon Is Slowly Shrinking xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Moon continues to shrink as its interior cools, creating moonquakes and giant surface cliffs.

As the Moon slowly cools over billions of years, its interior contracts, causing the surface to wrinkle and crack. This gradual shrinking creates cliffs known as thrust faults and can trigger moonquakes, some of which have been detected by instruments left behind during the Apollo missions. Unlike Earth, the Moon has no active plate tectonics, so these features preserve a long record of its geological evolution. Scientists continue studying these changes to better understand the Moon's past and its future stability.

Did you know?

As the Moon cools and contracts, its crust develops thrust faults called lobate scarps, evidence that geological change is still occurring today.
