Pluto Lives in the Kuiper Belt xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Pluto is one of the largest objects in the Kuiper Belt beyond Neptune. This icy region is home to countless frozen worlds.

Pluto is one of the largest and best-known members of the Kuiper Belt, a vast region of icy objects beyond Neptune's orbit. This distant ring contains millions of frozen bodies left over from the formation of the Solar System more than 4.5 billion years ago. Once considered the ninth planet, Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006 because it shares its orbital neighborhood with many similar objects. Even so, Pluto remains one of the most fascinating worlds in the outer Solar System, featuring mountains of water ice, frozen plains, and a surprisingly active surface.

Did you know?

The Kuiper Belt begins beyond Neptune and contains many icy bodies left over from the Solar System's formation, including several known dwarf planets.
