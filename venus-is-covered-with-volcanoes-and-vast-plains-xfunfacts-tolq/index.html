Venus Is Covered with Volcanoes and Vast Plains xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Venus is covered with volcanoes, mountains, and vast lava plains that reveal a dramatic geological history.

Radar observations from orbit have revealed that Venus is covered with thousands of volcanoes, broad volcanic plains, rugged mountain ranges, and massive lava flows. Because the planet's thick clouds permanently hide its surface from visible light, scientists depend on radar imaging to study its geology. Many regions appear relatively young, suggesting widespread volcanic resurfacing occurred in the distant past. Recent observations even hint that some volcanoes may still be active today. Understanding Venus' volcanic history helps researchers compare geological processes across rocky planets and better understand how planetary interiors evolve over billions of years.

Did you know?

Much of Venus appears to have been resurfaced by volcanism, leaving broad basaltic plains that cover a large fraction of the planet.
