Four Elements Make Up Most of the Human Body xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, and nitrogen make up about 96% of the human body's total mass.

About 96 percent of the human body's mass comes from just four elements: oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, and nitrogen. Oxygen accounts for the largest share because it is a major component of both water and many biological molecules. Carbon forms the backbone of proteins, carbohydrates, fats, and DNA, while hydrogen is found in water and nearly all organic compounds. Nitrogen is essential for amino acids and genetic material. Although the remaining elements make up only a small percentage of body weight, minerals such as calcium, phosphorus, potassium, and iron are still vital for healthy bones, muscles, nerves, and blood.

Did you know?

Oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, and nitrogen together account for about 96% of human body mass, with calcium and phosphorus making up much of the remainder.
