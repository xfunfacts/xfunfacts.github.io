Some Tarantulas Can Survive Years Without Food xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Some tarantulas can survive for years without food by relying on their slow metabolism and stored energy.

Certain tarantulas can survive for more than two years without food if water is available and environmental conditions remain favorable. Their slow metabolism allows them to conserve energy far more efficiently than many other animals. After consuming a large meal, a tarantula may remain inactive for extended periods while gradually digesting its prey. This adaptation helps them survive in habitats where food is unpredictable. Although they can withstand long fasting periods, tarantulas still require appropriate care and regular access to water when kept in captivity.

Did you know?

Tarantulas can dramatically reduce their metabolic rate during food shortages, helping them conserve energy for long periods between successful meals.
