Rhode Island Had the Longest Official State Name xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Rhode Island once had America's longest official state name: State of Rhode Island and Providence Plantations, before shortening it in 2020.

Although Rhode Island is the smallest U.S. state by land area, it was once known by the longest official state name in the country: "State of Rhode Island and Providence Plantations." The lengthy title reflected the union of two early colonial settlements, with Rhode Island referring to Aquidneck Island and Providence Plantations describing the mainland settlements founded by Roger Williams. In 2020, Rhode Island voters approved a constitutional amendment to shorten the official name to simply "State of Rhode Island," removing the phrase "Providence Plantations." The change was made because the word "plantations" had become widely associated with slavery, even though it originally referred to settlements rather than slave plantations in this historical context. The state's evolving name illustrates how history, language, and public opinion continue to shape official identities over time.
