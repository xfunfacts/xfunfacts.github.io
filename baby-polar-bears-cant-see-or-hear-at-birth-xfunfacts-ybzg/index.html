Baby Polar Bears Can't See or Hear at Birth xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Baby polar bears are born blind and deaf, depending entirely on their mother during the first weeks of life.

Newborn polar bear cubs are born blind and deaf, relying entirely on their mother's warmth and milk during the first weeks of life. Born inside insulated snow dens during the Arctic winter, the cubs weigh only about half a kilogram and are covered with fine white fur. Their eyes and ears gradually open during the first month, allowing them to slowly become aware of their surroundings while remaining protected inside the den. The mother survives on stored body fat throughout this period without hunting, devoting all her energy to caring for her young. When the family emerges in spring, the cubs are much stronger and begin learning the survival skills they will need in one of the harshest environments on Earth.

Did you know?

Polar bear cubs are usually born inside snow dens, where their mother keeps them sheltered until they are strong enough to venture outside.
