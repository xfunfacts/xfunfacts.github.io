Scrappy-Doo Disappeared After Massive Fan Backlash xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Scrappy-Doo became so unpopular with fans that the character largely disappeared from Scooby-Doo cartoons for decades.

Scrappy-Doo was introduced to the Scooby-Doo franchise in 1979 as Scooby's energetic young nephew, but his reception eventually became overwhelmingly negative among many fans. Intended to revive declining television ratings, the enthusiastic puppy initially succeeded in attracting viewers with his fearless personality and catchphrase, "Puppy Power!" Over time, however, many audiences found him irritating because of his reckless behavior and constant eagerness to confront dangerous monsters. The backlash became so widespread that the character gradually disappeared from animated Scooby-Doo productions after 1988. Later installments even included jokes and references acknowledging his unpopularity, making Scrappy-Doo one of the few fictional characters openly criticized within his own franchise. Although some newer fans have developed greater appreciation for the character, his disappearance remains one of animation's most famous examples of fan backlash significantly influencing the direction of a long-running series.

Did you know?

Scrappy-Doo helped keep Scooby-Doo commercially active during a difficult period, and his later disappearance reflected changing formats and creative choices as well as his growing unpopularity with some fans.
