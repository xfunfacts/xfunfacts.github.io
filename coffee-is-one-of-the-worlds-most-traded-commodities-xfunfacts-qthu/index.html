Coffee Is One of the World's Most Traded Commodities xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Coffee is one of the world's most traded agricultural commodities, supporting millions of farmers and businesses.

Coffee is one of the world's most heavily traded agricultural commodities and supports the livelihoods of millions of farmers, exporters, and retailers around the globe. Grown primarily in tropical regions known as the Coffee Belt, coffee beans are harvested, processed, roasted, and shipped to nearly every country. The two main commercial species are Arabica, valued for its smooth flavor, and Robusta, known for its stronger taste and higher caffeine content. Global coffee prices are influenced by weather, crop yields, transportation, and consumer demand, making coffee an important product in international trade as well as everyday life.

Did you know?

Coffee supports livelihoods across tropical farming regions, but much of its retail value is generated through roasting, branding, distribution, and sales rather than being received directly by growers.
