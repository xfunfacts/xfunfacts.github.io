Wales Is Home to an Incredibly Long Place Name xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Wales is home to Llanfairpwllgwyngyll, one of the longest place names in the English-speaking world and a famous tourist attraction.

The Welsh town officially known as Llanfairpwllgwyngyll is famous for having one of the longest place names in the English-speaking world. The full ceremonial name is even longer and was popularized during the nineteenth century as a way to attract visitors. Although the complete name describes local landmarks in the Welsh language, residents usually shorten it to simply Llanfair in everyday conversation. The town has become a popular tourist destination, with many visitors stopping to photograph its famously long railway station sign.

Did you know?

Llanfairpwllgwyngyll is famous for its long Welsh name, but the extended 58-character form was promoted in the nineteenth century partly as a publicity device.
