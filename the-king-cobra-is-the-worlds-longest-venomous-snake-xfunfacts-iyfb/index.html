The King Cobra Is the World's Longest Venomous Snake xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The king cobra is the world's longest venomous snake and can grow beyond 4 meters while primarily feeding on other snakes.

The king cobra is the world's longest venomous snake and can grow to more than four meters in length, with exceptional individuals exceeding five meters. Unlike most cobras, the king cobra primarily feeds on other snakes, including venomous species. When threatened, it can raise a large portion of its body above the ground, spread its hood, and produce a deep warning hiss. Despite its powerful venom, the snake usually avoids confrontation with humans whenever possible. Its intelligence, nesting behavior, and impressive size make it one of the most fascinating reptiles on Earth.

Did you know?

King cobras build nests for their eggs, an unusual behavior among snakes, and females may guard the nest until shortly before hatching.
