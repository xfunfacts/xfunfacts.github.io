Your Body Can Produce Alcohol xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Auto-brewery syndrome is a rare condition in which microbes inside the gut produce alcohol from carbohydrates after meals.

Although it sounds impossible, the human body can produce alcohol under rare medical conditions known as auto-brewery syndrome. In affected individuals, certain yeasts or other microbes living in the digestive system ferment carbohydrates into ethanol, causing alcohol to enter the bloodstream even when no alcoholic drinks have been consumed. People with this condition may experience symptoms similar to intoxication after eating foods rich in sugars or starches. Because the disorder is extremely uncommon, it is often difficult to diagnose. Studying auto-brewery syndrome has helped scientists better understand the complex relationship between the human body and the microorganisms that live within it.

Did you know?

Auto-brewery syndrome occurs when gut microbes ferment carbohydrates into ethanol, sometimes producing measurable blood alcohol even without drinking alcoholic beverages.
