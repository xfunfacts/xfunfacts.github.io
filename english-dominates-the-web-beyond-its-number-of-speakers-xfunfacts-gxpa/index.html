English Dominates the Web Beyond Its Number of Speakers xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] English represents about half of the world's most-visited webpages despite being spoken by only about a quarter of internet users. It remains the dominant language of the web.

Although only about one-quarter of internet users primarily speak English, the language dominates the web far beyond its share of speakers. Roughly half of the world's most visited webpages are published in English, making it the most influential language for online information, business, science, and technology. Russian ranks a distant second at around six percent of websites, followed by several other major languages. English became the internet's dominant language largely because of the early growth of the web in English-speaking countries and its widespread use as an international language. Today, millions of people use English online even when it is not their native language.

Did you know?

English accounts for a disproportionately large share of web content, but its exact percentage varies by measurement method and changes as internet use expands globally.
