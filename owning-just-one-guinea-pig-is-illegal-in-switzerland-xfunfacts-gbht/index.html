Owning Just One Guinea Pig Is Illegal in Switzerland xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] In Switzerland, owning a single guinea pig is against the law because these social animals are considered unhappy living alone.

Switzerland is well known for its strong animal welfare laws, including an unusual rule involving guinea pigs. Because guinea pigs are highly social animals that naturally live in groups, keeping only one guinea pig by itself is considered harmful to its well-being. Swiss animal protection regulations therefore make it illegal to own a single guinea pig without providing companionship from another of its species. The law reflects scientific understanding that prolonged social isolation can cause stress, anxiety, and behavioral problems in animals that evolved to live together. Some pet owners even temporarily rent guinea pigs to provide companionship if one animal dies while another remains alive. Switzerland's approach highlights the country's commitment to considering the psychological as well as physical welfare of domestic animals. The regulation has become one of the world's most famous examples of legislation recognizing the importance of social needs in animal care.

Did you know?

Guinea pigs communicate socially with whistles, purrs, squeaks, and body movements, which is one reason companionship is important for their welfare.
