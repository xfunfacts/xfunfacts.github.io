Mohammed Is One of the World's Most Common Names xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Mohammed and spelling variations such as Muhammad and Mohammad rank among the most common personal names used by families around the world.

Mohammed, along with its many spelling variations such as Muhammad, Mohamed, and Mohammad, is among the most common personal names in the world. The name honors the Prophet Muhammad and has been used by millions of families across diverse cultures and countries for centuries. Differences in spelling arise because Arabic names are transliterated into many different writing systems and languages. When all common variations are considered together, the name ranks among the most widely used globally. Its remarkable popularity reflects the worldwide presence of Muslim communities and the enduring cultural, religious, and historical significance associated with the name.

Did you know?

Muhammad and its many transliterations, including Mohammed and Mohammad, are among the world’s most widely used given names, though global counts depend heavily on spelling conventions.
