Your Fingers Have No Muscles xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Your fingers contain no muscles, relying instead on tendons connected to muscles in your hands and forearms.

Although your fingers move constantly throughout the day, they contain no muscles of their own. Instead, muscles located in the palms and forearms pull on long tendons that extend into each finger, creating precise movements. This arrangement allows the fingers to remain lightweight while maintaining remarkable strength and flexibility. The complex coordination between muscles, tendons, ligaments, and nerves enables everything from typing and writing to playing musical instruments.

Did you know?

Finger bones are moved mainly by tendons attached to muscles in the forearm and hand, although small muscles located within the hand contribute importantly to precise finger movements.
