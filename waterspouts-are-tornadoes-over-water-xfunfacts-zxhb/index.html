Waterspouts Are Tornadoes Over Water xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Waterspouts are tornado-like rotating columns of air that form over water and can create dangerous conditions for boats.

A waterspout is a rotating column of air that forms over water and resembles a tornado. Some waterspouts develop beneath thunderstorms and behave similarly to tornadoes, while others form in relatively fair weather under different atmospheric conditions. Although many are weaker than powerful land tornadoes, waterspouts can still produce strong winds, dangerous waves, and hazards for boats and coastal communities. They usually weaken quickly after moving onto land because they lose the warm, moist conditions that help sustain them.

Did you know?

Fair-weather waterspouts can form beneath developing cumulus clouds without a supercell, while tornadic waterspouts are associated with severe thunderstorms.
