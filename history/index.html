History of uwarboretum.org

Today, uwarboretum.org is home to Fun Facts, an independently operated educational web application. This domain previously served as a website for the University of Wisconsin - Madison Arboretum. The information below explains that earlier use and the purpose of the website today.

The Domain’s Earlier History

For many years, uwarboretum.org provided information about the University of Wisconsin - Madison Arboretum in Madison, Wisconsin, USA. Visitors used the website to explore the Arboretum’s natural areas and plant collections, read about ecological research, and find educational resources, visitor information, and community events.

The Arboretum was formally dedicated on June 17, 1934. With Aldo Leopold as its first research director, it helped advance the practice of ecological restoration: repairing damaged ecosystems and re-establishing native plant communities. Its work connects scientific research with land stewardship, conservation, and public education.

Older publications, research references, and community websites may still contain links to this domain because of that earlier role.

Looking for the UW–Madison Arboretum?

The Arboretum’s official website is now available at arboretum.wisc.edu. Please use that website for current information about visiting, trails, gardens, research, educational programs, events, and supporting the Arboretum.

The Fun Facts application currently operating at uwarboretum.org is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the University of Wisconsin - Madison or its Arboretum.

The Website Today: Learning Through Fun Facts

Under its current independent operation, uwarboretum.org has a new purpose: making interesting knowledge easy to explore through the Fun Facts application.

Our educational goal is to help people learn something new in the course of an ordinary day. The collection covers animals, science and nature, space, places, people and history, and culture and everyday life. Short facts provide an accessible starting point, while accompanying explanations add context and encourage further exploration.

The experience centers on a simple random fact feature. With a click, you can encounter an unfamiliar subject, read something surprising, and move on to another fact. You can also choose a topic and explore an area that already interests you. Each visit offers an opportunity to broaden your knowledge at your own pace.

A Clear, Visual Learning Experience

We approach user experience as part of the learning process. Readable text, visual topic categories, illustrations, and straightforward navigation help make the collection easy to understand and explore.

Our aim is to keep the focus on the knowledge itself. You should be able to recognize the subject, read the fact, explore its explanation, and find your next point of interest through clear, familiar controls.

Whether you read a single fact or spend time exploring several subjects, Fun Facts is designed to make everyday curiosity a simple, enjoyable learning habit.
