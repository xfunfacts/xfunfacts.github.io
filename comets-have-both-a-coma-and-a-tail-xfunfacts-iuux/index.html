Comets Have Both a Coma and a Tail xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] As comets approach the Sun, they develop a glowing coma and dramatic tails of gas and dust, creating one of space's most beautiful sights.

As a comet approaches the Sun, increasing heat causes frozen gases and ice inside its nucleus to vaporize. This process creates a glowing cloud surrounding the nucleus known as the coma. Solar radiation and the solar wind then push gas and dust away from the comet, forming one or more spectacular tails that can stretch for millions of kilometers. The dust tail and ion tail often point in different directions because they are affected by different physical forces. These brilliant features make comets some of the most beautiful and recognizable objects visible in the night sky.

Did you know?

A comet's coma can grow larger than Earth even though the solid nucleus at its center may be only a few kilometers across.
