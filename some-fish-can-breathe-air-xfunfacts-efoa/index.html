Some Fish Can Breathe Air xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Some fish, including lungfish, can breathe air directly, helping them survive in low-oxygen environments.

Some fish, including lungfish and several catfish species, can breathe oxygen directly from the air when water conditions become unfavorable. This adaptation allows them to survive in ponds and rivers with very low oxygen levels or even during temporary droughts. Lungfish possess specialized organs that function similarly to primitive lungs, enabling them to rely less on their gills. These remarkable abilities provide an important evolutionary link between aquatic and land-dwelling vertebrates. Air breathing has allowed certain fish to thrive in challenging environments.

Did you know?

Air-breathing fish use several different organs, including lungs, modified swim bladders, intestines, or skin, depending on the species and habitat.
