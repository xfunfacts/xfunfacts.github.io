An Ostrich's Eye Is Bigger Than Its Brain xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] An ostrich's eye is larger than its brain, giving the world's biggest bird exceptional vision for spotting predators.

The ostrich has the largest eyes of any living land animal, and each eye is actually bigger than its brain. Measuring around five centimeters across, these enormous eyes give ostriches exceptional eyesight that allows them to spot predators from great distances across open grasslands. Excellent vision is especially important because ostriches rely on speed rather than flight to escape danger. Their powerful legs can carry them at speeds exceeding 40 miles per hour, while their oversized eyes help detect threats early enough to begin running.

Did you know?

Ostriches have long eyelashes that help protect their eyes from dust and airborne particles in the dry, open environments where they live.
