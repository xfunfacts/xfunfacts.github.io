Polar Bears Are the Largest Land Carnivores xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Polar bears are the world's largest land carnivores, perfectly adapted for hunting in the Arctic environment.

Polar bears are the largest land carnivores on Earth, with adult males often weighing several hundred kilograms and standing over eight feet tall when upright. They occupy the top of the Arctic food chain, feeding primarily on seals that provide the high-fat diet needed for survival in freezing conditions. Their massive size, strength, and hunting skills allow them to thrive in one of the planet's most demanding environments. Despite their power, polar bears face increasing challenges as climate change reduces the sea ice they depend on for hunting.

Did you know?

Polar bears are marine mammals in ecology and spend much of their lives on sea ice, where they hunt seals rather than terrestrial prey.
