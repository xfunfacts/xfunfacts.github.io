A Blue Whale Can Weigh as Much as 30 Elephants xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A blue whale can weigh as much as 30 elephants, making it the largest animal known to have ever lived on Earth.

A blue whale can weigh as much as 30 elephants while stretching the length of three large tour buses, making it the largest animal known to have ever lived on Earth. Despite its immense size, the blue whale feeds primarily on tiny shrimp-like animals called krill by filtering enormous volumes of seawater through baleen plates. Its heart alone can weigh hundreds of kilograms, and its calls are among the loudest sounds produced by any animal. These gentle giants migrate thousands of kilometers each year between feeding and breeding grounds across the world's oceans.

Did you know?

Blue whales mainly eat krill, and a single adult can consume several tons of these tiny crustaceans during a productive feeding day.
