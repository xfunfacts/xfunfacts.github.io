Pilots and Co-Pilots Usually Eat Different Meals xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many airlines have pilots and co-pilots eat different meals to reduce the unlikely risk of food poisoning affecting both.

Many airlines encourage or require pilots and co-pilots to eat different meals during flights to reduce the unlikely risk of both becoming ill from contaminated food. By choosing separate menu options, airlines add another layer of safety to flight operations. Although food poisoning among flight crews is extremely rare, aviation relies on multiple backup systems to minimize every possible risk. This simple precaution reflects the industry's commitment to keeping qualified crew members available throughout the journey.

Did you know?

Some airlines separate pilot meal choices as a risk-reduction practice, but this is an airline policy rather than a universal aviation rule.
