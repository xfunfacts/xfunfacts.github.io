Most Household Dust Comes From Dead Skin Cells xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A significant portion of household dust comes from tiny flakes of dead skin shed by people and pets.

When Ellis Island opened in 1892, Annie Moore became the first immigrant officially processed there after arriving from Ireland with her two younger brothers aboard the SS Nevada. She was greeted with a commemorative coin and quickly became a symbol of new beginnings in America. Over the following decades, more than 12 million immigrants passed through Ellis Island in search of better opportunities. Annie Moore's arrival remains one of the most recognizable moments in U.S. immigration history.

Did you know?

Household dust contains skin flakes, textile fibers, soil, pollen, microbes, and other particles, so dead skin is only one component rather than necessarily the majority everywhere.
