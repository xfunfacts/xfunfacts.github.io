Koalas Eat Toxic Eucalyptus Leaves xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Koalas survive on eucalyptus leaves that are toxic to most animals thanks to specialized digestive adaptations.

Koalas survive on a diet consisting mainly of eucalyptus leaves, which contain chemicals that are toxic to most other animals. Specialized digestive adaptations and unique gut bacteria allow koalas to safely process these leaves and obtain enough nutrients to survive. Out of hundreds of eucalyptus species, koalas select only certain varieties that provide the best balance of nutrition and lower toxin levels. Because eucalyptus leaves are low in energy, koalas must conserve much of their strength. Their unusual diet makes them one of Australia's most specialized mammals.

Did you know?

Young koalas consume a special soft fecal material called pap from their mothers, helping establish gut microbes needed to digest eucalyptus leaves.
