Redheads Aren't Going Extinct xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Natural red hair remains rare, but the genes responsible continue to be inherited and are not disappearing.

Natural red hair is one of the rarest hair colors in the world, but it is not disappearing. Red hair is primarily associated with variations in the MC1R gene, which can be passed through generations even when parents do not have red hair themselves. Because the gene is recessive, it may remain hidden for generations before appearing again in children. Genetic diversity ensures that red hair will continue to exist as long as these inherited gene variants remain part of the human population.

Did you know?

Red hair is usually linked to variants of the MC1R gene and can persist for generations because recessive traits can be carried by people who do not have red hair themselves.
