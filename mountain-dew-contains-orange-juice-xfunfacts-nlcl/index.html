Mountain Dew Contains Orange Juice xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Some Mountain Dew recipes contain orange juice concentrate. The citrus ingredient helps create its signature flavor despite the drink's appearance.

Many people think of Mountain Dew as a lemon-lime soft drink, but some formulations include orange juice concentrate among their ingredients. Depending on the country and recipe, the concentrate may appear relatively high on the ingredient list, often following carbonated water and sweeteners. The orange juice contributes subtle citrus notes that blend with other flavorings rather than creating an obvious orange taste. Beverage recipes frequently combine multiple fruit concentrates to achieve a balanced and recognizable flavor profile. Manufacturers may also adjust ingredients over time to meet changing regulations, consumer preferences, or regional markets. Reading ingredient labels often reveals surprising details that are impossible to detect from taste alone. Mountain Dew's recipe demonstrates how familiar products can contain unexpected ingredients that help create their distinctive flavor without dominating the final taste.

Did you know?

The original U.S. Mountain Dew formula lists concentrated orange juice among its ingredients, alongside citrus flavorings and acids, although recipes differ across countries and product varieties.
