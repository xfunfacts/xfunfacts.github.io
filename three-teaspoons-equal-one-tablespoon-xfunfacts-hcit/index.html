Three Teaspoons Equal One Tablespoon xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Three teaspoons equal one tablespoon, one of the most useful kitchen conversions for accurate cooking and successful baking.

In standard cooking measurements, three teaspoons equal one tablespoon, making this one of the most useful kitchen conversions to remember. Accurate measuring is especially important in baking, where even small differences in ingredient amounts can affect texture, flavor, and consistency. Measuring spoons are standardized in many countries to ensure recipes produce predictable results. Professional chefs often weigh ingredients for maximum precision, but volume measurements such as teaspoons and tablespoons remain the most common system in home kitchens around the world.

Did you know?

In U.S. customary cooking measurements, one tablespoon equals three teaspoons, or approximately 14.8 milliliters, but Australian standard tablespoons contain 20 milliliters, making regional recipe conventions important.
