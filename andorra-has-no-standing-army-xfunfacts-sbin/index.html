Andorra Has No Standing Army xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Andorra has no permanent standing army but has historically relied on citizens for defense. Weapons could be issued if the country ever needed protection.

Andorra is one of the few countries in the world without a permanent standing army. Nestled between France and Spain in the Pyrenees Mountains, the small principality has traditionally relied on diplomacy and defense agreements with neighboring nations for its security. Historically, however, citizens could be called upon to help defend the country if necessary, and weapons could be issued during emergencies. Today, public security is handled primarily by the national police rather than military forces. Andorra's long history of neutrality, political stability, and international cooperation has allowed it to exist peacefully without maintaining a conventional army. Its unique defense system reflects the country's distinctive place in European history.

Did you know?

Andorra relies on its police for domestic security and maintains defense arrangements involving neighboring France and Spain, while its ceremonial forces preserve military traditions without constituting a conventional standing army.
