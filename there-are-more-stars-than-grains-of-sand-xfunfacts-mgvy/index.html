There Are More Stars Than Grains of Sand xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The observable universe contains more stars than all the grains of sand on Earth's beaches combined.

Astronomers estimate that the observable universe contains more stars than there are grains of sand on all of Earth's beaches. Current estimates suggest there may be hundreds of sextillions of stars spread across billions of galaxies, making the total almost impossible to comprehend. Even our own Milky Way contains hundreds of billions of stars, many with their own planetary systems. As telescopes become more powerful, scientists continue discovering previously unseen galaxies and refining these estimates. The immense number of stars highlights the extraordinary scale of the universe.

Did you know?

Comparisons between stars and grains of sand are order-of-magnitude estimates, because neither the total number of stars nor Earth's sand grains can be counted exactly.
