Massachusetts Is the Birthplace of Basketball and Volleyball xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Both basketball and volleyball were invented in Massachusetts, giving the state a unique place in sports history.

Massachusetts holds a unique place in sports history as the birthplace of both basketball and volleyball. Basketball was invented in 1891 by Dr. James Naismith at a YMCA training school in Springfield, while volleyball was created just a few years later in 1895 by William G. Morgan in Holyoke. Both sports were designed as indoor activities that could be played during the winter months, yet they eventually grew into two of the world's most popular games. Today, millions of athletes participate in basketball and volleyball at every level, from local recreation to international competition.
