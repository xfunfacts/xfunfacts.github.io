Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Fun Facts is an independently operated educational web application at uwarboretum.org. This policy explains how information is handled when you browse facts, use the website’s features, or contact us.

You do not need an account to use Fun Facts. We do not provide registration forms or public comments, and you do not need to give us your name or email address to read facts.

Information Stored in Your Browser

Your browser stores information that supports the website’s features:

  • Viewed facts: A list covering the facts you opened during the last 30 days. It helps show your daily discovery count and reduce repeated facts. Older entries are removed automatically.
  • Saved facts: The facts you mark with Save remain in your browser until you remove them or clear this website’s stored data.
  • Selected topic: Your topic selection is stored for the current browsing-tab session.

We do not upload your browser’s complete viewing history or saved-facts list to our servers.

Requests and Fact Counts

When you request another random fact, the website receives the selected topic and the identifier of the current fact so it can return another one.

Opening a fact increases that fact’s total view count. Saving or removing a saved fact updates its total save count, which requires sending the fact identifier and the save or remove action.

We keep these as aggregate counts for each fact and do not use them to create individual reader profiles.

Technical Information and IP Addresses

Delivering the website and its images requires processing connection information, including IP addresses and requested URLs. Hosting and content-delivery providers may process and retain technical information under their service policies.

We do not use IP addresses for advertising or to build profiles of individual readers.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

We use a security-related session cookie to help protect the website against forged requests. This cookie is intended for the browsing session and is not used for advertising or tracking you across other websites.

Fun Facts does not display advertisements or load third-party analytics scripts, advertising networks, or social media tracking pixels.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies or browser storage may affect some website features.

Information You Send by Email

If you email [email protected], we receive your email address, any sender name included with the message, and the contents and attachments you choose to send.

We use this information to respond to questions, review corrections, consider suggestions, and address website problems. We do not use your correspondence for advertising.

Our mailbox uses Gmail, so messages are also processed through Google’s email service. You can read Google’s Privacy Policy for information about its handling of data.

We retain correspondence only for as long as needed to address the matter or meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of personal information you have sent.

Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Service providers involved in hosting, delivering content, and handling email may process information needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Children’s Privacy

Fun Facts includes a collection for children. Reading facts and using the random fact feature do not require a child to submit a name, email address, school, home address, or phone number.

Children should not send personal details by email. We encourage a parent or guardian to contact us on behalf of younger readers, especially children under 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without any legally required parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

A parent or guardian can contact us to ask about personal information held about their child, request its deletion, or request that we stop further use or collection of that information.

Your Choices

You can remove saved facts using the Save control. You can also clear this website’s data in your browser settings to remove locally stored viewing history and saved facts.

Clearing browser data does not remove the website’s aggregate fact counts, which are not kept as individual reader records.

For questions or requests concerning information you have sent by email, contact us at [email protected].

Changes to This Policy

If our information practices change, we will update this policy and the date shown above.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, email [email protected] or visit our Contact page.
