Hawaii Is America's Coffee-Growing State xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hawaii is the only U.S. state with commercial coffee production, supported by volcanic soil and an ideal tropical climate.

Hawaii is the only U.S. state with a climate suitable for commercial coffee production. Rich volcanic soil, abundant rainfall, and warm tropical temperatures create ideal growing conditions, particularly on the Big Island, where the famous Kona coffee is produced. Hawaiian coffee is prized worldwide for its smooth flavor and careful cultivation methods. Although coffee can grow in small quantities elsewhere in the United States, Hawaii remains the country's only major commercial coffee-producing state, supplying specialty beans that are exported around the globe.

Did you know?

Hawaiian coffee includes famous growing regions such as Kona, where elevation, volcanic soils, cloud cover, and mild temperatures support high-quality beans.
