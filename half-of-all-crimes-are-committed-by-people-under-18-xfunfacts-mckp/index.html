Half of All Crimes Are Committed by People Under 18 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] In some crime statistics, people under 18 account for a significant share of offenses, emphasizing the importance of youth prevention programs.

Crime statistics in some regions have shown that a significant share of recorded offenses involve offenders under the age of 18, although the exact proportion varies by country, crime type, and reporting period. Juvenile crime is influenced by many social factors, including education, family environment, economic conditions, and community support. Many justice systems emphasize rehabilitation for young offenders because adolescence is a period of continued psychological and social development. Early intervention programs, mentoring, and educational opportunities have been shown to reduce repeat offending and improve long-term outcomes for many young people.

Did you know?

People under 18 do not account for half of all crimes in U.S. national statistics; the claim confuses different measures, such as arrests, reported offenses, and selected crime categories, and should not be presented as an established fact.
