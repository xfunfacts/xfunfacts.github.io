Nebraska Is Triply Landlocked xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Nebraska is the only U.S. state that is triply landlocked, lying at least three states away from any ocean in every direction.

Nebraska is the only U.S. state considered triply landlocked, meaning that in every direction, travelers must cross at least three states before reaching an ocean. Its central location places it farther from any coastline than every other state in the country. Although this geographic distinction has little effect on daily life, it illustrates just how deep within the North American continent Nebraska lies. The state's landscape is instead shaped by rivers, plains, agriculture, and some of the world's most productive farmland.

Did you know?

Nebraska is considered triply landlocked because reaching an ocean requires crossing at least three state boundaries, distinguishing it from states that border or lie closer to coastal regions.
