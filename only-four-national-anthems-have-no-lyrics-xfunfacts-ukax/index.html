Only Four National Anthems Have No Lyrics xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Only four countries currently have official national anthems without lyrics, relying entirely on music to represent national identity.

Most national anthems include lyrics that citizens sing during ceremonies and sporting events, but only four countries currently use official national anthems without words. These instrumental anthems are performed entirely through music, allowing them to represent national identity without relying on language. Instrumental anthems can also avoid issues related to multiple official languages or changing political circumstances. Although lyrics may exist for historical or ceremonial purposes in some cases, the officially adopted versions remain wordless, making them a fascinating exception among the world's national symbols.

Did you know?

Counts of lyric-free national anthems vary because some countries have official instrumental anthems while others have unofficial, disputed, or optional lyrics, so “only four” is not a stable universal figure.
