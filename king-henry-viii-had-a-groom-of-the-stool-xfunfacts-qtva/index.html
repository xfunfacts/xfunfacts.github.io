King Henry VIII Had a Groom of the Stool xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] King Henry VIII's Groom of the Stool held an unusual role that also carried surprising political influence.

Among King Henry VIII's royal household was an official known as the Groom of the Stool, a position that involved assisting the king with highly personal daily routines, including using the toilet. Although the title sounds humorous today, it was actually considered one of great trust and influence because the official spent significant private time with the monarch. Holders of the position often gained political power and close access to the king, allowing them to influence important decisions within the royal court. The role demonstrates how unusual responsibilities could become prestigious in medieval and Tudor governments.

Did you know?

The Groom of the Stool attended to the king's private bodily needs and enjoyed exceptional access to the monarch, making the position surprisingly influential in Tudor court politics.
