Your Skin Is the Largest Organ in the Human Body xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Skin is the largest organ in the human body, protecting, regulating, and sensing the world around us.

Human skin is the body's largest organ, covering an average adult with about 20 square feet of protective tissue. It serves as a barrier against harmful microbes, helps regulate body temperature through sweating and blood flow, prevents excessive water loss, and contains millions of sensory receptors that detect touch, pressure, pain, and temperature. Skin also plays an essential role in producing vitamin D when exposed to sunlight. Keeping skin healthy through proper care and sun protection is important for overall health because it performs so many vital functions.

Did you know?

Adult skin covers roughly 1.5 to 2 square meters and serves as a protective barrier while supporting temperature regulation, immune defense, and sensory perception.
