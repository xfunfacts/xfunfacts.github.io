Male and Female Turkeys Have Different Names xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Learn why male turkeys are called toms and females hens, plus how these birds raise young and interact in the wild.

Male turkeys are called toms, while females are known as hens, a naming system similar to several other bird species. During the breeding season, toms display colorful feathers, fan their tails, and perform elaborate courtship behaviors to attract mates. Hens are responsible for building nests, laying eggs, and raising the young poults with little help from males. Understanding these differences helps wildlife biologists study turkey populations and breeding success. These distinct roles have helped wild turkeys thrive across North America.

Did you know?

Young male turkeys are often called jakes, while young females are called jennies before they mature into adult toms and hens.
