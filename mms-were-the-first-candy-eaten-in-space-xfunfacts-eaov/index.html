M&M's Were the First Candy Eaten in Space xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] M&M's became the first candy eaten in space thanks to their hard shell, which made them ideal for astronauts in zero gravity.

M&M's became the first candy eaten in space when astronauts brought them aboard early space missions. Their hard candy shell prevented chocolate from melting or creating messy crumbs, making them especially practical in microgravity. The colorful treats quickly became a favorite snack among astronauts because they were easy to handle inside spacecraft and required no special preparation. Over the years, M&M's have appeared on numerous NASA missions, becoming a small but memorable part of spaceflight history. This simple design demonstrates how even familiar foods sometimes need unique qualities to work successfully beyond Earth.

Did you know?

Astronauts commonly call M&M's "candy-coated chocolates," and their shells help reduce loose crumbs that could float into equipment in microgravity.
