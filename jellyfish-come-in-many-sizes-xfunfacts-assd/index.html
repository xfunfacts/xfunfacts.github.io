Jellyfish Come in Many Sizes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Jellyfish range from tiny species under an inch wide to giants measuring more than six feet across.

Jellyfish come in an astonishing range of sizes, from tiny species measuring less than an inch across to giants with bells over six feet wide. Some of the largest species also possess tentacles that stretch for more than one hundred feet beneath the water. Size often depends on the species, habitat, and stage of development. Despite these differences, all jellyfish share the same basic body design, consisting of a soft bell-shaped body and trailing tentacles. Their diversity makes them one of the most fascinating groups of ocean animals.

Did you know?

Jellyfish vary enormously in size, from species only millimeters across to giant forms with bells over a meter wide and extremely long tentacles.
