The First Rugby Club Was Founded in 1839 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Guy's Hospital Rugby Football Club, founded in 1839, is widely recognized as the oldest rugby club still in existence.

Guy's Hospital Rugby Football Club, founded in 1839 in London, is widely recognized as the oldest rugby club still in existence. Established decades before organized international rugby competitions, the club played an important role in the early development of the sport. Rugby itself evolved from football-style games played at English schools before formal rules gradually emerged. Today the sport is enjoyed by millions of players and fans around the world, with major international tournaments including the Rugby World Cup attracting audiences from every continent.

Did you know?

Guy's Hospital RFC is often cited as the oldest surviving rugby club, although historical claims depend on how continuous existence and club identity are defined.
