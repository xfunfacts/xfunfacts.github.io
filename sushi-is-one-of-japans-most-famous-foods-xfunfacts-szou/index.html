Sushi Is One of Japan's Most Famous Foods xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Sushi combines vinegared rice with fresh ingredients, making it one of Japan's most celebrated traditional dishes worldwide.

Sushi is one of Japan's most famous traditional foods, combining vinegared rice with ingredients such as raw or cooked seafood, vegetables, egg, and seaweed. Although many people associate sushi primarily with raw fish, the seasoned rice is actually the defining element of the dish. Over centuries, sushi evolved from an ancient method of preserving fish into the diverse cuisine enjoyed around the world today. Regional styles and creative variations continue to develop while maintaining traditional preparation techniques. Sushi remains an important symbol of Japanese culinary culture.

Did you know?

Sushi originally developed from methods of preserving fish with fermented rice, but modern Japanese sushi centers on seasoned rice, which may accompany seafood, vegetables, eggs, or other ingredients.
