Lake Superior Holds 10% of Surface Freshwater xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Lake Superior contains about 10% of the world's surface freshwater. It is one of the largest and most important freshwater lakes on Earth.

Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake in the world by surface area and contains roughly 10 percent of all the liquid surface freshwater on Earth. Stretching across the border between the United States and Canada, the lake covers an area comparable to the size of South Carolina and holds approximately 2,900 cubic miles of water. Its immense volume helps regulate regional weather, supports diverse ecosystems, and provides drinking water for millions of people. Lake Superior also reaches depths of more than 1,300 feet, making it one of the deepest freshwater lakes in North America. Because of its size, the lake behaves almost like an inland sea, producing large waves and sudden storms that have claimed numerous ships throughout history. Its extraordinary scale makes Lake Superior one of the planet's most remarkable freshwater resources.

Did you know?

Lake Superior contains approximately 12,100 cubic kilometers of water, roughly 10% of the world's fresh surface water held in lakes, and its enormous volume moderates surrounding regional temperatures.
