Most Hard Liquor Is Naturally Vegan xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Most distilled hard liquor is naturally vegan because distillation removes nearly all animal-derived ingredients. Many major spirits contain no animal products.

Nearly all major brands of distilled hard liquor are considered vegan because the distillation process typically removes proteins and other animal-derived ingredients from the finished product. Spirits such as vodka, whiskey, rum, gin, tequila, and most brandy generally contain only alcohol, water, and flavor compounds after distillation. However, some alcoholic beverages may not be vegan if animal-based ingredients are added afterward for flavoring, coloring, or clarification. Unlike many wines and beers that sometimes use substances such as isinglass or gelatin during production, distilled liquors usually avoid these concerns. Consumers seeking certified vegan products should still check individual brands, but most standard distilled spirits naturally meet vegan dietary requirements.

Did you know?

Plain distilled spirits generally contain no animal-derived ingredients, but flavored products and certain production methods may introduce honey, dairy, or animal-based processing agents, so vegan suitability varies by brand.
