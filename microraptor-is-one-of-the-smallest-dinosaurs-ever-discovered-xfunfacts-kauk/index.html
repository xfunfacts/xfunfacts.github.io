Microraptor Is One of the Smallest Dinosaurs Ever Discovered xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Microraptor was one of the smallest known dinosaurs, with feathered wings that likely helped it glide between trees.

Microraptor is one of the smallest dinosaurs ever discovered, measuring only about 16 inches long from nose to tail. Fossils found in northeastern China revealed that this feathered dinosaur possessed wings on both its forelimbs and hind limbs, making it unlike any living animal today. Scientists believe Microraptor could glide between trees while hunting insects, small mammals, and other prey. Its exceptionally preserved fossils have provided valuable evidence supporting the evolutionary connection between dinosaurs and modern birds.

Did you know?

Microraptor lived approximately 120 million years ago and possessed long flight feathers on both its forelimbs and hindlimbs, suggesting a four-winged arrangement that helped it move through the air.
