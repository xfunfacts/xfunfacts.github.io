The Great Lakes Form the World's Largest Freshwater System xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Great Lakes form the world's largest freshwater system by surface area, supplying water to millions of people.

The Great Lakes form the world's largest freshwater system by surface area, consisting of Lakes Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, and Ontario. Together they hold roughly one-fifth of Earth's surface freshwater and provide drinking water for tens of millions of people. The lakes support shipping, fishing, tourism, recreation, and diverse ecosystems across both the United States and Canada. They also influence regional weather by moderating temperatures and creating lake-effect snow during winter. Protecting water quality remains essential because these lakes are among North America's most valuable natural resources.

Did you know?

The five Great Lakes contain approximately 21% of the world's unfrozen surface freshwater by volume, but Lake Michigan is the only one located entirely within the United States.
