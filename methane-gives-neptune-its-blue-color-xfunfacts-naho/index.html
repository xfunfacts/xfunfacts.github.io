Methane Gives Neptune Its Blue Color xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Methane absorbs red light in Neptune's atmosphere, creating the planet's striking deep blue appearance seen from space.

Methane is the key reason Neptune appears such a vivid blue. This gas absorbs much of the red light from the Sun while allowing blue wavelengths to be scattered back into space. However, scientists now believe methane alone cannot fully explain Neptune's unusually deep color. Additional atmospheric particles and cloud chemistry may also play important roles in creating the planet's rich blue appearance. Spacecraft observations and modern telescopes continue helping researchers better understand the complex processes taking place high above Neptune's cloud tops.

Did you know?

Neptune's methane absorbs red wavelengths, but additional atmospheric particles are probably needed to explain why Neptune looks bluer than Uranus.
