Tigers Mark Their Territory xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Tigers mark their territory with scent and claw marks, helping communicate boundaries to other tigers.

Tigers communicate with one another by marking the boundaries of their territories using scent, scratches on trees, and other visual signals. They spray urine, leave scent from specialized glands, and create claw marks that inform other tigers about their presence. These territorial markers help reduce unnecessary confrontations by allowing neighboring tigers to recognize occupied areas. Communication through scent is especially important because tigers rarely meet face to face. Territorial marking helps maintain order across large forest landscapes.

Did you know?

Tiger scent marks contain chemical information that can reveal identity, sex, and reproductive status to other tigers entering the same area.
