Female Octopuses Can Lay 100,000 Eggs xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Female octopuses can lay up to 100,000 eggs and devote themselves entirely to protecting them until they hatch.

Female octopuses can lay up to 100,000 eggs, depending on the species, and devote themselves entirely to protecting them until they hatch. During this period, mothers carefully clean, guard, and oxygenate the eggs while often refusing to eat. After the young emerge, the exhausted female usually dies, having invested all of her remaining energy into reproduction. This extraordinary level of parental care is rare among marine invertebrates. It greatly increases the survival chances of the developing offspring.

Did you know?

Octopus egg numbers vary enormously by species, from dozens in some large-egged species to tens or even hundreds of thousands in species producing tiny eggs.
