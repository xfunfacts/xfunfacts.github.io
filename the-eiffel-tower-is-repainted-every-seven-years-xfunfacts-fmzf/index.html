The Eiffel Tower Is Repainted Every Seven Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Eiffel Tower is repainted about every seven years to protect its iron structure from corrosion and preserve its iconic appearance.

The Eiffel Tower is typically repainted about every seven years to protect its iron framework from corrosion and preserve one of the world's most recognizable landmarks. Each repainting requires dozens of skilled workers who carefully remove old paint, inspect the structure, and apply multiple protective coatings by hand. The process uses many tons of paint and can take well over a year to complete while the monument remains open to visitors. Built for the 1889 World's Fair in Paris, the tower was originally intended to stand only temporarily but soon became a symbol of French engineering and culture. Regular maintenance ensures that this iconic structure remains safe and visually striking for millions of visitors every year.

Did you know?

Gustave Eiffel recommended regular repainting to prevent corrosion, and the tower has historically received new protective coatings approximately every seven years; its color has changed repeatedly since its completion in 1889.
