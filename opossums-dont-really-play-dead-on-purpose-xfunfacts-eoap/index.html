Opossums Don't Really Play Dead on Purpose xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Opossums do not consciously play dead but enter an involuntary defensive state when terrified by predators.

When an opossum becomes extremely frightened, it does not consciously pretend to be dead. Instead, it enters an involuntary state called tonic immobility, during which it becomes limp, unresponsive, and may even release a foul-smelling fluid that resembles the scent of decay. This automatic response can discourage predators that prefer live prey. The episode may last from several minutes to several hours before the animal naturally recovers and resumes normal activity.

Did you know?

When an opossum enters its defensive immobile state, it may also release a foul-smelling fluid that makes it less appealing to predators.
