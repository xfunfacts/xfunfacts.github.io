The Most Expensive Bottle of Wine Sold for $558,000 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A 1945 bottle of Domaine de la Romanée-Conti sold for $558,000, making it one of history's costliest wines.

The 1945 Domaine de la Romanée-Conti is considered one of the rarest and most desirable wines ever produced. In 2018, a single bottle sold at auction for $558,000, setting a record for one of the highest prices ever paid for a bottle of wine. Only around 600 bottles were produced from that exceptional vintage before the vineyard's vines were replaced. Collectors value rare wines not only for their taste but also for their history, scarcity, and investment potential.

Did you know?

A bottle of 1945 Romanée-Conti sold at Sotheby's in New York for $558,000 in 2018; only about 600 bottles were produced that vintage, shortly before the vineyard's old vines were replaced.
