MasterCard Was Originally Called Master Charge xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Before becoming MasterCard, the global payment company was known as Master Charge during its early years.

Before becoming MasterCard, the international payment company was known as Master Charge. The name was introduced during the 1960s as banks collaborated to create a nationwide credit card network capable of competing with other major payment systems. In 1979, the company adopted the simpler name MasterCard to strengthen its global brand identity. Since then, it has grown into one of the world's largest payment networks, processing billions of electronic transactions each year across more than 200 countries and territories.

Did you know?

Master Charge emerged in 1966 through a cooperative network of banks, later adopting the Mastercard name in 1979 as the payment system expanded internationally beyond its original American banking network.
