Sunlight Takes About 8 Minutes to Reach Earth xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Every ray of sunlight you see left the Sun more than eight minutes ago. That travel time reveals the enormous distance between Earth and its star.

When you look at the Sun, you're actually seeing it as it appeared about 8 minutes and 20 seconds ago. That's because sunlight travels approximately 150 million kilometers, or 93 million miles, before reaching Earth. Since light moves at nearly 300,000 kilometers per second, even this incredible speed requires several minutes to cross the distance between our planet and the Sun. This delay also means that if the Sun suddenly disappeared, Earth would continue receiving its light and gravitational influence for about eight minutes before any changes became noticeable.

Did you know?

Sunlight takes only about eight minutes to reach Earth, but the energy may spend thousands of years moving outward through the Sun before escaping its surface.
