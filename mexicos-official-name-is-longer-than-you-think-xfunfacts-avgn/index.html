Mexico's Official Name Is Longer Than You Think xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Mexico's official name is the United Mexican States, reflecting its federal system of government and 31 states.

Mexico's official name is the United Mexican States, reflecting its federal system of government made up of 31 states and Mexico City as a federal entity. Although the shorter name "Mexico" is used in everyday conversation around the world, the full official name appears in legal and constitutional documents. The country's federal structure is similar in concept to that of the United States, with powers shared between national and state governments. Mexico's rich history, diverse cultures, and regional traditions have shaped one of the most influential nations in the Americas. Its official name highlights both its political organization and national identity.

Did you know?

Mexico's official Spanish name is Estados Unidos Mexicanos, meaning United Mexican States; its federation consists of 31 states and Mexico City, which has a special constitutional status.
