Strawberries Wear Their Seeds on the Outside xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Strawberries carry their seeds on the outside, making them one of the few fruits with this unusual structure.

Unlike most fruits, strawberries display their tiny seeds on the outside instead of enclosing them within the flesh. Those small specks are actually individual fruits called achenes, each containing a single seed. The juicy red portion that people eat is not a true berry but an enlarged flower receptacle. This unusual structure makes strawberries one of the most distinctive fruits in the plant kingdom.

Did you know?

The tiny structures on a strawberry's surface are technically achenes, each containing a seed, while the familiar red flesh develops from the flower's enlarged receptacle rather than its ovary.
