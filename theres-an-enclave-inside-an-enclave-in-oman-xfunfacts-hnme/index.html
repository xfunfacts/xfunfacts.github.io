There's an Enclave Inside an Enclave in Oman xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The border between the UAE and Oman hides one of the world's rarest geographic oddities: an enclave inside another enclave.

The border between the United Arab Emirates and Oman contains one of the world's rarest geographical arrangements: an enclave located inside another enclave. The Omani territory of Madha is completely surrounded by land belonging to the United Arab Emirates. Even more remarkably, within Madha lies the village of Nahwa, which belongs to the UAE, creating an enclave inside an enclave. This unusual boundary developed through historical agreements and local decisions made by tribal communities rather than through modern political planning. Residents of the region cross international borders during ordinary daily travel, and despite the complex geography, relations between the neighboring countries remain peaceful. Similar geographical arrangements are extremely rare worldwide, making Madha and Nahwa popular examples in geography textbooks. The area demonstrates how historical negotiations, local allegiances, and centuries of regional development can produce borders that appear surprisingly complex when viewed on a modern map.

Did you know?

Madha is an Omani enclave surrounded by the United Arab Emirates, while Nahwa is a UAE enclave inside Madha, creating a rare nested arrangement of international borders.
