Babe Ruth Began His Career as a Pitcher xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Babe Ruth began his legendary baseball career as a dominant left-handed pitcher before becoming a home run icon.

Before becoming baseball's greatest home run hitter, Babe Ruth began his professional career as an outstanding left-handed pitcher. Early in his career with the Boston Red Sox, he established himself as one of the league's best pitchers before his incredible batting ability transformed the sport. After joining the New York Yankees, Ruth revolutionized baseball by making the home run a central part of the game. His success on both the mound and at the plate remains unmatched by most players in history.

Did you know?

Before becoming baseball's greatest power hitter, Babe Ruth won more than 20 games as a pitcher in two separate seasons for the Boston Red Sox.
