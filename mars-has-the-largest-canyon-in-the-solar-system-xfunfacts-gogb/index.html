Mars Has the Largest Canyon in the Solar System xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Valles Marineris on Mars is the largest canyon in the solar system, stretching thousands of kilometers.

Mars is home to Valles Marineris, the largest canyon system in the solar system. Stretching for about 2,500 miles, or 4,000 kilometers, and reaching depths of more than 4 miles, it dwarfs Earth's Grand Canyon in both length and depth. Scientists believe the canyon formed as Mars' crust stretched and cracked during the planet's early geological history, possibly aided by volcanic activity and erosion. Its steep cliffs expose layers of ancient rock that preserve valuable information about Mars' past. Valles Marineris remains one of the most spectacular geological features ever discovered on another planet.

Did you know?

Valles Marineris stretches for about 4,000 kilometers, making it long enough to span nearly the entire width of the continental United States.
