The Oort Cloud May Contain Trillions of Comets xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The distant Oort Cloud may contain trillions of icy comets surrounding the outer reaches of the solar system.

Far beyond Pluto, astronomers believe the Oort Cloud surrounds the solar system as a vast spherical shell containing trillions of icy objects and dormant comets. Although no spacecraft has reached this distant region, its existence is supported by observations of long-period comets entering the inner solar system. The Oort Cloud may extend nearly a quarter of the distance to the nearest star, making it one of the largest known structures associated with the Sun. Scientists think these icy bodies are remnants from the formation of the solar system. Studying them helps reveal how planetary systems develop over billions of years.

Did you know?

The Oort Cloud has never been directly observed; astronomers infer its existence from the long-period comets that enter the inner Solar System.
