The Quietest Room on Earth Lets You Hear Your Body xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The world's quietest room is so silent that visitors begin hearing their own heartbeat, breathing, and even subtle sounds inside their bodies.

The quietest room ever built pushes human hearing to its absolute limits. Located at Orfield Laboratories in Minnesota, the chamber measures below zero decibels, making it quieter than the threshold of normal human hearing. In such an environment, the absence of outside sound becomes almost overwhelming. Visitors often report hearing their own heartbeat, breathing, stomach movements, and even subtle sounds produced by muscles, joints, and blood circulating through the body. Without familiar background noise, the brain becomes intensely aware of internal bodily functions that usually go unnoticed. Many people also experience difficulty maintaining balance because humans partially rely on ambient sound to orient themselves in space. Few individuals can remain inside the chamber for extended periods, with some finding the silence psychologically uncomfortable after only a short time. Scientists use rooms like this to test sensitive audio equipment and study human hearing under controlled conditions. The chamber demonstrates that complete silence can be far stranger and more unsettling than most people imagine.

Did you know?

Anechoic chambers use deeply absorbing walls to suppress reflected sound, allowing measurements far quieter than those possible in ordinary rooms
