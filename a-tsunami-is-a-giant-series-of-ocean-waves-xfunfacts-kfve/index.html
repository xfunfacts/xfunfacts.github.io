A Tsunami Is a Giant Series of Ocean Waves xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A tsunami is a series of powerful ocean waves caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, landslides, or other major disturbances.

A tsunami is not a single giant wave but a series of powerful ocean waves generated by sudden disturbances beneath the sea. Underwater earthquakes are the most common cause, although volcanic eruptions, landslides, and meteor impacts can also trigger them. In the open ocean, tsunami waves may pass almost unnoticed because they are very long and relatively low. As they approach shallow coastal waters, however, they slow down while growing dramatically taller. This concentration of energy can produce destructive flooding that reaches far inland.

Did you know?

Tsunami waves can cross entire ocean basins at jet-aircraft-like speeds in deep water, yet become much taller and more destructive as they enter shallow coastal water.
