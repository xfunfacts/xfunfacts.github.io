Contact Us

Have a question, spotted a mistake, or thought of a fact worth sharing? We welcome your feedback and ideas.

Email us at [email protected].

What You Can Contact Us About

  • A fact that seems incorrect, unclear, or out of date.
  • An idea for a new fun fact or topic.
  • A problem with the website or one of its features.
  • Feedback about the reading experience, navigation, or illustrations.

Help Us Understand Your Message

If you are reporting a fact, please include a link to the page and explain what you think needs correcting. A reliable source, if you have one, can help us review the information.

If something on the website is not working, tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. Including your device type and browser can help us understand the problem.

Our Replies

We read every message, though we may not be able to reply to all of them.

A Note for Younger Readers

Please ask a parent, guardian, or teacher before emailing us. Do not include personal details such as your full name, home address, school, or phone number.

A parent or guardian can also contact us on your behalf.
