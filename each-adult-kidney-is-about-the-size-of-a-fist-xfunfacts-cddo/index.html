Each Adult Kidney Is About the Size of a Fist xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Your kidneys may be small enough to fit in your fist, but they quietly perform some of the body's most important jobs every day.

Although kidneys perform countless vital functions every day, many people are surprised by how compact these organs actually are. Each healthy adult kidney weighs approximately five ounces, or about 142 grams, and is roughly the size of a clenched fist. Despite their relatively small size, the kidneys continuously filter blood, remove waste products, regulate fluid balance, control important minerals, and help maintain healthy blood pressure. Together, the two kidneys process enormous amounts of blood every day while producing urine to eliminate excess water and waste. Their remarkable efficiency demonstrates how highly specialized organs can perform complex biological tasks within a limited space. Understanding their size also helps explain why kidney disease may progress unnoticed, since these organs possess significant reserve capacity before symptoms become obvious.

Did you know?

An adult kidney is roughly fist-sized, yet each contains around a million filtering units called nephrons that regulate fluid, electrolytes, waste removal, and blood pressure.
