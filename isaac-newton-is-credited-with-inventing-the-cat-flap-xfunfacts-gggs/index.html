Isaac Newton Is Credited with Inventing the Cat Flap xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A popular legend credits Isaac Newton with inventing the cat flap, although historians have never confirmed the story.

Although Isaac Newton is often credited with inventing the cat flap, historians have found little reliable evidence to confirm the story. According to the popular tale, Newton cut a small opening in his laboratory door so his cat could enter and leave without interrupting his experiments, then supposedly added a smaller hole for kittens. While the story has become one of the most famous anecdotes about the scientist, no contemporary records verify that it actually happened. Regardless of its accuracy, the legend reflects Newton's reputation for deep concentration and has become a charming part of scientific folklore that continues to be widely shared today.

Did you know?

The story that Isaac Newton invented a cat flap, sometimes including separate holes for a cat and its kittens, is a popular anecdote without reliable evidence in contemporary accounts of his life.
