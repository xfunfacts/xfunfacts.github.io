The Internet Began as ARPANET xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] ARPANET laid the technological foundation for today's Internet through early computer networking research.

The Internet traces its origins to ARPANET, an experimental computer network funded by the United States Department of Defense in the late 1960s. Researchers created ARPANET to allow computers at different locations to communicate efficiently and reliably. Many technologies first developed for ARPANET, including packet switching and network protocols, later became essential foundations of the modern Internet. Today's global digital network connects billions of devices, but its roots began with a much smaller research project.

Did you know?

ARPANET introduced packet-switching networking and later adopted TCP/IP in 1983, a major step toward the architecture of the modern Internet.
