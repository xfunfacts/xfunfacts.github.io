You Can Smell a Skunk from 1.6 Kilometers Away xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A skunk's defensive spray can sometimes be smelled from as far as 1.6 kilometers under favorable conditions.

A skunk's defensive spray can sometimes be detected from as far as 1.6 kilometers away under favorable weather conditions. The powerful odor comes from sulfur-containing chemicals called thiols, which are among the strongest naturally occurring smells recognized by humans. When threatened, a skunk first warns predators by stamping its feet, raising its tail, or turning its back before releasing its spray with surprising accuracy. The oily liquid can temporarily irritate the eyes and produce an intense odor that may linger on fur, clothing, or other surfaces for days. Because producing the spray requires energy and time to replenish, skunks generally avoid using it unless absolutely necessary. Their bold black-and-white coloration serves as a warning signal, teaching many predators to avoid them before an encounter ever occurs.

Did you know?

Skunk spray contains sulfur-rich compounds called thiols, which are so potent that humans can detect them at extremely low concentrations.
