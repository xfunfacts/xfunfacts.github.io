Ryan Gosling Nearly Joined the Backstreet Boys xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Ryan Gosling was once offered the chance to join the Backstreet Boys before choosing an acting career instead.

Before achieving international fame as an actor, Ryan Gosling was reportedly offered an opportunity to join the Backstreet Boys because he knew several future members through his time on The Mickey Mouse Club. He ultimately chose a different career path, focusing on acting instead of joining the boy band. The decision changed the course of his life, leading to acclaimed performances in films such as The Notebook, La La Land, and Barbie. The story is a reminder that major career opportunities often depend on the choices people make at pivotal moments.

Did you know?

Ryan Gosling knew future Backstreet Boys member A.J. McLean as a teenager, but the popular claim that Gosling received and rejected a formal offer to join the group is not well documented.
