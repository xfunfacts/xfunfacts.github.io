20,000 Bees Followed a Car for Two Days xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Around 20,000 bees followed a car for two days after their queen became trapped inside. The remarkable swarm stayed together until rescuers safely reunited the colony.

A remarkable swarm of about 20,000 bees once followed a car for two days after their queen became trapped inside. Worker bees rely heavily on pheromones released by the queen, allowing them to locate and protect her wherever she goes. As long as she remained inside the vehicle, the swarm continued following despite the unusual journey. Eventually, beekeepers safely removed the queen and relocated the entire colony. The story demonstrates the extraordinary organization of honeybee societies and the powerful biological bond that keeps a colony united around its queen.

Did you know?

Worker honeybees can communicate the location of food through a waggle dance, using movement and vibration to indicate both direction and distance.
