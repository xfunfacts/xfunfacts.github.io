Why Many Los Angeles Apartments Have No Refrigerator xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Renting an apartment in Los Angeles doesn't always mean getting a refrigerator. In many homes, tenants must bring their own.

Many people assume a refrigerator is a standard feature of every rental apartment, but that is not always true in Los Angeles. In fact, roughly half of rental apartments in the city do not include a refrigerator when tenants move in. California housing regulations generally require landlords to provide essentials such as heating, plumbing, electricity, and safe living conditions, but refrigerators are typically classified as optional amenities rather than legally required appliances. As a result, many renters purchase their own refrigerators or negotiate with landlords before signing a lease. The practice often surprises newcomers from other parts of the United States, where refrigerators are commonly included with rental housing. Some landlords prefer this arrangement because it reduces maintenance costs and allows tenants to choose appliances that suit their preferences. Although unusual by international standards, the absence of refrigerators has become a well-known feature of the Los Angeles rental market and an important detail for prospective tenants to consider before moving.

Did you know?

Los Angeles landlords historically were not always required to supply refrigerators, but California law now requires working refrigerators for new, amended, or renewed residential leases beginning January 1, 2026.
