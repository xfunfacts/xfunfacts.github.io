Alaska Is America's Largest State xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Alaska is America's largest state, known for its vast wilderness, towering mountains, glaciers, and abundant wildlife.

Alaska is the largest U.S. state by land area, covering more than twice the combined size of Texas and California. Purchased from Russia in 1867, it is famous for towering mountains, glaciers, vast forests, and abundant wildlife. Alaska contains more coastline than all other U.S. states combined and is home to Denali, North America's tallest mountain. Its remote wilderness supports bears, whales, moose, wolves, and countless bird species. Rich natural resources and spectacular landscapes make Alaska one of the world's most extraordinary regions for nature and outdoor adventure.

Did you know?

Alaska covers approximately 1.72 million square kilometers, making it more than twice the size of Texas and larger than the combined area of the next three largest U.S. states.
