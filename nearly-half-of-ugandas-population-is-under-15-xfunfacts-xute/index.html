Nearly Half of Uganda’s Population Is Under 15 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Nearly half of Uganda's population is younger than 15 years old. This youthful demographic shapes the country's future opportunities and challenges.

Uganda has one of the youngest populations in the world, with nearly half of its citizens under the age of 15. This youthful population creates both significant opportunities and major challenges for the country. A large number of young people can become a powerful workforce in the future if education, healthcare, and employment opportunities continue to improve. At the same time, rapid population growth places additional pressure on schools, infrastructure, and public services. Uganda's unique demographics make it an important example for researchers studying population growth and economic development across Africa.

Did you know?

Uganda's youthful population reflects decades of high fertility rates, creating growing demand for schools, jobs, housing, and healthcare as large generations of children enter adulthood.
