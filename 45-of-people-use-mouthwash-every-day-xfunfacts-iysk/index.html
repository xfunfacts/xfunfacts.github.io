45% of People Use Mouthwash Every Day xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] About 45% of people use mouthwash daily as part of an oral care routine alongside brushing and flossing.

Around 45 percent of people report using mouthwash as part of their daily oral hygiene routine. Mouthwash can help reduce bacteria, freshen breath, and reach areas that brushing alone may miss, but it is most effective when combined with regular brushing and flossing rather than used as a replacement. Different products are formulated for specific purposes, including cavity prevention, plaque reduction, or relief from dry mouth. Dentists generally recommend choosing a mouthwash that matches individual oral health needs and using it according to the manufacturer's directions.

Did you know?

Mouthwash can supplement brushing and interdental cleaning, but its benefits depend on the formulation; fluoride rinses help prevent cavities, while certain antiseptic rinses can reduce plaque and gingivitis.
