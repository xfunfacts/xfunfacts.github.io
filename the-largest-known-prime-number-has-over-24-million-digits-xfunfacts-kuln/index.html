The Largest Known Prime Number Has Over 24 Million Digits xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The largest known prime number is so enormous that writing it out would fill millions of digits before you reached the end.

The largest known prime number is so enormous that writing it out in full would require more than 24 million digits. Known as a Mersenne prime, it is calculated as 2 raised to the power of 82,589,933 minus 1. This extraordinary number was discovered through the Great Internet Mersenne Prime Search (GIMPS), a global volunteer computing project that allows thousands of computers to work together searching for new prime numbers. Prime numbers are integers divisible only by 1 and themselves, making them fundamental building blocks of mathematics. Although gigantic primes have little everyday use, they play an important role in number theory, computer science, and modern cryptography. Each new record-breaking prime pushes the limits of computing power while helping mathematicians better understand the fascinating patterns hidden within numbers. Discoveries like this show how collaboration between volunteers and technology continues expanding the boundaries of mathematical knowledge.

Did you know?

The current largest known prime contains more than 41 million digits, so the older 24-million-digit record is no longer up to date
