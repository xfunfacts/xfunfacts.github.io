Processed Cheese Was Invented in Switzerland xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Processed cheese was first invented in Switzerland in 1911, not America. The innovation helped extend cheese shelf life for export.

Although processed cheese is often associated with the United States, its origins actually trace back to Switzerland. In 1911, Walter Gerber and Fritz Stettler developed a method of heating cheese with emulsifying salts to create a product that remained stable for longer periods without separating. Their goal was to extend shelf life and make cheese easier to transport for export. The innovation later spread internationally, where manufacturers created many new varieties suited to different markets. Processed cheese became especially popular because it melts evenly, stores well, and offers consistent texture. Today it appears in sandwiches, burgers, sauces, and countless convenience foods around the world. Its history illustrates how practical food preservation techniques often lead to products that become everyday staples across many cultures.

Did you know?

Swiss inventors Walter Gerber and Fritz Stettler developed an early processed cheese in 1911 by heating cheese with emulsifying salts, helping create a smoother product with improved storage properties.
