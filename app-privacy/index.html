xFunFacts App Privacy Policy

xFunFacts is an educational reading app provided by xFacter.

Saved facts and reading history

Saved facts and your reading history are stored on your device. The app does not send these records to our servers. You can remove saved facts in the Saved tab. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings, Apps, xFunFacts, Storage, Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Automatic Android backup and device transfer of app data are disabled in this release.

Internet connections

The app requests the fact catalog and article pages from xfunfacts.github.io. Illustrations are loaded from funfacts-cdn.whitescreen.dev. These services receive the connection information required to deliver content, such as your IP address and the requested URL, and may keep operational and security logs under their own service settings and policies. The app does not attach your saved facts, reading history, advertising ID, or a custom user identifier to these requests.

Advertising and analytics

This version has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account registration, in-app purchases, or location permission. We do not sell app user data. Fonts and the app icon are included in the app.

Sharing

Share and Copy link run only when you choose them. Android lets you select the destination for a shared fact. The selected app or service handles the shared information under its own policies.

App updates

The app asks Google Play whether an update is available when you open or return to it. Google Play handles the update check, download and installation under its own terms and privacy practices. Saved facts and reading history are not included in the app's update requests. Your choice to postpone an update reminder is stored on your device. You choose whether to start an update and when to restart the app after it downloads.

Younger readers

The app includes a For Kids collection. No account or personal details are required to read facts. Younger readers should ask a parent or guardian before contacting support and should not include their address, school, or phone number in messages.

Support and policy updates

Contact xfunfacts9@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to support. We use those messages to respond to your request. Hosting providers control their own operational records. The public policy is available at https://xfunfacts.github.io/app-privacy/. If app data practices change, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated.
