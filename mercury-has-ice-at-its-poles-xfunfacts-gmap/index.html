Mercury Has Ice at Its Poles xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Water ice survives inside permanently shadowed craters near Mercury's poles despite the planet's intense heat.

Although Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun, scientists have confirmed that water ice exists inside permanently shadowed craters near its poles. These deep craters never receive direct sunlight because of Mercury's very small axial tilt, allowing temperatures to remain cold enough for ice to survive for billions of years. Radar observations from Earth first hinted at the presence of ice, and NASA's MESSENGER mission later confirmed the discovery. The finding demonstrated that even one of the hottest planets can preserve frozen water under the right conditions, changing scientists' understanding of where water can exist in the solar system.

Did you know?

Mercury's polar ice survives because some crater floors never receive direct sunlight, keeping them cold enough to preserve water for billions of years.
