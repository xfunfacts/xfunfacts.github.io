Indiana University's Main Library Slowly Sinks xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Indiana University's Main Library is said to sink because its books were too heavy, but this popular campus story is widely considered a myth.

A long-running campus legend claims that Indiana University's Main Library slowly sinks because engineers forgot to account for the weight of the books when designing the building. While the story is entertaining, it is not true. Engineers certainly considered the expected weight of books during construction, and there is no evidence that the library is gradually sinking because of its collection. The myth has survived for decades because it is memorable and often shared with new students. Like many university legends, it reflects the way humorous stories can become part of campus culture even when they have little basis in reality.

Did you know?

The Indiana University sinking-library story is widely treated as folklore, and engineers account for heavy book loads when designing major library buildings.
