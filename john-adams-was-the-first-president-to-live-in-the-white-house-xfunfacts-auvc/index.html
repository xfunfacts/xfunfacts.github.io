John Adams Was the First President to Live in the White House xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] John Adams became the first U.S. president to live in the White House after moving into the newly completed residence in 1800.

John Adams became the first U.S. president to live in the White House when he moved into the newly completed executive residence in 1800. Although the building was still unfinished, Adams and First Lady Abigail Adams began establishing traditions that continue today. Abigail famously used the large East Room to dry laundry because construction was incomplete. Over the following centuries, the White House expanded through renovations and additions, becoming one of the world's most recognizable political landmarks.

Did you know?

When John Adams moved into the unfinished White House in 1800, he famously wrote a prayer asking that only honest and wise men rule under its roof.
