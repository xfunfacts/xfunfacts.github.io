Brazil Borders Every South American Country Except Chile and Ecuador xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Brazil borders every South American country except Chile and Ecuador, sharing land boundaries with ten nations.

Brazil shares land borders with every South American country except Chile and Ecuador, giving it frontiers with ten neighboring nations. This extensive network of borders reflects Brazil's immense size and central location on the continent. The country's connections with so many neighbors have shaped centuries of trade, migration, cultural exchange, and regional cooperation. Combined with its long Atlantic coastline, Brazil's geography has played a major role in its economic development and its influence across South America.

Did you know?

Brazil shares land borders with nine independent South American countries and French Guiana, an overseas territory of France, leaving Chile and Ecuador as its only non-neighboring mainland nations.
