Submarines Are Traditionally Called Boats xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Naval tradition refers to submarines as boats rather than ships, regardless of their size or technology.

Although many people call them ships, submarines are traditionally referred to as "boats" in naval culture. The custom dates back to the earliest submarines, which were much smaller than surface warships and retained the historical designation even as they grew much larger. Sailors and naval officers continue using the term out of tradition and professional practice. It remains one of the many unique customs preserved within naval history.

Did you know?

The term "boat" for submarines comes from naval tradition and historical usage, even though modern submarines can be larger than many surface ships.
