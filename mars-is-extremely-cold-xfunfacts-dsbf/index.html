Mars Is Extremely Cold xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Mars has an average temperature near -80°F, making it one of the coldest planets in the solar system.

Mars is an extremely cold world with an average surface temperature of about -80°F, or -62°C, although temperatures vary widely depending on location and season. Near the equator during summer afternoons, temperatures can briefly rise above the freezing point of water, while polar winter nights can plunge below -195°F, or -125°C. The planet's thin atmosphere is unable to trap heat effectively, causing rapid cooling after sunset. Frost and frozen carbon dioxide regularly form across parts of the surface during winter. These harsh conditions present major challenges for future human explorers while helping scientists understand how planetary climates evolve.

Did you know?

Mars can still become warm enough near the equator for surface temperatures to briefly rise above freezing on sunny afternoons.
