England and Portugal Have the Oldest Alliance in History xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] England and Portugal have remained allies since 1386, making their partnership the longest continuously active alliance in recorded history.

The alliance between England and Portugal is widely recognized as the oldest continuously active diplomatic alliance in the world. Its foundations were established through the Treaty of Windsor in 1386, creating a formal partnership that has endured for more than six centuries. Throughout history, the alliance has influenced trade, military cooperation, and political relations during major European conflicts, including the Napoleonic Wars and both World Wars. The agreement strengthened commercial ties between the two nations while providing mutual support during periods of external threat. Although international politics has changed dramatically since the Middle Ages, neither country has formally ended the alliance, allowing it to remain in effect today. Historians frequently cite this remarkable continuity as evidence of one of the most stable long-term diplomatic relationships ever maintained between sovereign states. The enduring partnership continues to symbolize centuries of cooperation built on shared strategic interests and mutual trust.

Did you know?

The Anglo-Portuguese alliance was formalized by the Treaty of Windsor in 1386, building on earlier agreements and surviving major changes in both countries' governments.
