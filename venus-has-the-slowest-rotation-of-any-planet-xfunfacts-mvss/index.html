Venus Has the Slowest Rotation of Any Planet xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Venus rotates more slowly than any other planet, taking 243 Earth days to complete one rotation.

Venus has the slowest rotation of any planet in the solar system, requiring about 243 Earth days to complete a single spin on its axis. Even more unusual, Venus rotates backward compared with most other planets, causing the Sun to appear to rise in the west and set in the east. Scientists are still investigating why the planet spins so slowly, with possible explanations including ancient giant impacts or long-term interactions between its atmosphere and the Sun. Because of this extremely slow rotation, a single day on Venus lasts longer than its entire year. The planet's unusual motion remains one of the greatest puzzles in planetary science.

Did you know?

Venus rotates once every about 243 Earth days, but because it spins backward while orbiting the Sun, its solar day lasts about 117 Earth days.
