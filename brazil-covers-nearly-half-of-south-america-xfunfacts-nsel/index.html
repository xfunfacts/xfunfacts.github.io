Brazil Covers Nearly Half of South America xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Brazil covers nearly half of South America's land area, making it the continent's largest country by size.

Brazil covers nearly half of South America's land area, occupying about 47 percent of the continent and making it the largest country in the region. Its vast territory includes the Amazon Rainforest, extensive wetlands, grasslands, mountains, and thousands of kilometers of Atlantic coastline. Because of its enormous size, Brazil contains an extraordinary diversity of ecosystems, wildlife, cultures, and climates. It also has the largest population and economy in South America, giving it an important role in regional and global affairs.

Did you know?

Brazil's Amazon Basin contains the world's largest tropical rainforest and an immense river network, making the country's territory crucial to biodiversity, freshwater circulation, and regional climate.
