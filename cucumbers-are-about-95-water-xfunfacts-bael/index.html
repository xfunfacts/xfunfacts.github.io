Cucumbers Are About 95% Water xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Cucumbers are about 95% water, giving them their refreshing taste and making them a naturally hydrating snack.

Cucumbers consist of about 95% water, making them one of the most water-rich vegetables commonly eaten. Their crisp texture and refreshing taste come largely from this high water content, which also makes them a popular choice during warm weather. In addition to helping with hydration, cucumbers provide fiber, vitamin K, and small amounts of other nutrients. They are enjoyed raw, pickled, or added to salads in cuisines around the world.

Did you know?

Cucumber flesh contains very little energy because of its high water content, while much of its dietary fiber and several beneficial plant compounds are concentrated in or near the peel.
