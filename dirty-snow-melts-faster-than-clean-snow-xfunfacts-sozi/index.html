Dirty Snow Melts Faster Than Clean Snow xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dirty snow melts faster than clean snow because darker particles absorb more sunlight and generate additional heat.

Dirty snow often melts faster than clean snow because darker particles such as dust, soot, or soil absorb more sunlight instead of reflecting it. Clean, white snow reflects most incoming sunlight due to its high albedo, helping it stay frozen longer. When snow becomes darker, it converts more solar energy into heat, accelerating the melting process. Scientists study this effect because pollution and wildfire ash can influence snowpack in mountain regions, affecting freshwater supplies and the timing of seasonal runoff that many communities depend on.

Did you know?

Dark dust, soot, and other impurities lower snow's reflectivity, causing it to absorb more solar energy and melt faster than clean bright snow.
