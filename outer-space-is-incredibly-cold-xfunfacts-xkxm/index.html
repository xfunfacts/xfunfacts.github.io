Outer Space Is Incredibly Cold xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Outer space averages about -455°F, placing it only a few degrees above absolute zero.

Outer space is incredibly cold, with an average temperature of about -455°F, or approximately -270°C, only a few degrees above absolute zero. This temperature comes from the faint glow of the Big Bang known as the cosmic microwave background radiation. However, objects exposed directly to sunlight in space can become extremely hot because there is no atmosphere to distribute heat. As a result, spacecraft require advanced thermal control systems to protect equipment and astronauts from both extreme cold and intense solar heating.

Did you know?

The cosmic microwave background has a temperature of about 2.7 kelvin, but an object's temperature in space depends greatly on sunlight and its own heat radiation.
