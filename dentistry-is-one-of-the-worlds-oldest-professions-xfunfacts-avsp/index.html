Dentistry Is One of the World's Oldest Professions xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] People have been treating tooth problems for thousands of years, making dentistry one of the oldest professions in recorded history.

Dentistry ranks among humanity's oldest known professions, with evidence of dental treatment dating back approximately 9,000 years. Archaeologists have discovered human teeth from around 7000 BC showing signs of careful drilling, suggesting that prehistoric people attempted to treat painful dental problems using surprisingly advanced techniques for their time. These early procedures were likely performed with simple stone tools long before the invention of metal instruments or modern medicine. Ancient civilizations including the Egyptians, Greeks, Romans, and Chinese later developed increasingly sophisticated methods for cleaning teeth, relieving pain, replacing missing teeth, and treating oral disease. Although early dental care was often painful due to the absence of anesthesia, it represented an important step toward modern healthcare. Today's highly advanced dentistry has evolved from thousands of years of continuous innovation, making oral medicine one of the oldest and most enduring medical professions in human history.

Did you know?

Evidence of dental treatment dates back thousands of years, including drilled teeth and early prosthetic work, making dentistry one of humanity’s oldest specialized healing practices.
