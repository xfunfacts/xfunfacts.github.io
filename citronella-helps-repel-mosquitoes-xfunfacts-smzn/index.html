Citronella Helps Repel Mosquitoes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Citronella helps repel mosquitoes by masking human scents, making it a common ingredient in outdoor candles and sprays.

Citronella helps repel mosquitoes by interfering with the insects' ability to detect the scents and carbon dioxide that guide them toward people. Extracted mainly from certain tropical grasses, citronella oil is commonly used in candles, sprays, lotions, and outdoor products designed to reduce mosquito activity. While citronella can help discourage mosquitoes for short periods, its effectiveness depends on factors such as wind, concentration, and application method, so it may need to be reapplied regularly. Combining citronella with protective clothing, window screens, and eliminating standing water provides more reliable protection against mosquito bites. Researchers continue developing improved insect repellents by studying how mosquitoes locate their hosts.

Did you know?

Citronella can reduce mosquito attraction at close range, but its protection is limited and depends strongly on formulation, concentration, airflow, and how the product is used.
