Guinness World Records Began with Guinness Beer xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Guinness World Records began as an idea from Guinness Brewery and grew into the world's best-known collection of record-breaking achievements.

Guinness World Records began after Sir Hugh Beaver, managing director of Guinness Brewery, discussed a debate about Europe's fastest game bird during a hunting trip. Unable to find a reliable reference book, he supported the creation of a publication containing verified world records. First released in 1955, the book quickly became an international bestseller. It now documents thousands of remarkable achievements from around the globe.

Did you know?

The Guinness record book originated from a question about the fastest game bird in Europe, inspiring Guinness managing director Hugh Beaver to commission a reference book that could settle arguments in pubs.
