Warm Ocean Water Makes Hurricanes Stronger xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Warm ocean water fuels hurricanes by supplying the heat and moisture needed for storms to strengthen and intensify.

Warm ocean water is the primary energy source that allows hurricanes to form and become stronger. As seawater evaporates, it transfers heat and moisture into the atmosphere, fueling the storm's powerful thunderstorms. Ocean temperatures above about 80°F are generally needed for hurricane development. Warm currents such as the Gulf Stream can provide additional energy that rapidly intensifies storms under favorable conditions. Scientists closely monitor ocean temperatures because they strongly influence hurricane behavior.

Did you know?

Tropical cyclones usually require sea-surface temperatures around 26.5°C or warmer over sufficient depth, although warm water alone cannot create a hurricane.
