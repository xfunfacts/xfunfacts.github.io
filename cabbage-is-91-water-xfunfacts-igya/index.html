Cabbage Is 91% Water xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Cabbage is about 91% water while still providing fiber, vitamins, and nutrients that support a balanced diet.

Cabbage is made up of about 91 percent water, making it a refreshing vegetable that contributes to daily hydration while remaining naturally low in calories. Despite its high water content, cabbage is also rich in vitamin C, vitamin K, fiber, and beneficial plant compounds that have been studied for their antioxidant properties. It belongs to the cruciferous vegetable family alongside broccoli, cauliflower, kale, and Brussels sprouts, all of which are valued for their nutritional benefits. Cabbage can be enjoyed raw in salads and coleslaw, fermented into foods such as sauerkraut and kimchi, or cooked in soups, stir-fries, and stews. Its versatility, affordability, and long storage life have made cabbage an important food crop in cultures around the world for centuries.

Did you know?

Cabbage belongs to the same species as broccoli, cauliflower, and kale, Brassica oleracea, and supplies vitamin C, vitamin K, and fiber despite its high water content.
