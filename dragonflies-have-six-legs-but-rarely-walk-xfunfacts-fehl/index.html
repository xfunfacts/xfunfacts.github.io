Dragonflies Have Six Legs but Rarely Walk xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dragonflies have six legs like all insects but mainly use them to catch prey instead of walking.

Dragonflies have six legs like all insects, but they rarely use them for walking. Instead, their legs are positioned to form a basket-like structure that captures flying insects during flight. Dragonflies spend most of their lives either flying or resting on vegetation, making walking unnecessary for everyday survival. Their exceptional vision, speed, and aerial agility make them among the most effective insect predators in the natural world.

Did you know?

Dragonflies can catch prey in midair with impressive accuracy, forming a basket with their legs before bringing captured insects toward the mouth.
