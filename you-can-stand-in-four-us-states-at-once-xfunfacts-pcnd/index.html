You Can Stand in Four U.S. States at Once xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Four U.S. states meet at a single point at the Four Corners Monument, where Arizona, Utah, Colorado, and New Mexico share one border.

The Four Corners Monument is the only place in the United States where four states meet at a single point. Visitors can stand with one hand and one foot in Arizona, Utah, Colorado, and New Mexico at the same time. Located within the Navajo Nation and the Ute Mountain Ute Tribal Nation, the monument is both a popular tourist attraction and an important cultural site. Although modern surveying techniques have refined geographic measurements, the monument remains an iconic symbol of American geography.

Did you know?

The Four Corners Monument marks the only point in the United States where four states meet, and the Navajo Nation manages the site, which also lies near Ute Mountain Ute tribal lands.
