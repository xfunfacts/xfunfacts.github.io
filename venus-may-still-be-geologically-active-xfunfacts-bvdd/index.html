Venus May Still Be Geologically Active xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Evidence suggests Venus may still be geologically active, with possible signs of relatively recent volcanic eruptions.

Although Venus was once thought to be geologically inactive, growing evidence suggests the planet may still experience volcanic activity today. Radar images from orbit have revealed surface features that appear relatively young, while recent observations have detected changes that may indicate fresh lava flows or volcanic eruptions. Scientists also measure gases in the atmosphere that could be released by active volcanoes beneath the thick cloud cover. Confirming ongoing volcanism would help explain how Venus continues to reshape its surface and atmosphere. Future spacecraft are expected to investigate this possibility in much greater detail, offering new insight into the evolution of rocky planets.

Did you know?

Changes detected at volcanic features such as Maat Mons provide some of the strongest evidence that Venus may still experience active eruptions.
