A Starfish's Body Is Essentially Its Head xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Starfish have no separate head, with their body and arms sharing sensory and feeding functions throughout the animal.

Unlike most animals, starfish do not have a separate head or centralized brain. Their mouth, sensory organs, and nervous system are distributed throughout the body and arms, leading scientists to describe the entire body as functioning somewhat like a head. Each arm can detect light, chemicals, and touch while coordinating movement with the rest of the animal. This decentralized body plan also allows many starfish to regenerate lost arms, and in some species, even regrow an entire body from a single surviving arm.

Did you know?

Sea stars lack a centralized head and brain; instead, a nerve ring and radial nerves coordinate their five or more arms and distributed sensory structures.
