Blueberries Are Rich in Antioxidants xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Blueberries are packed with antioxidants, vitamins, and plant compounds that support overall health as part of a balanced diet.

Blueberries are widely recognized for their high antioxidant content, particularly compounds called anthocyanins that give the berries their deep blue color. Antioxidants help protect cells from damage caused by unstable molecules known as free radicals. In addition to antioxidants, blueberries provide fiber, vitamin C, vitamin K, and manganese. Regularly including berries in a balanced diet has been associated with numerous health benefits, making blueberries one of the most nutrient-dense fruits available.

Did you know?

Blueberries contain anthocyanins, the pigments responsible for their blue-purple color, but laboratory antioxidant measurements do not establish that eating them directly prevents particular diseases.
