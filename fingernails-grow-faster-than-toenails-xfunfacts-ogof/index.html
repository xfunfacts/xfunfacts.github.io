Fingernails Grow Faster Than Toenails xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Fingernails grow much faster than toenails thanks to better circulation and frequent use throughout daily activities.

Fingernails typically grow about four times faster than toenails because fingers receive better blood circulation and are used more frequently during daily activities. Nail growth can also vary with age, nutrition, health, and even the seasons, often increasing slightly during warmer months. Healthy nails provide protection for sensitive fingertips while improving grip and precision. Their growth rate offers useful clues about overall health and metabolism.

Did you know?

Fingernails usually grow several times faster than toenails, with average growth often around 3 millimeters per month for fingernails and roughly 1 millimeter for toenails.
