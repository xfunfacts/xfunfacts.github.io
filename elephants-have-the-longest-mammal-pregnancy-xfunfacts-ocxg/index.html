Elephants Have the Longest Mammal Pregnancy xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Elephants have the longest pregnancy of any living mammal, carrying their calves for about 22 months before birth.

Elephants have the longest pregnancy of any living mammal, carrying their calves for approximately 22 months before giving birth. This exceptionally long gestation allows the developing calf's brain, nervous system, and body to mature more fully before birth, increasing its chances of survival. Newborn elephants can stand and begin walking within hours, an essential ability for keeping up with the herd. Because female elephants usually give birth to only one calf every several years, each offspring represents a major investment of time and energy, making successful survival especially important for the species.

Did you know?

Elephant calves are born with highly developed brains compared with many mammals, which helps them quickly learn complex social behavior from the herd.
