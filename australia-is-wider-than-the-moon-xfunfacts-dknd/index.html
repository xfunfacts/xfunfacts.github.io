Australia Is Wider Than the Moon xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Australia stretches farther east to west than the Moon's diameter, making for a surprising size comparison.

Many people are surprised to learn that Australia is actually wider than the Moon. Australia's east-to-west distance measures nearly 4,000 kilometers, while the Moon's diameter is about 3,474 kilometers. The comparison sounds unbelievable because the Moon appears enormous in the night sky, but its apparent size is only an optical effect caused by its closeness to Earth. This fact highlights how difficult it can be for our brains to judge the true sizes of distant objects and demonstrates why scale is one of the most fascinating concepts in astronomy.

Did you know?

Australia spans roughly 4,000 kilometers from east to west, exceeding the Moon's mean diameter of approximately 3,475 kilometers by more than 500 kilometers.
