Kotex Began with Material Used for WWI Bandages xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Kotex originated from highly absorbent material first developed for World War I medical bandages.

The absorbent material that eventually became Kotex sanitary products was originally developed during World War I for surgical bandages. Nurses noticed that the highly absorbent cellulose material worked well for menstrual hygiene, inspiring a completely new commercial product after the war. Introduced in the early 1920s, Kotex helped transform menstrual care by providing a disposable alternative to reusable cloth products. The invention is an interesting example of wartime innovation finding unexpected civilian applications.

Did you know?

Kotex emerged from Kimberly-Clark's Cellucotton, an absorbent material used in World War I surgical dressings; the company adapted the material for disposable sanitary pads after the war.
