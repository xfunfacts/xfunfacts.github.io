Coffee Provides Most of America's Caffeine xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Coffee provides roughly 75% of the caffeine consumed in the United States, making it the leading dietary caffeine source.

Coffee accounts for roughly 75 percent of the caffeine consumed in the United States, making it the primary source of this naturally occurring stimulant for most Americans. Caffeine works by blocking adenosine receptors in the brain, helping people feel more alert and less tired for several hours. Although many beverages contain caffeine, including tea, soft drinks, and energy drinks, coffee remains the dominant source because of its popularity and relatively high caffeine content per serving. Health experts generally recommend moderate caffeine intake for most healthy adults, while recognizing that sensitivity varies widely between individuals depending on genetics, age, and overall health.

Did you know?

Coffee is the leading caffeine source for many American adults, although its exact share of national intake varies by age and survey year, especially as energy drinks and other caffeinated products gain popularity.
