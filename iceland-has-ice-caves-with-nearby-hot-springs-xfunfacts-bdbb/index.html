Iceland Has Ice Caves with Nearby Hot Springs xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Iceland's volcanic landscape creates the rare combination of dazzling ice caves and naturally heated geothermal hot springs.

Iceland is one of the few places on Earth where visitors can find sparkling ice caves located near naturally heated geothermal hot springs. This remarkable contrast exists because the island sits on an active volcanic region beneath massive glaciers. Heat rising from underground creates hot springs while nearby glaciers continuously shape spectacular blue ice formations. The combination of fire and ice has made Iceland famous for its dramatic landscapes and unique geological activity.

Did you know?

Iceland's glaciers and geothermal systems coexist because the island sits above both a mantle hotspot and the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, creating intense volcanic activity beneath a cold climate.
