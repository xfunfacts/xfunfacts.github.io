35% of Personal Dating Ad Users Were Married xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Reports suggested that about 35% of people using personal dating ads were already married at the time.

Reports have suggested that around 35 percent of people who placed personal dating advertisements were already married. Before online dating became common, newspaper personal ads offered a popular way for people to seek relationships or companionship. Although the reported figure has been widely repeated, it likely varied depending on the publication and survey. The statistic highlights how human relationships have always been complex, even before the internet transformed dating.

Did you know?

The often-cited 35% figure comes from older reports about personal-ad users and should not be treated as a universal rate for modern online dating populations.
