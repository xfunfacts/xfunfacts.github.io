Coffee Could Be Grounds for Divorce xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Historical accounts suggest coffee was once important enough to influence divorce claims. The story reflects coffee's extraordinary role in Ottoman society.

Coffee became so deeply woven into Ottoman daily life that historical accounts claim a woman could seek a divorce if her husband failed to provide enough coffee. Historians continue to debate whether this was a formal legal right or simply a popular tradition, but the story illustrates how valuable coffee had become in Ottoman society. More than just a beverage, coffee represented hospitality, social status, and family life. Whether completely accurate or partly legendary, the tale reflects the extraordinary influence coffee had on culture, customs, and everyday relationships throughout the empire.

Did you know?

Ottoman coffeehouses became important social institutions during the sixteenth century, but the popular story that wives could automatically divorce husbands for failing to provide coffee lacks convincing historical evidence.
