Dogs and Cats Can Be Allergic to Humans xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dogs and cats can develop allergies to human skin flakes, proving that pets can sometimes be allergic to people too.

People are not the only ones who develop allergies. Dogs and cats can also become allergic to humans, particularly to tiny flakes of dead skin known as dander. When sensitive pets are exposed to these allergens, they may develop symptoms such as itchy skin, excessive scratching, ear infections, or watery eyes. Veterinarians diagnose these allergies in much the same way they diagnose other environmental allergies and often recommend treatments that reduce exposure or relieve symptoms. Although uncommon, human allergies in pets remind us that allergic reactions can affect many different animal species.

Did you know?

Pet allergies can also be triggered by foods, pollen, dust mites, fleas, and environmental substances, so identifying the exact cause may require testing.
