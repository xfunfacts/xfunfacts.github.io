Beer Was Once Considered a Soft Drink in Russia xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Until 2011, Russian law classified many beers as non-alcoholic beverages because of their relatively low alcohol content.

Until 2011, Russian law classified many beers with relatively low alcohol content as food products rather than alcoholic beverages. This legal definition affected advertising, sales, and public consumption for many years before new legislation officially recognized beer as alcohol. The change reflected growing concerns about excessive drinking and public health. It also marked an important shift in Russia's alcohol regulations and drinking culture.

Did you know?

Russia's 2011 alcohol legislation extended alcohol-market regulations to beer, with additional retail restrictions taking effect in 2013; beer was already legally recognized as alcoholic before the reform.
