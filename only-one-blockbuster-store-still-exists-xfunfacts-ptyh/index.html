Only One Blockbuster Store Still Exists xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Thousands of Blockbuster stores once covered the world. Today, just one remains open as a nostalgic piece of movie history.

Out of roughly 9,000 Blockbuster video rental stores operating during the company's peak, only one official location remains in business today. The final Blockbuster is located in Bend, Oregon, where it has become both a functioning rental store and a popular tourist attraction. As streaming services such as Netflix, Hulu, and Disney+ transformed home entertainment, thousands of Blockbuster locations gradually closed during the late 2000s and 2010s. Rather than disappearing entirely, the Bend store embraced its unique status by selling themed merchandise, hosting nostalgic events, and preserving the experience of browsing physical movie shelves. Visitors from around the world travel to the store as a reminder of an era when renting DVDs and VHS tapes was a weekend tradition for millions of families. The surviving location has become a symbol of changing technology, consumer habits, and the rapid evolution of the entertainment industry over the past few decades.

Did you know?

The Bend, Oregon Blockbuster became the world's last operating Blockbuster store after other franchise locations closed, turning it into both a business and a nostalgia destination.
