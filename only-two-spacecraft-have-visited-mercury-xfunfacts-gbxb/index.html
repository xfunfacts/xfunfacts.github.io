Only Two Spacecraft Have Visited Mercury xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Mariner 10 and MESSENGER are the only spacecraft to closely explore Mercury and study its surface.

Only two spacecraft have ever closely explored Mercury, making it one of the least visited planets in the solar system. NASA's Mariner 10 flew past Mercury three times during the 1970s and captured the first close-up images of its cratered surface. Decades later, the MESSENGER spacecraft became the first to orbit Mercury, spending more than four years mapping its geology, magnetic field, and chemical composition before ending its mission in 2015. Today, the joint European and Japanese BepiColombo mission is traveling toward Mercury to continue expanding our understanding of the smallest planet in the solar system.

Did you know?

BepiColombo has also flown past Mercury on its journey, although Mariner 10 and MESSENGER remain the missions that historically provided most close-up Mercury data.
