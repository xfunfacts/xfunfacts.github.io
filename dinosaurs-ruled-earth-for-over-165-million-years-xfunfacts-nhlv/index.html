Dinosaurs Ruled Earth for Over 165 Million Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dinosaurs dominated Earth's land ecosystems for more than 165 million years before their mass extinction.

Dinosaurs ruled Earth for more than 165 million years, making them one of the most successful groups of land animals in the planet's history. They first appeared during the Triassic Period and diversified into thousands of species that occupied nearly every terrestrial environment before most became extinct about 66 million years ago. Dinosaurs ranged from small feathered hunters to enormous long-necked herbivores weighing dozens of tons. Modern scientific evidence has shown that birds are the direct descendants of certain dinosaur groups, meaning dinosaurs are not completely extinct. Fossil discoveries continue to reveal new species and provide fresh insights into their behavior, growth, and evolution.

Did you know?

Non-avian dinosaurs appeared approximately 230 million years ago during the Triassic Period and disappeared around 66 million years ago, although their evolutionary descendants, modern birds, survive today.
