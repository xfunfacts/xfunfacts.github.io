You Could Reach Space in About One Hour by Car xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The edge of space lies about 100 kilometers above Earth, roughly an hour away by highway speed in a straight vertical journey.

The internationally recognized boundary of space lies about 100 kilometers above Earth's surface, a distance known as the Kármán line. If a car could somehow travel straight upward at typical highway speeds without obstacles or gravity-related limitations, it would reach this altitude in roughly one hour. Of course, real vehicles cannot operate this way because Earth's atmosphere becomes too thin for conventional engines long before reaching space. The comparison simply illustrates that space is surprisingly close in terms of distance, even though reaching it requires advanced rocket technology.

Did you know?

The 100-kilometer Kármán line is a convenient boundary rather than a physical wall; Earth's atmosphere gradually thins and continues far above that altitude.
