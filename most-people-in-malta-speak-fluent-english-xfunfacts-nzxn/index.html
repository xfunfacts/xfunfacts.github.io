Most People in Malta Speak Fluent English xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] English is widely spoken throughout Malta alongside the Maltese language. Most residents are fluent in both official languages.

Malta is one of the few countries where nearly everyone speaks English despite it not being the native language. While Maltese is the nation's official language, English also holds official status due to Malta's long history as part of the British Empire. English is widely used in schools, government, business, media, and tourism, making it easy for international visitors to communicate almost anywhere on the islands. This bilingual culture has also helped Malta become a popular destination for language schools and international companies. The country's ability to seamlessly switch between two languages makes it one of Europe's most accessible English-speaking travel destinations.

Did you know?

Malta's official languages are Maltese and English, with Maltese being a Semitic language written in the Latin alphabet and containing substantial vocabulary influenced by Italian and English.
