The Average American Diet Is 55% Junk Food xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Around 55% of the average American diet comes from ultra-processed foods, highlighting modern eating habits and nutrition trends.

About 55 percent of the average American diet is made up of ultra-processed or junk foods, according to several nutrition studies examining modern eating habits. These foods often contain high amounts of added sugar, salt, unhealthy fats, and refined carbohydrates while providing fewer vitamins, minerals, and fiber than minimally processed foods. Frequent consumption has been associated with increased risks of obesity, heart disease, and type 2 diabetes when combined with poor overall dietary patterns. Nutrition experts generally recommend emphasizing whole foods such as fruits, vegetables, legumes, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats while limiting highly processed products. Small, consistent improvements in food choices over time often have a greater impact on long-term health than drastic short-term diet changes.

Did you know?

Research using the NOVA food classification has found that ultra-processed foods provide more than half of American dietary calories, but the category includes diverse products and is not identical to “junk food.”
