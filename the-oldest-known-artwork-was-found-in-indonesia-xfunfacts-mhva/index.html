The Oldest Known Artwork Was Found in Indonesia xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A 44,000-year-old cave painting in Indonesia ranks among the oldest known examples of human artwork.

The oldest known figurative artwork was discovered in a cave on Indonesia's Sulawesi Island and is estimated to be around 44,000 years old. The painting depicts wild pigs and human-like hunters using simple pigments made from natural minerals. Its remarkable age shows that early humans were capable of symbolic thinking, storytelling, and artistic expression much earlier than once believed. Discoveries like this continue to reshape our understanding of prehistoric culture and suggest that sophisticated creativity developed independently in different parts of the world.

Did you know?

Indonesian cave art includes paintings tens of thousands of years old, with newer dating studies identifying works older than the famous 44,000-year-old hunting scene at Leang Bulu' Sipong.
