Coriander and Cilantro Come From the Same Plant xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Cilantro and coriander come from the same plant. The leaves are usually called cilantro, while the dried seeds are known as coriander.

Many people assume cilantro and coriander are two completely different plants, but they actually come from the same species, Coriandrum sativum. The difference lies mainly in which part of the plant is being discussed. In American English, the fresh leaves and stems are called cilantro, while the dried seeds are known as coriander. In many other English-speaking countries, however, the entire plant is simply called coriander regardless of which part is being used. The leaves have a bright, citrus-like flavor that some people strongly dislike because of genetic differences affecting taste perception. Meanwhile, the seeds offer a warm, slightly sweet flavor commonly used in spice blends and baked goods. This difference in terminology often causes confusion, especially when following international recipes from different countries.

Did you know?

Cilantro and coriander are both names for Coriandrum sativum, but usage differs by region: American English usually calls the leaves cilantro and the seeds coriander.
