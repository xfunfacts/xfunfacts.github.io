Some of the World's Oldest Parliaments Were Founded Over 1,000 Years Ago xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Several of the world's oldest parliaments have served their communities for more than a thousand years.

Several of the world's oldest parliamentary traditions date back more than a thousand years. Iceland's Alþingi, established in 930 AD, is widely recognized as the oldest continuously operating parliament, while the Faroe Islands and the Isle of Man also maintain ancient legislative assemblies with roots in the Viking Age. These early gatherings originally served as places where community leaders created laws, settled disputes, and made important decisions. Their survival over many centuries reflects the enduring importance of representative government and public debate in shaping democratic institutions.

Did you know?

Iceland's Alþingi dates to around AD 930, while the Tynwald on the Isle of Man also claims a millennium-long parliamentary tradition; continuity and institutional definitions affect such records.
