Octopuses Have Arms, Not Tentacles xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Octopuses have eight arms rather than tentacles, with suckers running along nearly the entire length of each limb.

Despite the common misconception, an octopus has eight arms rather than tentacles. In biology, tentacles are limbs with suckers concentrated mainly near the tips, while octopus limbs have suckers running along nearly their entire length. Squid and cuttlefish possess both arms and tentacles, but octopuses rely exclusively on arms for movement, exploration, hunting, and manipulating objects. Their remarkable dexterity is one reason octopuses are considered among the most intelligent invertebrates.

Did you know?

Biologists call octopus limbs arms because suckers extend along most of their length, whereas true cephalopod tentacles usually have suckers concentrated near their tips.
