Canada Has the Most Doughnut Shops Per Person xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Canada has more doughnut shops per person than any other country, reflecting its long-standing love of coffee and pastries.

Canada has more doughnut shops per person than any other country, reflecting the nation's long-standing enthusiasm for this popular baked treat. Doughnuts are commonly enjoyed with coffee for breakfast, during work breaks, or as a quick snack throughout the day. Large national chains and independent bakeries alike contribute to Canada's thriving doughnut culture by offering both traditional favorites and creative new flavors. Regional specialties, seasonal recipes, and locally inspired ingredients have helped keep doughnuts popular across generations. The country's strong coffee culture has also encouraged the widespread pairing of fresh doughnuts with hot beverages, making them a familiar part of everyday life for many Canadians.

Did you know?

Canada's doughnut culture is strongly associated with chains such as Tim Hortons, founded in 1964, but claims that the country leads the world in doughnut shops per capita require consistent international data.
