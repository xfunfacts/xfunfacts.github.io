Puma and Adidas Were Founded by Brothers xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Puma and Adidas were founded by rival brothers whose family split created two of the world's biggest sports brands.

Puma and Adidas were founded by two brothers whose relationship changed the history of athletic footwear. Rudolf Dassler established Puma, while his younger brother Adolf "Adi" Dassler founded Adidas after the family business split in the late 1940s. Their rivalry fueled decades of innovation in sports equipment and sponsorships, helping both companies become globally recognized brands. Even today, the story remains one of the most famous family rivalries in business history.

Did you know?

Adolf and Rudolf Dassler originally operated a shoe business together in Herzogenaurach, Germany, but their deteriorating relationship led to separate companies in 1948, creating Adidas and Puma.
