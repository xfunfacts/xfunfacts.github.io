Supermarket Apples Can Be Over a Year Old xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many supermarket apples remain fresh for months through controlled storage and protective edible coatings.

Many apples sold in supermarkets were harvested months earlier and carefully stored under controlled conditions before reaching store shelves. After harvest, apples are coated with a thin edible wax to reduce moisture loss and are placed in temperature-controlled rooms with reduced oxygen levels that slow the ripening process. These storage techniques allow fresh apples to remain crisp for many months without freezing. As a result, consumers can enjoy apples throughout the year instead of only during harvest season.

Did you know?

Controlled-atmosphere storage reduces oxygen and regulates carbon dioxide around harvested apples, slowing respiration and ripening; some varieties can remain marketable for many months under carefully monitored conditions.
