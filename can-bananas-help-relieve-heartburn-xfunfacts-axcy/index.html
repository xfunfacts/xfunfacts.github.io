Can Bananas Help Relieve Heartburn? xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Bananas may help reduce heartburn for some people by coating the stomach and esophagus. Their soothing effect varies depending on the individual.

Bananas are often recommended as a gentle food for people experiencing occasional heartburn because of their soft texture and relatively low acidity. Some individuals find that bananas temporarily soothe discomfort by coating the lining of the stomach and esophagus, potentially reducing irritation caused by stomach acid. However, responses vary from person to person. While many people experience relief, others may notice little difference or even worsening symptoms depending on their digestive system and the underlying cause of their reflux. Medical experts generally consider bananas a safe option for most people with mild heartburn, especially when combined with other healthy dietary habits. Persistent or severe acid reflux should always be evaluated by a healthcare professional, as it may indicate gastroesophageal reflux disease or another medical condition requiring treatment.

Did you know?

Bananas are generally low in acidity and may be tolerated by some people with acid reflux, but there is little evidence that they coat the esophagus or reliably treat heartburn.
