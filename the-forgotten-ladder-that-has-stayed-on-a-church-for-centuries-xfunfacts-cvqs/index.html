The Forgotten Ladder That Has Stayed on a Church for Centuries xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A simple wooden ladder has sat on a Jerusalem church for centuries because no one can move it without every church authority agreeing.

One of Jerusalem's most famous curiosities is a simple wooden ladder that has remained in the same location on the Church of the Holy Sepulchre for centuries. Believed to have been left by a mason during the eighteenth century, the ladder sits on a stone ledge beneath one of the church's windows. Its continued presence is the result of the Status Quo agreement, a historic arrangement governing the shared administration of the church by six different Christian denominations. Because any significant change to certain parts of the building requires unanimous approval from all six groups, even moving the seemingly insignificant ladder has become nearly impossible. Over time, it has transformed into a symbol of the delicate balance between religious communities sharing one of Christianity's holiest sites. The forgotten ladder continues to fascinate visitors as a reminder of how history, tradition, and cooperation can preserve even the smallest objects.
