Freelancers Were Once Sword-Wielding Mercenaries xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The word freelancer originally described independent mercenary soldiers before becoming the modern term for self-employed professionals.

The word "freelancer" originally referred to medieval mercenary soldiers who offered their services to whoever paid them rather than serving a single ruler. The term gained popularity after appearing in Sir Walter Scott's 1820 novel Ivanhoe. Over time, its meaning expanded to describe independent professionals working for multiple clients. Today, freelancers contribute to industries ranging from writing and design to software development and consulting.

Did you know?

Walter Scott used the expression “free lances” in his 1820 novel Ivanhoe to describe mercenary soldiers whose weapons were not committed to one lord.
