Insects Outnumber Humans by One Billion to One xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Scientists estimate insects outnumber humans by roughly one billion to one. They are by far the most abundant group of animals on Earth.

Scientists estimate that insects outnumber humans by roughly one billion to one, making them by far the most abundant group of animals on Earth. From ants and beetles to butterflies and flies, insects occupy nearly every ecosystem, playing essential roles in pollination, decomposition, soil health, and food chains. Their extraordinary diversity includes millions of known and undiscovered species. Although many insects are tiny and often overlooked, together they form one of the planet's most important foundations for ecological balance. Without insects, countless plants, animals, and ecosystems would struggle to survive.

Did you know?

Ants alone may number in the quadrillions globally, illustrating how a few highly successful insect groups account for enormous portions of insect abundance.
