The Oldest “Your Mom” Joke Is 3,500 Years Old xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The oldest known "your mom" joke dates back about 3,500 years to ancient Babylon. Although the original tablet was later lost, the joke itself was preserved in written records.

Humor about mothers has existed for far longer than most people imagine. The oldest known "your mom" joke appears on a Babylonian clay tablet dating back roughly 3,500 years. Although the original tablet was later lost, scholars preserved its inscription before it disappeared. Much of the ancient joke is difficult to interpret because parts of the text are damaged, but its existence shows that playful insults between friends are anything but a modern invention. Even across thousands of years, human humor has remained surprisingly familiar.

Did you know?

A Babylonian tablet from roughly 3,500 years ago contains a fragment sometimes described as an ancient “your mother” joke, but the damaged text makes its exact meaning uncertain.
