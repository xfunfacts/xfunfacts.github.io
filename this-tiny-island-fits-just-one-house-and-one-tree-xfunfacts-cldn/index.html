This Tiny Island Fits Just One House and One Tree xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Imagine owning an island so tiny it fits just one house, one tree, and almost nothing else.

Hidden in the St. Lawrence River between the United States and Canada lies one of the world's smallest inhabited islands, appropriately named Just Enough Room Island. Measuring only about 310 square meters, the tiny island has barely enough space to hold a single house, one tree, a few shrubs, and a narrow strip of shoreline. The island became famous after photographs spread across the internet, capturing its remarkably crowded appearance. Purchased by the Sizeland family in the 1950s, it was intended as a peaceful summer retreat away from neighbors. Ironically, its unusual size attracted worldwide attention, turning it into one of the most photographed private islands on Earth. The property perfectly illustrates how people can make creative use of even the smallest pieces of land while creating an unforgettable geographical curiosity.

Did you know?

Just Room Enough Island, part of New York's Thousand Islands region, contains a small house and a tree; its extremely limited shoreline leaves almost no usable outdoor space.
