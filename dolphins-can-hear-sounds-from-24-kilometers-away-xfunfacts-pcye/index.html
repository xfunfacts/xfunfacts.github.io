Dolphins Can Hear Sounds from 24 Kilometers Away xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dolphins can detect underwater sounds from as far as 24 kilometers away using their exceptional hearing.

Dolphins possess extraordinarily sensitive hearing that allows them to detect underwater sounds from as far as 24 kilometers away under favorable conditions. Sound travels much faster and farther through water than through air, making hearing one of the dolphin's most important senses. Dolphins also use echolocation by producing rapid clicks and interpreting the returning echoes to locate prey, avoid obstacles, and navigate even in dark or murky water. Their sophisticated acoustic abilities rank among the most advanced in the animal kingdom and continue to inspire research into underwater communication and sonar technology.

Did you know?

Dolphins receive returning echolocation clicks through specialized tissues in the lower jaw, which help transmit sound toward the middle and inner ear.
