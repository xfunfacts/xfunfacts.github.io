Jellyfish Use One Opening for Everything xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Jellyfish use a single opening for both eating food and removing waste through their simple digestive system.

Jellyfish use a single opening for both eating food and expelling waste, making their digestive system remarkably simple. Food enters through the mouth, where it is partially digested inside the body cavity before any remaining waste exits through the same opening. Although this design appears basic, it has supported jellyfish survival for hundreds of millions of years. Their simple anatomy requires very little energy while remaining highly effective in aquatic environments. Evolution has shown that simplicity can be remarkably successful.

Did you know?

Most jellyfish have a gastrovascular cavity with a single main opening that functions in both food intake and the expulsion of undigested material.
