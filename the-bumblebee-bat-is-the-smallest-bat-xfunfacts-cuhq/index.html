The Bumblebee Bat Is the Smallest Bat xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The bumblebee bat is the world's smallest bat, measuring just over one inch long and weighing very little.

The bumblebee bat is the smallest bat species in the world and one of the tiniest mammals on Earth. Also known as Kitti's hog-nosed bat, it measures just over one inch in length and weighs less than a penny. Native to parts of Thailand and Myanmar, it lives in limestone caves near rivers and forests. Despite its tiny size, the bumblebee bat is an agile flier that feeds primarily on insects. Its miniature body demonstrates the remarkable diversity found within the bat family.

Did you know?

The bumblebee bat roosts in limestone caves in Thailand and Myanmar and is so small that it can weigh less than a U.S. penny.
