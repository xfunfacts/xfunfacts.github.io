Flying Champagne Corks Can Cause Serious Eye Injuries xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Champagne corks can fly at high speed and cause serious eye injuries if bottles are opened carelessly.

A champagne cork can leave a bottle at speeds exceeding 50 kilometers per hour, making it capable of causing serious eye injuries. The pressure inside a bottle of sparkling wine is several times greater than the pressure inside a typical car tire. To open a bottle safely, it should always be pointed away from people while the cork is held firmly and released slowly with a gentle twisting motion. Safety experts recommend avoiding loud, forceful popping whenever possible because controlled opening greatly reduces the risk of accidents.

Did you know?

Pressure inside a sparkling-wine bottle can reach approximately six atmospheres, propelling an uncontrolled cork fast enough to damage the eye's delicate structures and potentially cause permanent vision loss.
