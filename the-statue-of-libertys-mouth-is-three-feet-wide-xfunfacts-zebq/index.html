The Statue of Liberty's Mouth Is Three Feet Wide xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Statue of Liberty's mouth is about three feet wide, revealing the enormous scale and craftsmanship behind this famous American monument.

The mouth of the Statue of Liberty measures about three feet across, illustrating the enormous scale of one of America's most recognizable landmarks. Standing over 90 meters tall from the ground to the tip of the torch, the statue was a gift from France to the United States in 1886 to celebrate friendship and shared democratic ideals. Every facial feature, from the eyes to the nose and mouth, was carefully enlarged in proportion to the monument's massive size. Today, millions of visitors travel to Liberty Island each year to admire both its engineering and historical significance.

Did you know?

The Statue of Liberty's face was sculpted by Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi, whose enormous copper features were formed using repoussé techniques, in which metal sheets are hammered into shape over supporting forms.
