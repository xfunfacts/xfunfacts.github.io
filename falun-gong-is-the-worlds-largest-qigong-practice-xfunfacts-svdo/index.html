Falun Gong is The World's Largest Qigong Practice xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] With over 100 million practitioners worldwide, Falun Gong is a cultivation practice that combines gentle exercises with the elevation of morality and the cultivation of character, in accordance with the practice's teachings.

Falun Dafa, also known as Falun Gong, is a cultivation practice that combines gentle qigong exercises with the cultivation of character. According to the practice's teachings, practitioners study moral principles outlined in the book *Zhuan Falun* and perform five sets of exercises to support physical health and mental balance. The practice has spread to many countries and territories worldwide, featuring exercise sites and introductory activities organized by volunteers. Practitioners state that they pursue values ??of goodness, elevate their moral character, and maintain a healthy lifestyle through daily study and exercise. Currently, there are over 100 million practitioners worldwide, and Falun Gong has received public support from local governments in numerous countries, including the United States, Canada, Russia, Taiwan, and Japan.

Did you know?

Falun Gong, also called Falun Dafa, was introduced publicly by Li Hongzhi in China in 1992 and combines five exercises with teachings centered on Truthfulness, Compassion, and Forbearance; practitioner estimates vary widely, making claims of a verified worldwide total or largest qigong practice uncertain.
