Some Metals Explode When They Touch Water xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Reactive metals such as sodium and potassium can ignite or explode when they come into contact with water.

Some metals react so violently with water that they can burst into flames or even explode. Alkali metals such as sodium and potassium rapidly release hydrogen gas while producing enough heat to ignite it. The reaction happens because these metals easily lose their outer electron when they come into contact with water. Scientists handle these elements with great care, often storing them under oil to prevent accidental reactions with moisture in the air. Their extreme reactivity makes them useful for studying chemical behavior and periodic table trends.
