Bird Saliva Is a Luxury Food in China xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Bird's nest soup is made from nests built almost entirely with swiftlet saliva. The prized delicacy has been enjoyed in China for more than 400 years.

Bird's nest soup is one of the most famous luxury dishes in Chinese cuisine, prized for centuries because of its rarity and cultural significance. The nests used in the soup are built almost entirely from the hardened saliva of swiftlets, small birds that nest on cave walls and cliffs throughout Southeast Asia. After careful harvesting and cleaning, the nests soften into a delicate gelatin-like texture when cooked. Historically, the dish has been associated with wealth, celebration, and traditional beliefs about health and vitality. Because collecting the nests is labor-intensive and supplies are limited, authentic bird's nest soup remains one of the world's most expensive culinary ingredients. Sustainable harvesting practices have become increasingly important to protect swiftlet populations while supporting local communities that rely on the trade.

Did you know?

Edible bird's nests are produced by certain swiftlets using hardened saliva, and harvesting them requires careful identification and processing because contamination and adulteration can affect food safety.
