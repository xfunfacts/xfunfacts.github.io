Dogs Are Banned in Antarctica xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dogs are prohibited in Antarctica to protect native wildlife from diseases and environmental disruption.

Dogs are prohibited in Antarctica under international environmental agreements because they could introduce diseases that threaten native wildlife, particularly seals. Sled dogs once assisted scientific expeditions across the frozen continent, but they were completely removed during the 1990s to protect the fragile ecosystem. Antarctica is governed by strict environmental rules that limit human impact and help preserve one of the planet's most pristine natural environments. These protections are considered vital for conserving its unique wildlife.

Did you know?

Sled dogs were once used extensively during Antarctic expeditions, but they were removed from the continent before the environmental ban took full effect.
