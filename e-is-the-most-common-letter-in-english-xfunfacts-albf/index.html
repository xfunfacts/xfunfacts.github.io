E Is the Most Common Letter in English xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The letter "E" appears more frequently than any other letter in English, making it the language's most common character.

The letter "E" is the most frequently used letter in the English language, appearing more often than any other character in books, newspapers, and everyday conversation. Because of its high frequency, it plays an important role in word games, cryptography, and keyboard design. Writers have even created entire novels that avoid using the letter, a challenging literary style known as a lipogram. The popularity of "E" reflects patterns that have developed naturally over centuries of English language evolution.

Did you know?

In ordinary English text, E is usually the most frequent letter, although exact frequencies change with vocabulary, genre, and the sample being analyzed.
