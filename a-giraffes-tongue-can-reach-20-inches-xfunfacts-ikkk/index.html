A Giraffe's Tongue Can Reach 20 Inches xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A giraffe's tongue can grow to around 20 inches long, helping it feed among thorny branches and clean its face.

A giraffe's tongue can grow to around 20 inches, or about 50 centimeters, making it one of the longest tongues of any land mammal. This extraordinary length allows giraffes to reach leaves growing between sharp thorns on acacia trees, one of their favorite food sources. Their tongue is also highly flexible, muscular, and covered with a thick protective surface that helps prevent injuries while feeding. In addition to gathering food, giraffes use their long tongues to clean their noses and faces, making this remarkable organ useful in several aspects of their daily lives.

Did you know?

Giraffes spend much of the day browsing leaves from trees, especially acacias, using their lips and tongues to maneuver around sharp thorns.
