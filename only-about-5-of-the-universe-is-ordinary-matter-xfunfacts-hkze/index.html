Only About 5% of the Universe Is Ordinary Matter xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Everything visible in the universe accounts for only about 5% of its total content. Most of the cosmos remains hidden in forms scientists still cannot explain.

Everything we can see, including stars, planets, galaxies, people, and atoms, makes up only about 5% of the universe. Scientists believe the remaining 95% consists of dark matter and dark energy, mysterious components that cannot be directly observed with ordinary telescopes. Dark matter appears to provide additional gravity that shapes galaxies, while dark energy is thought to drive the accelerating expansion of the universe. Although researchers have gathered strong indirect evidence for both, their true nature remains one of the greatest unsolved mysteries in modern physics and astronomy.

Did you know?

Current cosmological measurements indicate roughly 5% ordinary matter, about 27% dark matter, and about 68% dark energy in the universe.
