Around One in 18 People Has a Third Nipple xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Around one in 18 people has an extra nipple, a harmless genetic variation known medically as polythelia.

Around one in eighteen people is born with an extra nipple, a harmless condition known as polythelia. During early embryonic development, humans briefly form structures called milk lines that extend from the armpits to the groin. In most people these disappear completely except for the chest, but occasionally a small portion remains and develops into an additional nipple. Most extra nipples are small, cause no medical problems, and often go unnoticed for years. Because the condition is relatively common and usually harmless, it serves as an interesting example of normal variation in human development.

Did you know?

An extra nipple, called a supernumerary nipple or polythelia, usually develops along the embryonic milk lines and is often harmless and unnoticed.
