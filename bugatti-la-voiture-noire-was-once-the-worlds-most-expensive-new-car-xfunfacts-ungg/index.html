Bugatti La Voiture Noire Was Once the World's Most Expensive New Car xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Bugatti La Voiture Noire debuted with a reported $19 million price, making it one of the costliest new cars ever unveiled.

When Bugatti introduced La Voiture Noire in 2019, it carried a reported price of around $19 million, making it one of the most expensive new production cars ever announced. Inspired by the legendary Bugatti Type 57 SC Atlantic, the car combines modern engineering with classic styling while featuring an eight-liter quad-turbocharged W16 engine. Built in extremely limited numbers, it represents the highest level of automotive craftsmanship and exclusivity. Vehicles like this often serve as engineering showcases as much as luxury products.

Did you know?

La Voiture Noire was a one-off Bugatti inspired by Jean Bugatti's lost Type 57 SC Atlantic, making its value depend partly on rarity and historical symbolism.
