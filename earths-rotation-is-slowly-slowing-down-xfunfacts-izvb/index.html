Earth's Rotation Is Slowly Slowing Down xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Earth's rotation is becoming slightly slower over time. The Moon's gravity is responsible for this tiny but measurable change.

Earth's rotation is gradually slowing because the Moon continuously pulls on our planet through gravity. This interaction creates ocean tides that generate friction, slowly transferring rotational energy from Earth to the Moon. As a result, Earth's days become about 1.7 milliseconds longer every century, while the Moon slowly drifts farther away. Although this change is far too small to notice in everyday life, scientists can measure it with remarkable precision using atomic clocks and laser reflections from mirrors left on the Moon during the Apollo missions.

Did you know?

Because Earth's rotation is not perfectly constant, international timekeeping sometimes uses leap seconds to keep atomic time aligned with Earth's orientation.
