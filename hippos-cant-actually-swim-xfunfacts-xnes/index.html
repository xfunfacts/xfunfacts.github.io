Hippos Can't Actually Swim xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hippos don't truly swim but move by walking and bouncing along river and lake bottoms.

Despite spending much of their lives in the water, hippos do not actually swim in the way most aquatic animals do. Their extremely dense bones make them too heavy to float easily, so they move by walking, pushing off, or gently bouncing along the bottoms of rivers and lakes. Hippos can hold their breath for several minutes and instinctively rise to the surface for air, even while sleeping. This unusual method of movement allows them to conserve energy while staying cool during the hottest parts of the day.

Did you know?

Hippos can stay underwater for several minutes by automatically holding their breath, even while sleeping in shallow water.
