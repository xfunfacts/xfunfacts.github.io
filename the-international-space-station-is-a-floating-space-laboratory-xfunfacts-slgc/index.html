The International Space Station Is a Floating Space Laboratory xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The International Space Station serves as an orbiting laboratory where astronauts conduct research in microgravity.

The International Space Station is a permanently inhabited laboratory orbiting Earth at an altitude of about 250 miles. Traveling at roughly 17,500 miles per hour, it completes one orbit around the planet approximately every 90 minutes, allowing astronauts to witness multiple sunrises and sunsets each day. Scientists aboard the station conduct experiments in biology, medicine, physics, materials science, and Earth observation that cannot be performed under normal gravity. The ISS is also a symbol of international cooperation, bringing together astronauts and researchers from many countries. Decades of research aboard the station continue to benefit both space exploration and life on Earth.

Did you know?

Experiments aboard the ISS investigate subjects ranging from human physiology and materials science to plant growth and fluid behavior in microgravity.
