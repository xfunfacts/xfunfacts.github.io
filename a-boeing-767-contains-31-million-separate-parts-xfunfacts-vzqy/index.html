A Boeing 767 Contains 3.1 Million Separate Parts xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A Boeing 767 is assembled from about 3.1 million individual parts supplied by manufacturers around the world.

A Boeing 767 contains roughly 3.1 million individual parts supplied by thousands of manufacturers around the world. These components include everything from tiny fasteners and electronic sensors to massive engines and landing gear. Modern commercial aircraft rely on strict quality control and detailed inspections to ensure every part meets rigorous safety standards. The complexity of an airliner reflects the remarkable level of international cooperation required to build modern aviation technology.

Did you know?

Modern airliners use millions of components because even a single aircraft combines complex structures, wiring, hydraulics, avionics, engines, and cabin systems.
