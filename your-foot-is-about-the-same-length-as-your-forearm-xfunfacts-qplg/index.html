Your Foot Is About the Same Length as Your Forearm xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] For many people, foot length is surprisingly close to the distance from the elbow to the wrist.

For many people, the length of the foot is surprisingly close to the distance between the elbow and the wrist, making it a useful rough body proportion. While this relationship varies from person to person, artists, clothing designers, and anthropologists have long studied body proportions to better understand human anatomy. Similar proportional guidelines have also been used throughout history in architecture and classical art. Although not precise enough for medical measurements, this comparison offers an interesting example of the body's natural symmetry.

Did you know?

Foot length and forearm length may be similar in some individuals, but body proportions vary substantially, so neither measurement reliably predicts the other.
