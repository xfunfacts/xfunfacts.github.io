Moon Footprints Can Last Millions of Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Footprints on the Moon can survive for millions of years. With no wind or rain, even Apollo astronaut tracks remain almost untouched.

Footprints left on the Moon can remain almost unchanged for millions of years because the Moon has virtually no atmosphere, wind, or flowing water to erase them. The famous tracks made by Apollo astronauts during the Moon landings still exist today, preserved in the fine lunar dust. Even the American flag appeared to wave only because astronauts twisted the flagpole while planting it. Without air, the fabric could not actually flutter in the wind. Although tiny meteorite impacts slowly disturb the surface over extremely long periods, these changes happen far more slowly than erosion on Earth. The Moon's unique environment allows human footprints and other landing equipment to remain as remarkable records of humanity's first journeys beyond our planet.

Did you know?

Micrometeorite impacts and solar radiation slowly weather the lunar surface, so astronaut footprints are long-lived but not literally permanent.
