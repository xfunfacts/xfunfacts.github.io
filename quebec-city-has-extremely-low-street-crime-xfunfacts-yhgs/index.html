Quebec City Has Extremely Low Street Crime xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Quebec City is widely recognized for exceptionally low street crime and consistently ranks among North America's safest cities.

Quebec City is frequently praised as one of the safest cities in North America, with remarkably low levels of street crime compared with many large urban areas. Its walkable historic district, active community policing, and high quality of life contribute to its reputation for safety. While no city is completely crime-free, visitors often describe Quebec City as calm and welcoming. The comparison with Disney World is intended to emphasize just how unusually low visible street crime has been reported to be.

Did you know?

Quebec City's historic districts are popular with visitors, but crime comparisons depend on whether statistics measure violent offenses, property crime, police reports, or population-adjusted rates.
