Some Golf Balls Are Filled with Honey xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Some novelty golf balls have been made with honey inside as part of unusual experimental or promotional designs.

Some specialty and novelty golf balls have been manufactured with honey inside their cores or internal chambers, often for promotional purposes, experimentation, or unique product designs rather than competitive play. Standard tournament golf balls instead use carefully engineered rubber and synthetic materials to maximize performance and durability. The honey-filled versions demonstrate how manufacturers occasionally explore unconventional designs to attract attention or test creative concepts, adding another unusual chapter to the long history of golf equipment.

Did you know?

Honey-filled golf balls are novelty products rather than standard sporting equipment, showing how manufacturers sometimes use unusual fillings for promotional effects.
