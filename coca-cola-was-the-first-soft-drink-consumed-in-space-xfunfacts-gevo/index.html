Coca-Cola Was the First Soft Drink Consumed in Space xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Coca-Cola earned an unusual place in space history as the first commercial soft drink consumed in orbit. Engineers even designed a special container to make it possible.

Coca-Cola became the first commercial soft drink consumed in space after specially designed containers were developed for astronauts. Ordinary soda cannot simply be opened in microgravity because the lack of gravity prevents bubbles and liquid from separating normally. Engineers created a unique delivery system that allowed astronauts to safely drink carbonated beverages without creating floating droplets throughout the spacecraft. The achievement became a memorable piece of space history and is still celebrated by Coca-Cola today, including a UFO-themed vending machine displayed in Roswell, New Mexico, highlighting the company's connection to space exploration.

Did you know?

NASA tested specially designed beverage containers in orbit because carbonation behaves differently in microgravity and ordinary bottles would not work normally.
