Underwater Volcanoes Can Trigger Tsunamis xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Underwater volcanic eruptions can trigger tsunamis by rapidly displacing massive amounts of seawater beneath the ocean.

Volcanic eruptions beneath the ocean can sometimes generate tsunamis by rapidly displacing enormous amounts of seawater. Underwater explosions, collapsing volcanic slopes, or massive landslides triggered by eruptions may all create powerful waves capable of traveling across entire ocean basins. Although earthquakes are the most common cause of tsunamis, volcanic events have produced several devastating examples throughout history. Modern monitoring systems help detect these hazards and provide early warnings. Understanding submarine volcanoes remains an important part of tsunami research.

Did you know?

Submarine eruptions can generate tsunamis when explosions, caldera collapse, landslides, or rapid displacement of water occurs, but most underwater eruptions do not create destructive waves.
