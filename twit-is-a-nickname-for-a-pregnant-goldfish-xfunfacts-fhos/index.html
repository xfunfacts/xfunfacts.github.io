Twit Is a Nickname for a Pregnant Goldfish xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] "Twit" is an informal nickname sometimes used for a pregnant goldfish, though it is not an official biological term.

The informal nickname "twit" has occasionally been used to describe a pregnant goldfish, although it is not an official scientific or biological term. Fish enthusiasts and hobbyists sometimes create colorful slang to describe different stages of breeding and development. Because these expressions vary by region and community, they are rarely used in formal publications. Scientific terminology instead focuses on reproductive biology rather than informal nicknames.

Did you know?

Goldfish do not actually become pregnant because they lay eggs; females develop and release eggs that are fertilized externally by males.
