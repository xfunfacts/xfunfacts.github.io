Libya Once Held the Record for the Hottest Temperature Ever Recorded xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] El Azizia in Libya once held the world's hottest temperature record before scientists later reviewed and overturned the measurement.

For many years, El Azizia in Libya was believed to hold the record for the highest air temperature ever measured after a reading of 57.8°C was reported in 1922. However, in 2012 the World Meteorological Organization determined that the measurement was unreliable because of likely observational errors and problems with the equipment. The official record is now 56.7°C, recorded at Furnace Creek in California's Death Valley in 1913. The revision highlights how scientific records are continually reviewed as new evidence becomes available.

Did you know?

The El Azizia record was rejected by the World Meteorological Organization in 2012 after investigators found serious problems with the instrument and observing conditions.
