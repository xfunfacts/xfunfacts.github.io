Mars Has Planet-Wide Dust Storms xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Planet-wide dust storms can engulf Mars for weeks, dramatically changing visibility and surface conditions.

Mars experiences some of the largest dust storms in the solar system, with certain storms growing so large that they can cover the entire planet. These global dust storms may last for several weeks or even months before gradually fading. Fine dust particles are lifted into the atmosphere by strong winds and can block much of the sunlight reaching the surface. This presents serious challenges for solar-powered spacecraft, as seen when NASA's Opportunity rover lost power during a massive storm in 2018. Scientists continue studying these storms to better understand Martian weather and climate. Learning how dust behaves on Mars is also essential for planning future human missions and protecting equipment on the planet.

Did you know?

Global Martian dust storms can reduce sunlight so dramatically that solar-powered spacecraft may struggle to generate enough electricity to operate.
