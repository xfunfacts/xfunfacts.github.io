Female Chickens Can Reject Sperm from Certain Roosters xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hens can influence fertilization by selectively rejecting sperm from certain roosters after mating.

Hens are capable of influencing which rooster fathers their chicks through a process known as cryptic female choice. After mating, a female chicken can sometimes reduce the chances of fertilization by sperm from less desirable males while favoring sperm from healthier or more dominant roosters. This remarkable reproductive strategy gives females greater control over the genetic quality of their offspring. Similar forms of post-mating selection have also been observed in several other bird species.

Did you know?

Hens can store sperm for weeks after mating, allowing them to fertilize multiple eggs without needing to mate again each day.
