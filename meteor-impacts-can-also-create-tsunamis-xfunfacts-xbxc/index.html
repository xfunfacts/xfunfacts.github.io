Meteor Impacts Can Also Create Tsunamis xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Large meteor impacts in the ocean can generate massive tsunami waves, although such events are extremely rare.

Although extremely rare, a large meteor striking the ocean could generate enormous tsunami waves by displacing massive amounts of seawater in a very short time. The size of the resulting tsunami would depend on the meteor's speed, mass, impact angle, and location. Geological evidence suggests that ancient asteroid impacts likely produced giant tsunamis during Earth's history. Fortunately, such events are exceptionally uncommon compared with earthquake-generated tsunamis. Astronomers continue monitoring near-Earth objects to identify potential future impact risks.

Did you know?

A large ocean impact can displace enormous volumes of water and generate tsunami-like waves, but the effects depend strongly on impact size, depth, and distance from shore.
