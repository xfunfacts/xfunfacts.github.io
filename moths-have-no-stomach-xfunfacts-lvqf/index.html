Moths Have No Stomach xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many adult moth species have no functional stomach and survive using energy stored from their caterpillar stage.

Adult moths in many species do not have a functional stomach or mouthparts capable of feeding, relying entirely on energy stored during the caterpillar stage. Their brief adult lives are dedicated primarily to reproduction rather than growth or feeding. However, not all moths share this trait, and numerous species still feed on nectar, fruit, or tree sap. This wide diversity of life histories demonstrates the remarkable variety found within one of the largest groups of insects on Earth.

Did you know?

Some adult moths, including certain silk moths, live only long enough to mate and reproduce because they cannot feed after emerging.
