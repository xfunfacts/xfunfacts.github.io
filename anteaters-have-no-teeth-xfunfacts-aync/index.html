Anteaters Have No Teeth xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Anteaters have no teeth and rely on long sticky tongues to catch thousands of insects each day.

Anteaters have no teeth, relying instead on their long, sticky tongues to capture ants and termites by the thousands. Their tongues can extend up to two feet in some species and flick in and out more than 150 times per minute while feeding. Powerful stomach muscles and swallowed sand help grind food internally since chewing is impossible. Specialized claws allow anteaters to tear open insect nests while thick fur protects them from bites. These adaptations make anteaters highly efficient insect hunters.
