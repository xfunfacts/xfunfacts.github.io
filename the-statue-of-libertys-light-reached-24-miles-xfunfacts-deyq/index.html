The Statue of Liberty's Light Reached 24 Miles xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Statue of Liberty's lighthouse beacon once guided ships from as far as 24 miles across New York Harbor.

After its completion in 1886, the Statue of Liberty briefly served as a functioning lighthouse, with its powerful beacon visible from as far as 24 miles across New York Harbor. Although the light was eventually considered too dim for the growing needs of maritime navigation, it successfully guided ships entering one of America's busiest ports. The statue later became a universal symbol of freedom and hope for millions of immigrants arriving by sea. Today, its historical role as both a monument and navigational aid is often overlooked.

Did you know?

The statue's original torch was replaced in 1986, and the present flame is covered in thin sheets of 24-karat gold leaf.
