The Sun Will Become a Red Giant xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Sun will eventually expand into a red giant as it exhausts its hydrogen fuel billions of years from now.

The Sun is currently a stable, medium-sized star, but in about five billion years it will expand into a red giant as it exhausts the hydrogen fuel in its core. During this stage, its outer layers will grow enormously, likely engulfing Mercury and Venus and possibly reaching Earth's orbit. Eventually, the Sun will shed its outer layers and leave behind a dense white dwarf. This transformation is a normal part of stellar evolution for stars of similar size. Studying the Sun helps scientists understand the life cycles of billions of stars throughout the universe.

Did you know?

Before becoming a red giant, the Sun will spend roughly another five billion years fusing hydrogen in its core during its main-sequence phase.
