Hagfish Have Skulls but No True Spines xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hagfish are the only known animals with a skull but no true backbone, making them evolutionarily unique.

The hagfish is one of the most unusual vertebrates on Earth because it possesses a skull but lacks a true backbone. Instead of vertebrae, its body is supported mainly by a flexible rod called a notochord, a structure retained from early vertebrate evolution. Hagfish are also famous for producing enormous amounts of slime when threatened, helping them escape predators. Their primitive anatomy provides scientists with important clues about the early evolution of vertebrate animals.

Did you know?

Hagfish possess a skull but lack true vertebrae like those of most vertebrates, making their anatomy important for studying the early evolution of vertebrate skeletons.
