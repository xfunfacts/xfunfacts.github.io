The U.S. Population Once Increased Every 12 Seconds xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] In early 2004, the U.S. population grew by about one person every 12 seconds through births, deaths, and immigration.

At the beginning of 2004, the United States population was estimated to increase by roughly one person every 12 seconds. This net growth reflected the combined effects of births, deaths, and immigration, with births occurring more frequently than deaths. Population growth rates change over time because of economic conditions, healthcare, migration patterns, and demographic trends. Government agencies regularly update these statistics to help guide planning for housing, education, transportation, and public services.

Did you know?

Population growth combines births, deaths, and net international migration, so a net gain of one person every 12 seconds represents an average rather than someone arriving at perfectly regular intervals.
