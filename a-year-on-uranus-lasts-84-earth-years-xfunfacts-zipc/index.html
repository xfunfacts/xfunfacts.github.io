A Year on Uranus Lasts 84 Earth Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Uranus takes 84 Earth years to complete one orbit, resulting in extraordinarily long seasons.

A single year on Uranus lasts about 84 Earth years because the planet orbits nearly 1.8 billion miles from the Sun. Traveling such a vast distance means Uranus moves much more slowly around the Sun than the inner planets. Combined with its dramatic axial tilt, this long orbit creates seasons unlike anywhere else in the solar system. Each season lasts about 21 Earth years, giving scientists the rare opportunity to observe long-term atmospheric changes over decades. Understanding Uranus' lengthy orbit helps researchers compare planetary climates and learn more about how distance from a star influences weather and environmental conditions.

Did you know?

Uranus was discovered in 1781, so it did not complete its first full orbit after discovery until 1865, about 84 years later.
