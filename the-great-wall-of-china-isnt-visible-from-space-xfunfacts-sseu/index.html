The Great Wall of China Isn't Visible from Space xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Great Wall of China is not normally visible from space with the naked eye. The famous belief comes from a long-standing myth rather than astronaut observations.

Despite one of the world's most widespread myths, the Great Wall of China cannot normally be seen with the naked eye from low Earth orbit. Although the wall stretches for thousands of miles, it is relatively narrow and blends into the surrounding landscape, making it extremely difficult to distinguish from space without optical aids. Astronauts aboard the International Space Station have confirmed that many cities, roads, airports, and farmland are actually easier to spot. The myth likely became popular long before humans ever traveled into space, showing how persistent misconceptions can become accepted as fact.

Did you know?

The Great Wall can sometimes appear in photographs from orbit under favorable conditions, but astronauts generally cannot distinguish it easily with unaided vision.
