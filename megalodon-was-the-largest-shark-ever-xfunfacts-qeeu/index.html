Megalodon Was the Largest Shark Ever xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Megalodon was the largest shark ever known, reaching enormous sizes that made it one of history's greatest marine predators.

Megalodon was the largest shark known to have lived, reaching estimated lengths of around 18 meters with enormous teeth measuring up to 18 centimeters long. This prehistoric giant dominated the oceans millions of years ago, feeding on whales and other large marine mammals. Although only fossilized teeth and vertebrae remain, scientists have reconstructed much of its appearance by comparing it with modern sharks. Megalodon eventually became extinct several million years ago, likely because of changing climates, shifting food supplies, and competition from other predators. It remains one of the most famous prehistoric animals ever discovered.

Did you know?

Megalodon is estimated from fossil teeth and vertebrae because sharks have cartilaginous skeletons that rarely fossilize, leaving its exact maximum size uncertain.
