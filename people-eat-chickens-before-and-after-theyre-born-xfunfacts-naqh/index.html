People Eat Chickens Before and After They're Born xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Chickens are one of the few foods people eat both before birth as eggs and later as meat after adulthood.

Chickens are unusual because people commonly eat them both before and after they are born. Eggs provide a rich source of protein long before a chick develops, while adult chickens are among the most widely consumed meats in the world. This combination has made chickens one of humanity's most important domesticated animals for thousands of years. Today, billions of chickens are raised annually to supply both eggs and meat across nearly every country.

Did you know?

Commercial chicken eggs are usually unfertilized, meaning no embryo develops inside them; fertilized eggs only develop into chicks when kept under suitable incubation conditions.
