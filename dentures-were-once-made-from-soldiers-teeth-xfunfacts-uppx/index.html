Dentures Were Once Made from Soldiers' Teeth xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Early dentures were sometimes made from soldiers' teeth before modern dental materials transformed prosthetic dentistry.

Before modern plastics and ceramics transformed dentistry, dentures were sometimes made using teeth taken from deceased individuals, including soldiers killed in battle. Following major conflicts such as the Battle of Waterloo, human teeth became highly valued for dental prosthetics because they were often healthier than those available from living donors. These dentures were expensive and uncomfortable by today's standards, but they represented advanced dental technology for their time. Modern dentistry now relies on durable synthetic materials that are safer, stronger, and far more hygienic.

Did you know?

Human teeth were sometimes used in eighteenth- and nineteenth-century dentures, including teeth reportedly collected after battles such as Waterloo.
