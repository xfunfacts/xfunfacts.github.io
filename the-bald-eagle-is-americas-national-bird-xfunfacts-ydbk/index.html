The Bald Eagle Is America's National Bird xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The bald eagle has represented the United States since 1782 and remains a lasting symbol of freedom and resilience.

The bald eagle has served as the national bird and emblem of the United States since 1782, symbolizing freedom, strength, and resilience. Despite its name, the bird is not actually bald. Its white-feathered head simply contrasts with its dark brown body. Bald eagles can have wingspans exceeding seven feet and possess exceptional eyesight that allows them to spot fish from great heights. Once endangered due to habitat loss and pesticide use, conservation efforts helped their population recover dramatically. Today, the bald eagle stands as one of America's greatest wildlife conservation success stories.

Did you know?

Bald eagles were removed from the U.S. endangered species list in 2007 after major population recovery driven by legal protection and conservation efforts.
