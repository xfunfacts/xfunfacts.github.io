Venus Has Crushing Surface Pressure xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Surface pressure on Venus is about 92 times greater than Earth's, creating one of the harshest planetary environments.

The atmospheric pressure on the surface of Venus is about 92 times greater than Earth's, creating conditions similar to those found nearly one kilometer beneath Earth's oceans. This crushing pressure, combined with extreme heat, has destroyed many spacecraft within a short time after landing. Only a handful of specially designed probes have survived long enough to transmit images and scientific measurements from the surface. The dense atmosphere also slows descending spacecraft dramatically, allowing parachutes to remain effective much longer than they would on Earth. Studying these extreme conditions helps engineers design future missions capable of exploring one of the harshest environments in the solar system.

Did you know?

Venusian surface pressure is comparable to the pressure experienced roughly 900 meters beneath Earth's oceans, making ordinary spacecraft structures difficult to protect.
