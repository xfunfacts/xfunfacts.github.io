The First Angel Food Cake Recipe Was Published in 1878 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The earliest known Angel Food Cake recipe was published in 1878. The light, fluffy dessert has remained popular for nearly 150 years.

Angel Food Cake has delighted dessert lovers for well over a century, with the earliest known published recipe appearing in 1878. Famous for its incredibly light and airy texture, the cake is made primarily from whipped egg whites, sugar, and flour without using butter or egg yolks. The large amount of beaten egg whites creates countless tiny air bubbles that expand during baking, giving the cake its signature fluffy structure. Because it contains little fat, Angel Food Cake became popular as a lighter alternative to richer desserts. Traditionally baked in a tall tube pan to support its delicate texture, it is often served with fresh fruit, whipped cream, or simple glazes. Its enduring popularity demonstrates how a simple recipe can become a timeless classic in baking history.

Did you know?

An angel food cake recipe appeared in The Home Messenger Book of Tested Recipes in 1878; its airy structure comes from whipped egg whites rather than butter or conventional chemical leavening.
