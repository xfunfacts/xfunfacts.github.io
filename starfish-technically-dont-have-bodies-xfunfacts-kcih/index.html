Starfish Technically Don't Have Bodies xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Sea stars have an unusual body plan that leads scientists to describe them as being almost entirely made up of repeating head-like structures.

Sea stars, commonly called starfish, have body structures that differ dramatically from those of most familiar animals. Instead of having a clearly separated head, torso, and tail, many scientists describe their anatomy as consisting largely of repeated structures radiating from a central disc. Because their nervous system, digestive system, and sensory organs are arranged throughout their arms, some biologists jokingly describe them as being "all head." While this phrase simplifies a much more complex anatomy, it emphasizes just how unusual echinoderms are compared with vertebrates and many other animals.

Did you know?

Recent developmental research suggests the sea star body plan is dominated by head-like genetic patterning, but saying they literally have no body is an oversimplified interpretation.
