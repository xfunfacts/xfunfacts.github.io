Vernors Is America’s Oldest Soda Still Sold Today xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Vernors, created in 1866, is the oldest soft drink still sold in the United States. The famous ginger ale was invented by Detroit pharmacist James Vernor.

Vernors Ginger Ale is recognized as the oldest soft drink still sold continuously in the United States. Created in 1866 by Detroit pharmacist James Vernor, the beverage developed its distinctive flavor after aging in oak barrels, giving it a smooth vanilla-like taste unlike most other ginger ales. Over more than 150 years, Vernors has become a regional favorite, particularly in Michigan, where it is often enjoyed on its own or mixed into traditional ice cream floats. Its remarkable longevity makes it one of America's most enduring beverage brands and an important part of soft drink history.

Did you know?

Detroit pharmacist James Vernor introduced his ginger ale in 1866, making Vernors one of America's oldest surviving soft drink brands, although claims about the oldest soda depend on historical definitions.
