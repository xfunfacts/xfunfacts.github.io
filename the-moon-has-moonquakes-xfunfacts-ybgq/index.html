The Moon Has Moonquakes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Moonquakes are lunar earthquakes that can last much longer than earthquakes on Earth because of the Moon's dry interior.

The Moon experiences earthquakes known as moonquakes, although they differ from earthquakes on Earth in several important ways. Moonquakes are generally weaker but can last for much longer because the Moon's dry, rigid interior allows vibrations to continue with little energy loss. Some moonquakes are triggered by tidal forces from Earth's gravity, while others result from meteorite impacts or gradual cooling of the Moon's interior. Instruments placed on the lunar surface by Apollo astronauts detected thousands of these seismic events. Studying moonquakes helps scientists learn about the Moon's internal structure and supports planning for future lunar bases.

Did you know?

Some shallow moonquakes can reach surprisingly strong magnitudes, and their vibrations may persist because the dry lunar crust absorbs seismic energy less efficiently than Earth's.
