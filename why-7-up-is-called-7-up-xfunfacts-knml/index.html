Why 7-UP Is Called 7-UP xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The exact origin of the name 7-UP is uncertain, but one theory links the 7 to its original bottle size and UP to its rising bubbles.

The soft drink 7-UP was introduced in 1929, but the exact origin of its name has never been officially confirmed. One of the most popular explanations suggests that the number "7" referred to the original 7-ounce bottle, while "UP" represented the lively upward movement of the drink's bubbles. Other theories connect the name to the beverage's original ingredients or branding strategy, but no single explanation has been verified by the company. Regardless of its true origin, the memorable name helped establish 7-UP as one of the world's most recognizable lemon-lime soft drinks and has remained unchanged for nearly a century.

Did you know?

7 Up debuted in 1929 under the much longer name Bib-Label Lithiated Lemon-Lime Soda and originally contained lithium citrate; the true origin of its shorter name remains uncertain despite several popular theories.
