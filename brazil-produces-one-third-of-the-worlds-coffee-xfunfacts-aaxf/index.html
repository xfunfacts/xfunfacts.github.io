Brazil Produces One-Third of the World's Coffee xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Brazil produces about one-third of the world's coffee, supplying beans to markets across every continent.

Brazil produces about one-third of the world's coffee, making it the largest coffee-producing country on Earth. Vast plantations spread across regions with favorable climates and fertile soils provide ideal conditions for growing both Arabica and Robusta coffee varieties. Coffee has played a major role in Brazil's economy and history for more than a century, supporting millions of jobs in farming, transportation, processing, and export industries. Harvest seasons vary by region, and careful cultivation techniques help ensure consistent quality. Brazil's leadership in coffee production has made it one of the most influential countries in the global coffee market, supplying beans to consumers across nearly every continent.

Did you know?

Brazil grows both Arabica and Robusta-type coffee, with production concentrated in states such as Minas Gerais and Espírito Santo; its global market share changes with harvests and weather conditions.
