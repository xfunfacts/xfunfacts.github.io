Fruit Salad Trees Are Real xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Fruit salad trees use grafting to grow several different kinds of fruit on a single tree.

Fruit salad trees are real horticultural creations made through a technique called grafting, in which branches from different fruit trees are joined onto a single healthy trunk. As long as the fruits belong to closely related species, one tree can successfully produce peaches, plums, apricots, nectarines, cherries, and other fruits during different seasons. Grafting has been practiced for thousands of years to improve orchards, preserve valuable varieties, and increase productivity. These remarkable trees demonstrate both the flexibility of plant biology and the skill of experienced gardeners.

Did you know?

Fruit salad trees are created by grafting compatible varieties onto one rootstock, so a single tree can bear multiple cultivars, but unrelated fruit species generally cannot be combined successfully.
