Hundreds of Thousands of Pieces of Space Junk Orbit Earth xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hundreds of thousands of pieces of space junk orbit Earth, challenging scientists to protect satellites and future missions.

Hundreds of thousands of pieces of space junk currently orbit Earth, including old satellites, discarded rocket stages, and fragments created by collisions or explosions. Even tiny pieces of debris can travel at speeds exceeding 17,000 miles per hour, making them dangerous to active satellites and crewed spacecraft. Space agencies around the world carefully track the largest objects to reduce collision risks. Engineers are also developing technologies to remove debris and improve the long-term sustainability of space operations. Managing space junk has become one of the greatest challenges of the modern space age.

Did you know?

Much larger numbers of tiny debris fragments are too small to track individually, yet even millimeter-sized objects can damage spacecraft because of their orbital speed.
