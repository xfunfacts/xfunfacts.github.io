An 18-Year-Old Played the Child Grinch xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The young Grinch seen in the live-action movie was played by an 18-year-old actor, not an eight-year-old child.

One memorable casting decision in How the Grinch Stole Christmas involved an adult actor portraying a young child. The eight-year-old version of the Grinch was played by actor Josh Ryan Evans, who was eighteen years old during filming. Evans had a form of dwarfism that gave him a youthful appearance, allowing him to convincingly portray the young Grinch on screen. His performance became one of the film's most memorable flashback sequences, contributing to the emotional backstory of the character. Tragically, Evans passed away from complications related to a congenital heart condition just two years after the movie's release. His death occurred on the same day that the television series Passions aired the death of the character he portrayed, creating a heartbreaking coincidence remembered by many fans. Despite his short career, Evans left a lasting impression through his memorable performances and remarkable talent.

Did you know?

The young Grinch in the 2000 film was played by Josh Ryan Evans, who was actually 18 during production despite portraying the character as a child.
