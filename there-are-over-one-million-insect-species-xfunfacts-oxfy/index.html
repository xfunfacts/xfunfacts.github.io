There Are Over One Million Insect Species xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Scientists have identified more than one million insect species, making insects the most diverse group of animals on Earth.

Scientists have officially identified more than one million species of insects, making them by far the most diverse group of animals on Earth. New insect species continue to be discovered every year, especially in tropical rainforests and other understudied habitats. Insects perform essential ecological roles by pollinating plants, recycling nutrients, controlling pests, and serving as food for countless other animals. Their incredible adaptability has allowed them to thrive in almost every environment, from deserts and forests to freshwater ecosystems. Researchers believe that many millions of insect species may still remain undiscovered.

Did you know?

Insects account for more than half of all described animal species, yet scientists believe many millions of insect species remain undiscovered.
