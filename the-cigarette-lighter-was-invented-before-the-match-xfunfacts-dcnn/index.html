The Cigarette Lighter Was Invented Before the Match xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The first practical cigarette lighter was invented before modern friction matches, reversing what most people expect.

Many people assume matches came first, but the earliest practical cigarette lighter actually predates the modern friction match. Early lighters used chemical reactions to ignite a flame, while reliable friction matches were developed later in the nineteenth century. Although today's disposable lighters are simple and inexpensive, they are descendants of much larger and more complicated devices. This surprising timeline shows that technological progress does not always follow the order people expect.

Did you know?

Johann Wolfgang Döbereiner developed a hydrogen-producing lighter in 1823, before John Walker introduced friction matches in 1826; however, earlier forms of matches and fire-starting devices had existed for centuries.
