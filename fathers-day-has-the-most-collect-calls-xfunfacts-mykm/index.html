Father's Day Has the Most Collect Calls xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Father's Day generated more collect calls than any other day before mobile phones became widespread.

Before mobile phones became widespread, collect calls allowed the receiving person to pay for a telephone conversation. Father's Day consistently generated more collect calls than any other day of the year, as many children and families used the service to stay connected without paying upfront. Although collect calls are now relatively uncommon, they represent an interesting chapter in the history of long-distance communication before modern mobile networks.

Did you know?

Collect calls once allowed recipients to pay for long-distance calls, making them especially useful before mobile phones and inexpensive nationwide calling became common.
