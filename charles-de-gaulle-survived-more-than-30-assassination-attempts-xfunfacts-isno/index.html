Charles de Gaulle Survived More Than 30 Assassination Attempts xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Charles de Gaulle survived more than 30 assassination attempts before later dying of natural causes at age 79.

Former French president Charles de Gaulle survived more than 30 assassination attempts during his lifetime before eventually dying of natural causes in 1970. Many of the attacks occurred during the Algerian War, when extremist groups opposed his policies toward Algerian independence. One of the most famous attempts took place in 1962, when gunmen fired numerous shots at his moving car, yet he escaped unharmed. His survival through repeated attacks became an enduring symbol of his resilience and political determination during one of France's most turbulent periods.

Did you know?

De Gaulle survived several serious assassination plots, particularly during the Algerian War, but exact totals vary depending on how historians count individual attempts.
