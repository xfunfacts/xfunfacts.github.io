Dogs Can Hear Much Higher Frequencies Than Humans xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dogs can hear sounds at much higher frequencies than humans, allowing them to detect noises people cannot hear.

Dogs can hear sounds at much higher frequencies than humans, allowing them to detect noises that are completely inaudible to people. While humans typically hear frequencies up to about 20,000 hertz, many dogs can detect sounds approaching 45,000 hertz or more. This sensitive hearing helps dogs notice distant movements, communicate with other dogs, and respond quickly to changes in their environment. It also explains why dogs often react to whistles or electronic devices long before humans notice anything unusual. Their exceptional hearing is another reason they excel as working and companion animals.

Did you know?

Dogs can hear ultrasonic dog whistles because their upper hearing range extends well beyond the frequencies normally detectable by human ears.
