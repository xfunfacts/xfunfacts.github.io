Jupiter May Have a Metallic Hydrogen Layer xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Scientists believe Jupiter contains a deep layer of metallic hydrogen that helps generate its enormous magnetic field.

Scientists believe Jupiter contains an enormous layer of metallic hydrogen deep beneath its thick atmosphere. Under pressures millions of times greater than those on Earth, ordinary hydrogen is compressed into a remarkable state where it behaves like an electrically conductive metal. This unusual layer is thought to surround Jupiter's dense central core and plays a crucial role in generating the planet's immense magnetic field. Metallic hydrogen cannot be produced easily in laboratories, making Jupiter a natural laboratory for studying matter under extreme conditions. Understanding this exotic material helps scientists learn more about giant planets both within our solar system and around distant stars.

Did you know?

Metallic hydrogen is thought to conduct electricity extremely well, making it a major contributor to Jupiter's powerful planetary dynamo.
