Iceland Has Almost No Mosquitoes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Iceland has no established mosquito population because its unique climate interrupts the insects' breeding cycle.

Unlike almost every other country, Iceland has no established mosquito population. Scientists believe the island's unusual climate is largely responsible because repeated freezing and thawing disrupts the insects' breeding cycle before larvae can mature. Although a few mosquitoes occasionally arrive through travel or changing weather conditions, they have not formed permanent populations. Iceland's unique environment continues to make it one of the few places where residents and visitors rarely need to worry about mosquito bites.

Did you know?

Some mosquito species have occasionally reached Iceland aboard aircraft, but they have failed to establish permanent breeding populations.
