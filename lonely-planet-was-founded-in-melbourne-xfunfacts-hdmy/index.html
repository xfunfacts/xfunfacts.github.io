Lonely Planet Was Founded in Melbourne xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Lonely Planet began in Melbourne in 1973 and grew into one of the world's best-known travel guide publishers.

Lonely Planet was founded in Melbourne, Australia, in 1973 by Tony and Maureen Wheeler after they completed an overland journey through Europe and Asia. Their first travel guide was created to help budget travelers navigate unfamiliar destinations using practical advice based on personal experience. The company grew into one of the world's best-known travel publishers, producing guidebooks covering countries, cities, and regions across the globe. Its success helped shape modern independent travel culture.

Did you know?

Tony and Maureen Wheeler founded Lonely Planet after traveling overland from Europe to Asia and Australia; their first guidebook, Across Asia on the Cheap, was published in 1973 and helped popularize independent budget travel.
