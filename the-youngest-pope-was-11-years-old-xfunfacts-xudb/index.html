The Youngest Pope Was 11 Years Old xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Some historical accounts suggest the youngest pope was only 11 years old, though historians continue to debate the claim.

According to some historical accounts, the youngest pope may have been around 11 years old when he assumed the papacy, although historians continue to debate the accuracy of these records. Medieval documentation is often incomplete, making exact ages difficult to verify with certainty. Regardless of the precise number, the claim highlights how political influence and noble family connections played a major role in the selection of church leaders during parts of medieval European history.

Did you know?

Benedict IX is frequently named the youngest pope, yet estimates of his age vary from early adolescence to adulthood because reliable records of his birth are lacking.
