Daniel Radcliffe Was Allergic to Harry Potter’s Glasses xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Harry Potter's famous round glasses looked magical on screen, but they left Daniel Radcliffe with a real allergic reaction.

During the filming of the Harry Potter movies, Daniel Radcliffe experienced an unexpected problem caused by one of the franchise's most recognizable props-his round wire-frame glasses. After wearing the glasses repeatedly, he developed an allergic skin reaction around the sides of his face where the frames touched his skin. The irritation was eventually traced to nickel, a metal commonly used in inexpensive alloys that can trigger allergic contact dermatitis in sensitive individuals. Once the cause was identified, the production team replaced the original glasses with hypoallergenic versions, allowing filming to continue without further issues. This little-known behind-the-scenes fact demonstrates how even small costume details can affect actors during long film productions. It also raised awareness of nickel allergies, one of the most common metal allergies worldwide, affecting millions of people who wear jewelry, watches, or eyeglasses.
