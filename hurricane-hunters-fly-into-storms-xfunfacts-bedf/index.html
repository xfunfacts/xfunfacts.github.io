Hurricane Hunters Fly Into Storms xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hurricane Hunter aircraft fly directly into storms, collecting vital weather data that improves forecasts and public safety.

Hurricane Hunter aircraft are specially equipped to fly directly into hurricanes and collect real-time weather data that satellites cannot measure accurately. These aircraft release instruments called dropsondes that record temperature, humidity, wind speed, air pressure, and other important conditions throughout the storm. The information helps meteorologists improve hurricane forecasts and issue more accurate warnings. Flying through a hurricane is dangerous, but experienced crews and specially designed aircraft make these missions possible. Their work has saved countless lives by improving storm prediction.

Did you know?

Hurricane Hunter crews release instruments called dropsondes into storms to measure temperature, pressure, humidity, and wind from the aircraft to the ocean surface.
