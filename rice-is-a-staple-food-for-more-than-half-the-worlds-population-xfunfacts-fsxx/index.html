Rice Is a Staple Food for More Than Half the World's Population xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Rice is a staple food for more than half of the world's population and provides daily nutrition for billions of people.

Rice is the staple food for more than half of the world's population, providing a major source of calories for billions of people, especially across Asia. Thousands of rice varieties are cultivated in different climates, each adapted to local traditions, cooking methods, and agricultural conditions. In addition to supplying energy through carbohydrates, rice supports the livelihoods of millions of farmers worldwide. Because of its importance to global food security, scientists continue developing new rice varieties that produce higher yields while resisting pests, diseases, drought, and flooding.

Did you know?

Rice supplies a major share of dietary calories across Asia and other regions, with irrigated rice paddies supporting intensive agriculture while also contributing methane emissions under flooded conditions.
