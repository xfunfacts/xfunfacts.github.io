Buzz Aldrin Voiced a Character in Mass Effect 3 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Buzz Aldrin didn't just walk on the Moon. He also appeared in Mass Effect 3, lending his voice to a memorable character.

Buzz Aldrin became one of the first humans to walk on the Moon during the Apollo 11 mission, but decades later he also contributed to the world of science fiction gaming. In the 2012 video game Mass Effect 3, Aldrin voiced the character known as Stargazer, an elderly storyteller who reflects on humanity's future among the stars. His appearance carried powerful symbolic meaning because players were hearing the voice of a real astronaut who had personally helped make space exploration history. The casting added authenticity and emotional depth to the game's themes of discovery, exploration, and humanity's place in the universe. Aldrin has long supported public interest in science, technology, and space travel, making his participation especially fitting. His performance stands as a memorable example of how real-world pioneers sometimes contribute directly to fictional stories inspired by the very achievements they helped make possible.

Did you know?

Buzz Aldrin, the second person to walk on the Moon, voiced the elderly Stargazer in Mass Effect 3, connecting the game's fictional exploration of distant worlds with a real pioneer of space travel.
