Jupiter's Great Red Spot Has Raged for Over 200 Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Jupiter's Great Red Spot has remained active for more than 200 years as one of the largest storms ever observed.

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a gigantic storm that has been raging for more than 200 years and is large enough to fit Earth inside it. Scientists have continuously observed the storm since the nineteenth century, although it has gradually become smaller over time. Powerful winds within the Great Red Spot can exceed 400 miles per hour, making it one of the longest-lasting storms ever recorded. Studying this enormous vortex helps researchers understand the complex weather systems found on giant gas planets.

Did you know?

The Great Red Spot rotates counterclockwise and is an anticyclonic storm, with winds around its edges reaching hundreds of kilometers per hour.
