The Bible Has Been Translated Into Over 3,000 Languages xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Bible has reached more languages than any other book, including translations created for fictional worlds like Klingon and Elvish.

The Bible is the most widely translated book in human history, with portions now available in more than 3,000 languages spoken across the globe. Some languages contain complete translations of both the Old and New Testaments, while others currently include only selected books or passages. The enormous translation effort has taken centuries and continues today as linguists and religious organizations work to make biblical texts accessible to communities with previously unwritten or endangered languages. Beyond natural languages, creative translations have even appeared in fictional languages such as Klingon, Elvish, and Na'vi, reflecting the cultural influence of both the Bible and popular entertainment franchises. The continuing translation project represents one of humanity's largest linguistic and publishing efforts, preserving ancient texts while making them understandable to people from vastly different cultures, traditions, and linguistic backgrounds throughout the world.

Did you know?

The Bible has been translated more extensively than any other book, but counts above 3,000 usually include languages with only portions or individual biblical texts rather than a complete Bible.
