Why Z Is the Least Used Letter xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The letter Z is the least frequently used letter in written English. It appears slightly more often in American English than in British English.

The letter Z is generally considered the least frequently used letter in written English. It appears only rarely in everyday vocabulary compared with letters such as E, T, or A, making it a favorite topic in linguistic studies and word frequency analysis. Interestingly, Z occurs slightly more often in American English than in British English because of spelling differences in certain words, such as "analyze" versus "analyse." Despite its rarity, the letter plays an important role in science, mathematics, technology, and proper names. Its unusual status has also made it popular in games like Scrabble, where it carries one of the highest point values because of its limited frequency.

Did you know?

Z is usually the least frequent letter in ordinary English text, although exact rankings vary by corpus, spelling conventions, and whether proper names are included.
