Russia Has the Largest Forest Area in the World xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Russia contains the world's largest forest area, covering hundreds of millions of hectares across Eurasia.

Russia has the largest forest area of any country in the world, covering approximately 815 million hectares across Europe and Asia. Much of this vast woodland belongs to the boreal forest, also known as the taiga, which forms the largest continuous forest ecosystem on Earth. These forests store enormous amounts of carbon, provide habitats for countless species, and play a vital role in regulating the global climate. Protecting them is increasingly important as climate change, wildfires, and deforestation place growing pressure on one of the planet's greatest natural resources.

Did you know?

Russia contains more than 800 million hectares of forest, much of it forming the vast boreal taiga, which stores enormous amounts of carbon and provides habitat for species adapted to cold northern climates.
