King Charles III Doesn't Need a Passport xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] King Charles III does not need a passport because British passports are issued in the monarch's name.

King Charles III does not need a passport when traveling internationally because British passports are issued in the monarch's name. Since the sovereign cannot formally issue a passport to himself, the requirement does not apply. Other members of the Royal Family still travel using ordinary passports like other British citizens. This unique constitutional arrangement reflects the monarch's role as the source of official state authority within the United Kingdom and has existed for generations.

Did you know?

British passports are issued under royal authority, so the reigning monarch does not need one, while other members of the Royal Family still travel with passports.
