Every Dolphin Has a Unique Signature Whistle xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Every dolphin develops a unique signature whistle that functions much like a personal name among pod members.

Every dolphin develops a unique signature whistle that functions much like a personal name. Young dolphins create these distinctive whistles early in life and continue using them throughout adulthood. Other dolphins can recognize and even imitate these whistles to call specific individuals, demonstrating an impressive level of social communication. This ability highlights the intelligence of dolphins and the strong social bonds they maintain within their pods.

Did you know?

Bottlenose dolphins develop individually distinctive signature whistles, and other dolphins can imitate these whistles when addressing specific individuals.
