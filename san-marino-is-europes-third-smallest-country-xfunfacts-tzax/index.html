San Marino Is Europe's Third-Smallest Country xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Tiny San Marino is one of the world's oldest republics and ranks as the third-smallest country in Europe despite its remarkable history.

Despite covering only about sixty-one square kilometers, San Marino has played an outsized role in European history. Nestled entirely within Italy, it ranks as the fifth-smallest country in the world and the third-smallest in Europe. According to tradition, the republic was founded in AD 301 by Saint Marinus, making it one of the world's oldest surviving republics. Throughout centuries of political upheaval, wars, and changing borders across Europe, San Marino managed to preserve its independence and democratic institutions. Today, the country is known for its medieval architecture, scenic mountain views, and historic towers overlooking the surrounding countryside. Tourism plays an important role in its economy, attracting visitors interested in its unique history and cultural heritage. Although tiny in size, San Marino demonstrates that a nation can maintain a rich political identity and remarkable historical continuity over more than seventeen centuries.

Did you know?

San Marino covers approximately 61 square kilometers and traces its traditional founding to AD 301, although its early history is partly legendary and its modern institutions developed over centuries.
