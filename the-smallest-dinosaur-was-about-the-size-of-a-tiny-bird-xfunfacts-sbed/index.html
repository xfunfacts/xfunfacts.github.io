The Smallest Dinosaur Was About the Size of a Tiny Bird xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Oculudentavis was an incredibly tiny prehistoric reptile about the size of a bee hummingbird.

Oculudentavis was an extremely small prehistoric animal initially described as a tiny dinosaur about the size of a bee hummingbird. Although later research suggested it was more likely a lizard than a true dinosaur, the fossil remains one of the smallest known reptilian specimens from the age of dinosaurs. Preserved in amber from Myanmar, the remarkably detailed skull provided scientists with valuable information about ancient ecosystems. The discovery also demonstrated how new evidence can change scientific understanding as researchers continue studying prehistoric life.

Did you know?

Oculudentavis was initially described as a tiny bird-like dinosaur in 2020, but subsequent research identified it as a lizard; living hummingbirds are among the smallest known dinosaurs because birds belong to Dinosauria.
