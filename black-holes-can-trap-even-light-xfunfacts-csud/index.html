Black Holes Can Trap Even Light xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Black holes have gravity so powerful that even light cannot escape once it crosses the event horizon, making them among the universe's most extreme objects.

A black hole is a region of space where gravity becomes so powerful that nothing, not even light, can escape after crossing the event horizon. Most black holes form when massive stars collapse at the end of their lives, while the largest supermassive black holes occupy the centers of galaxies. Although black holes themselves cannot be seen directly, astronomers detect them by observing their effects on nearby stars, gas, and light. Modern telescopes have even captured images of material surrounding black holes. These extraordinary objects continue to challenge our understanding of space, gravity, and the laws of physics.

Did you know?

The boundary around a black hole from which nothing can escape is called the event horizon, while the singularity lies deeper inside according to general relativity.
