Baby Ruth Was Named After Ruth Cleveland xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many historians believe the Baby Ruth candy bar was named after Ruth Cleveland rather than baseball legend Babe Ruth.

The Baby Ruth candy bar has often been associated with baseball legend Babe Ruth, but many historians believe it was actually named after Ruth Cleveland, the daughter of President Grover Cleveland. The candy was introduced years after Ruth Cleveland had become a well-known public figure, although the manufacturer denied any connection to the famous baseball player. The true inspiration remains a topic of debate and has contributed to the candy bar's enduring history.

Did you know?

Curtiss Candy claimed Baby Ruth honored President Grover Cleveland's daughter, but the bar appeared in 1921 when baseball star Babe Ruth was enormously popular.
