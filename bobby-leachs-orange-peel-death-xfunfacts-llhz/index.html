Bobby Leach's Orange Peel Death xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Bobby Leach survived going over Niagara Falls in a barrel but later died after slipping on an orange peel. His bizarre fate remains one of history's strangest true stories.

Bobby Leach achieved worldwide fame in 1911 by becoming the second person to survive going over Niagara Falls inside a specially designed barrel. The daring stunt left him with numerous injuries, including broken bones, but he recovered and spent years giving public lectures about his remarkable achievement. Ironically, the event that ultimately ended his life was far less dramatic. In 1926, Leach slipped on an orange peel, seriously injuring his leg. The injury developed complications, eventually leading to gangrene and the amputation of his leg. His health continued to decline, and he later died from those complications. Although the orange peel itself did not directly cause his death, the chain of medical events following the accident proved fatal. The story has become one of history's most famous examples of surviving an extraordinary danger only to be defeated by an everyday accident, illustrating how unpredictable life can be.

Did you know?

Bobby Leach survived his 1911 Niagara Falls barrel plunge, but accounts differ on whether an orange peel specifically caused the injury that eventually led to his death.
