New Zealand Has More Sheep Than People xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] New Zealand is famous for having far more sheep than people, making sheep farming a defining part of its landscape.

New Zealand is well known for having far more sheep than people, although the exact ratio has changed over the years. Sheep farming has been a major part of the country's economy and rural culture for generations, thanks to its mild climate, abundant grasslands, and ideal grazing conditions. While the human population has steadily grown and sheep numbers have gradually declined from historic highs, sheep still greatly outnumber residents across much of the country. The image of rolling green hills filled with grazing sheep has become one of New Zealand's most recognizable symbols around the world.

Did you know?

New Zealand's sheep population has declined dramatically from its 1980s peak of more than 70 million, but sheep still outnumber people, reflecting the continuing importance of pastoral agriculture.
