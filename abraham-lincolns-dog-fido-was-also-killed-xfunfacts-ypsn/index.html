Abraham Lincoln's Dog Fido Was Also Killed xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Abraham Lincoln's loyal dog Fido was killed about a year after the president's assassination, ending a remarkable story.

Abraham Lincoln's beloved dog, Fido, became famous for faithfully accompanying him around Springfield, Illinois. Before leaving for Washington, Lincoln entrusted Fido to a local family because he believed the dog would not adapt well to city life. About a year after Lincoln's assassination, Fido was reportedly killed after being stabbed by an intoxicated man. The story remains one of the most well-known accounts involving a presidential pet and reflects the strong bond Lincoln shared with animals.

Did you know?

Fido did not move to Washington with Lincoln because the family feared the busy capital would distress him; he remained with another family in Springfield, Illinois.
