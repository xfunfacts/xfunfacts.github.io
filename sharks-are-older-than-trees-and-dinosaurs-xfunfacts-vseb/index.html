Sharks Are Older Than Trees and Dinosaurs xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Sharks first appeared about 450 million years ago, long before trees and dinosaurs evolved.

Sharks first appeared in Earth's oceans around 450 million years ago, making them far older than both trees and dinosaurs. Early sharks were already swimming through ancient seas nearly 100 million years before the first trees evolved and over 200 million years before the earliest dinosaurs appeared. During this immense span of time, sharks survived multiple mass extinction events that wiped out countless other species. Their long evolutionary history has allowed them to develop highly specialized senses, efficient bodies, and remarkable adaptability, making them one of the most successful groups of predators ever to inhabit the planet.

Did you know?

Early shark relatives existed hundreds of millions of years before the first dinosaurs, but modern shark species evolved much later; their cartilaginous skeletons make teeth especially important in reconstructing their evolutionary history.
