Facebook Can Make Friends Seem Happier xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Facebook can make other people's lives appear happier than they really are. This effect has been linked to increased loneliness and lower life satisfaction.

Social media allows people to share highlights of their lives, but this can unintentionally distort how others perceive reality. Research has shown that frequent Facebook users may overestimate how happy their friends are because people tend to post positive experiences more often than ordinary or negative moments. This imbalance can contribute to feelings of loneliness, envy, or lower life satisfaction as users compare their everyday lives with carefully selected online content. Psychologists describe this as a form of social comparison, where unrealistic perceptions influence emotional well-being. Understanding this phenomenon encourages healthier social media habits and reminds users that online profiles rarely reflect the complete picture of someone's life.

Did you know?

Social media can encourage upward social comparison because users selectively present positive experiences, but its effects on loneliness and well-being vary greatly between people and patterns of use.
