Owls Can Rotate Their Heads 270 Degrees xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Owls can rotate their heads up to 270 degrees thanks to specialized neck anatomy that expands their field of vision.

Owls can rotate their heads up to 270 degrees, giving them an impressive range of vision without moving their bodies. Their eyes are fixed in their sockets, so turning the head is the only way to change their field of view. Specialized blood vessels and flexible neck bones allow this extraordinary movement without cutting off circulation to the brain. This ability helps owls monitor their surroundings while remaining hidden from prey. It is one of the adaptations that make owls highly effective nighttime hunters.

Did you know?

Owls have 14 neck vertebrae, twice as many as humans, helping them turn their heads far around without damaging major blood vessels.
