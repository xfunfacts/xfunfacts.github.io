Dolphins Sleep with One Eye Open xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dolphins sleep with only half of their brain at a time, allowing one eye to remain open for awareness.

Dolphins cannot fall into a deep sleep like humans because they must regularly surface to breathe. Instead, they use a remarkable adaptation called unihemispheric sleep, in which one half of the brain rests while the other remains awake. During this time, one eye usually stays open to watch for predators and maintain awareness of the surroundings. After a period of rest, the two brain hemispheres switch roles. This unique sleeping pattern allows dolphins to remain alert while still getting the rest they need.

Did you know?

Dolphins use unihemispheric slow-wave sleep, resting one brain hemisphere at a time so they can continue surfacing to breathe and remain alert to their surroundings.
