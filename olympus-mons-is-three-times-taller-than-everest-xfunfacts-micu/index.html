Olympus Mons Is Three Times Taller Than Everest xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Olympus Mons on Mars is so enormous that Mount Everest would look tiny by comparison, standing only about one-third as tall.

Olympus Mons is the largest known volcano and tallest mountain in the entire solar system. Located on Mars, this enormous shield volcano rises approximately three times higher than Mount Everest when measured from its surrounding plains. Unlike Earth's volcanoes, which are affected by moving tectonic plates, Mars lacks active plate tectonics, allowing volcanic eruptions to occur repeatedly in the same location for millions of years. As layer upon layer of lava accumulated, Olympus Mons gradually reached its astonishing size. The volcano stretches hundreds of kilometers across and features a massive summit caldera formed by repeated volcanic collapse. Despite its immense scale, its slopes are relatively gentle because they were built from countless slow-moving lava flows. Scientists continue studying Olympus Mons to better understand the geological history of Mars and the processes that shaped the planet billions of years ago. Its extraordinary dimensions make it one of the most remarkable natural landforms ever discovered beyond Earth.

Did you know?

Olympus Mons is a shield volcano about 22 kilometers high, and its enormous size was possible partly because Mars lacks active plate tectonics.
