Japan Is Made Up of Thousands of Islands xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Japan consists of four main islands and thousands of smaller islands, creating one of the world's most distinctive island nations.

Japan is an island nation made up of four main islands—Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku—along with more than 14,000 smaller islands. Surrounded by the Pacific Ocean and the Sea of Japan, the country's geography has strongly influenced its history, culture, and economy. Many of Japan's largest cities are located along the coast, where shipping, fishing, and international trade have flourished for centuries. The islands also feature remarkable natural diversity, ranging from snowy mountains in the north to subtropical beaches in the south. Living on an island nation has shaped Japan into one of the world's leading maritime countries.

Did you know?

Japan's Geospatial Information Authority revised the country's official island count from 6,852 to 14,125 in 2023 using digital mapping, with the increase reflecting improved measurement rather than newly formed islands.
