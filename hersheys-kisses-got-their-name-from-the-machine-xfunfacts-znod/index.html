Hershey's Kisses Got Their Name from the Machine xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hershey's Kisses reportedly received their name because the production machine appeared to kiss the conveyor belt.

Hershey's Kisses reportedly received their name because the chocolate-dispensing machine appeared to "kiss" the conveyor belt as each piece was deposited. The bite-sized chocolates were first introduced in 1907 and quickly became one of America's best-known candy products. Today, millions of Hershey's Kisses are produced every day in a wide variety of flavors, colors, and seasonal packaging while maintaining their iconic teardrop shape.

Did you know?

Hershey's Kisses were introduced in 1907, and one popular explanation says their name came from the machine's kissing motion, although the exact origin of the trademark remains uncertain.
