Some Eagles Can Carry Young Deer xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Large eagle species are powerful enough to attack and briefly lift young deer under the right conditions.

Large eagle species, including the golden eagle, are capable of attacking prey as large as young deer under the right conditions. Although fully carrying such prey for long distances is uncommon, these powerful birds can briefly lift or drag surprisingly heavy animals using their strong talons and wings. Eagles usually target vulnerable or injured individuals rather than healthy adults. Their hunting abilities place them among the world's most formidable birds of prey.

Did you know?

Large eagles usually target prey they can subdue safely, and most cannot carry animals close to their own body weight over long distances.
