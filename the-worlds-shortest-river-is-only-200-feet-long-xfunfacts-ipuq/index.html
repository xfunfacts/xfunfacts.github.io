The World's Shortest River Is Only 200 Feet Long xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Montana's Roe River was once recognized as the world's shortest river, measuring only about 200 feet from source to mouth.

Montana's Roe River was once recognized as the world's shortest river, measuring only about 200 feet, or 61 meters, from its source to where it joined the Missouri River. Although later geographic classifications sparked debate about the official title, the river remains famous for its extraordinary brevity. Despite its tiny size, Roe River attracted visitors from around the world who wanted to see one of nature's most unusual waterways. It demonstrates that rivers come in an incredible range of lengths, from just a few dozen meters to thousands of kilometers.

Did you know?

Montana's Roe River measures approximately 201 feet and connects Giant Springs to the Missouri River, but Guinness discontinued its shortest-river category amid disputes over measurement methods.
