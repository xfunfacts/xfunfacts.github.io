Happy Birthday Was the First Song Played on Mars xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] NASA's Curiosity rover marked its first year on Mars by symbolically playing "Happy Birthday" to itself.

NASA's Curiosity rover celebrated its first anniversary on Mars by playing "Happy Birthday" to itself using vibrations from its onboard instruments rather than audible sound. Because Mars has a thin atmosphere, the tune could not be heard in the traditional way, but the symbolic performance marked an important milestone in the mission. Curiosity has continued exploring the Red Planet for years beyond its original mission, helping scientists better understand Mars' geology, climate, and potential to have supported ancient microbial life.

Did you know?

Curiosity's rover team used its Sample Analysis at Mars instrument to vibrate at specific frequencies, creating the melody without using a conventional speaker.
