Bluetooth Was Named After a Viking King xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Bluetooth takes its name from Viking king Harald Bluetooth, who united parts of Scandinavia.

Bluetooth technology is named after Harald "Bluetooth" Gormsson, a tenth-century Danish king who united parts of Denmark and Norway. The developers chose his nickname because they hoped their wireless standard would unite different electronic devices in a similar way. The familiar Bluetooth logo is also based on the combined Nordic runes representing Harald's initials. This clever historical reference has become one of the most recognizable technology brand names in the world.

Did you know?

Bluetooth's name honors Harald "Bluetooth" Gormsson, while its famous logo combines runic symbols representing his initials H and B.
