Hooker Is a Real Town With a Legendary Slogan xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hooker, Oklahoma embraces its unusual name with a slogan that has made the small town famous across the United States.

Hidden in the Oklahoma Panhandle is a small town with one of America's most unforgettable names. Hooker, Oklahoma, has embraced its unusual name with a famous slogan: "It's a location, not a vocation." Rather than avoiding the inevitable jokes, local residents turned the phrase into a humorous marketing tool that attracts curious travelers from around the country. The town's name actually comes from ranch owner John "Hooker" Threlkeld, whose nickname had nothing to do with its modern meaning. Over time, the playful slogan became widely known and now appears on souvenirs, signs, and promotional materials celebrating the community's unique identity. Visitors often stop simply to photograph the welcome sign before continuing their journey across the Panhandle. Hooker demonstrates how a small town successfully transformed an unusual historical name into a source of pride, tourism, and lasting popularity.

Did you know?

Hooker, Oklahoma, was named after cattleman John “Hooker” Threlkeld, not the modern slang term; the town embraced the coincidence with its slogan, “It's a location, not a vocation.”
