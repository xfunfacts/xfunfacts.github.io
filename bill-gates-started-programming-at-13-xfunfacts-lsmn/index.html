Bill Gates Started Programming at 13 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Bill Gates began programming at age 13, developing skills that later helped shape the personal computer revolution.

Bill Gates began learning computer programming at the age of 13 after gaining access to a computer terminal at Lakeside School in Seattle. At a time when computers were still rare and expensive, he spent countless hours writing code, experimenting with software, and solving programming challenges. His passion eventually led him to co-found Microsoft with childhood friend Paul Allen in 1975. Their work helped make personal computers accessible to millions of people around the world. Gates's early interest in programming demonstrates how curiosity, persistence, and access to learning opportunities can shape groundbreaking careers.

Did you know?

Gates learned programming on a Teletype terminal at Lakeside School, where unusually early access to computers helped shape his future career.
