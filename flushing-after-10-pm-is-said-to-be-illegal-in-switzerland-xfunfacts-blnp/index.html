Flushing After 10 PM Is Said to Be Illegal in Switzerland xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] You've probably heard it's illegal to flush a toilet after 10 PM in Switzerland, but the truth behind this claim is more complicated.

A long-running claim suggests that flushing a toilet after 10 PM is illegal in Switzerland because of strict noise regulations. In reality, there is no nationwide Swiss law that prohibits nighttime flushing. The misconception likely comes from apartment buildings where landlords or housing associations may establish quiet-hour guidelines intended to reduce unnecessary noise between neighbors. While residents are generally expected to respect nighttime quiet periods, flushing a toilet is considered a normal and necessary household activity rather than a punishable offense. Legal experts and Swiss authorities have repeatedly clarified that ordinary sanitation is not prohibited by national law. The story remains one of the internet's most persistent myths about Switzerland despite lacking a legal basis in most situations.

Did you know?

Switzerland has no nationwide law prohibiting toilet flushing after 10 PM; the popular story confuses general residential noise rules with a nonexistent specific ban.
