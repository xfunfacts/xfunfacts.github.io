Green Tea Has More Vitamin C Than Black Tea xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Green tea usually contains more vitamin C than black tea because it undergoes less processing after harvest.

Green tea generally contains more vitamin C than black tea because it undergoes much less processing after harvesting. Fresh tea leaves naturally contain vitamin C, but oxidation during the production of black tea reduces the amount that remains in the finished product. Green tea also contains high levels of catechins, a group of antioxidants that have been widely studied for their potential health benefits. For centuries, green tea has been consumed throughout East Asia as both a daily beverage and part of traditional cultural practices. While brewing temperature and storage conditions influence nutrient levels, green tea remains one of the world's most popular drinks for people seeking a refreshing beverage with naturally occurring beneficial plant compounds.

Did you know?

Green tea leaves may retain more vitamin C than fully oxidized black tea leaves, but the amount reaching a brewed cup varies greatly and is generally too small to make tea a dependable vitamin C source.
