Koalas Have Two Opposable Thumbs xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Koalas have two opposable thumbs on each hand, giving them an excellent grip for climbing trees.

Koalas have two opposable thumbs on each front paw, giving them an exceptionally strong grip while climbing trees. These specialized digits work against the other fingers, allowing koalas to grasp branches securely as they move among eucalyptus trees. Their rough paw pads and sharp claws provide even greater stability on bark. Combined with powerful limbs, these adaptations make koalas highly skilled climbers despite their stocky bodies. Tree climbing is central to every aspect of a koala's daily life.

Did you know?

Koalas also have two fused toes on each hind foot that form a grooming comb used to clean and maintain their dense fur.
