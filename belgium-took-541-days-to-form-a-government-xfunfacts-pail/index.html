Belgium Took 541 Days to Form a Government xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Belgium holds the world record for the longest government formation, taking 541 days to establish an official government. The political deadlock became international news.

Belgium holds the Guinness World Record for the longest period spent forming a national government after an election. Between 2010 and 2011, the country went 541 days before political parties successfully negotiated a governing coalition. The unusually long process was largely the result of Belgium's complex political system, which balances the interests of Dutch-speaking Flanders, French-speaking Wallonia, and smaller linguistic communities. Despite the record-breaking delay, the country continued operating under a caretaker government that handled day-to-day administration, preventing major disruptions to public services. The event demonstrated both the challenges and resilience of coalition politics in parliamentary democracies. Belgium's experience remains one of the most remarkable examples of prolonged government negotiations while maintaining economic stability and essential government functions.

Did you know?

Belgium's political parties spent 541 days negotiating a coalition after the June 2010 election, while a caretaker administration continued operating until Elio Di Rupo's government took office in December 2011.
