Sloths Digest Food Very Slowly xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Sloths digest a single meal over 11 to 30 days, giving them one of the slowest digestive systems among mammals.

Sloths have one of the slowest digestive systems of any mammal, often taking between 11 and 30 days to digest a single meal. Their extremely low metabolism allows them to survive on nutrient-poor leaves while conserving energy. Because digestion happens so slowly, sloths spend much of their lives resting in trees. This remarkable adaptation helps them thrive in tropical forests despite having a limited and low-energy diet.

Did you know?

Sloths descend from trees only rarely, and they typically defecate about once a week, a risky trip that exposes them to predators on the ground.
