Most Toilet Paper in France Is Pink xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Pink toilet paper has long been a common household product in France instead of traditional white rolls.

Visitors are often surprised to find that much of the toilet paper sold in France is pink rather than white. The tradition dates back decades and became popular simply because manufacturers and consumers preferred the color, not because of any practical advantage. Although white toilet paper is now increasingly common, pink remains widely available in many French stores. It is one of those everyday cultural differences that often catches travelers by surprise.

Did you know?

Pink toilet paper has historically been associated with French household preferences, but claims that most toilet paper sold in France is pink lack reliable contemporary retail statistics.
