Lions Are Highly Social Animals xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Lions spend much of their lives grooming, resting, and caring for one another, making them the most social of all big cats.

Lions are the most social of all big cats, spending much of their lives in close-knit family groups called prides. Members often rest together, groom one another, play, and cooperate in raising cubs and defending territory. Grooming helps strengthen social relationships while also removing parasites and keeping fur clean. Living in groups provides important advantages, including better protection against rivals, improved hunting success, and shared care of young, making lion society remarkably cooperative compared with other large feline species.

Did you know?

Lion prides are usually built around related females that remain together for years, while young males often leave and form coalitions elsewhere.
