Seahorses Often Mate for Life xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many seahorse pairs form long-term bonds, and males carry the developing young in a specialized brood pouch.

Many seahorse species form long-term pair bonds and may stay with the same partner throughout an entire breeding season or even for life. Pairs perform daily greeting dances that strengthen their bond before mating, often swimming side by side while holding tails. Seahorses are also famous because males, rather than females, carry the developing babies in a specialized brood pouch until birth. Their unusual reproductive behavior makes them one of the most distinctive fish in the ocean.

Did you know?

Male seahorses give birth by contracting their brood pouch, releasing fully formed young into the water after carrying the embryos through development.
