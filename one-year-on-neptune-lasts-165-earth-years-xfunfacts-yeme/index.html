One Year on Neptune Lasts 165 Earth Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] One year on Neptune lasts 165 Earth years. Since its discovery, the distant planet has completed only a single trip around the Sun.

A single year on Neptune lasts about 165 Earth years because the distant planet takes that long to complete one orbit around the Sun. Since Neptune was discovered in 1846, it has completed only one full orbit, returning to the same position in its journey around the Sun in 2011. Located nearly 4.5 billion kilometers, or 2.8 billion miles, from the Sun, Neptune receives only a small fraction of the sunlight that reaches Earth. Despite its great distance, the planet experiences powerful storms and the fastest winds in the Solar System, with speeds exceeding 2,000 kilometers per hour. Studying Neptune helps scientists understand how giant planets behave under extremely cold conditions and offers valuable clues about similar worlds orbiting distant stars.

Did you know?

Neptune travels about 4.5 billion kilometers from the Sun on average, so sunlight reaching the planet has taken more than four hours to get there.
