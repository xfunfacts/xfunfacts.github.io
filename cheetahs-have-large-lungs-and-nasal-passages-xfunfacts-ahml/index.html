Cheetahs Have Large Lungs and Nasal Passages xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Large lungs and wide nasal passages help cheetahs take in more oxygen during high-speed chases across open grasslands.

Cheetahs have exceptionally large lungs and wide nasal passages that allow them to take in massive amounts of oxygen during high-speed pursuits. Their enlarged respiratory system works together with a powerful heart to deliver oxygen-rich blood to muscles that are working at maximum capacity. After an intense chase, a cheetah breathes rapidly to recover from the tremendous physical effort. These specialized anatomical features support the incredible athletic performance that makes the cheetah the fastest runner on land.

Did you know?

Cheetahs have relatively large hearts as well as enlarged respiratory passages, supporting the intense oxygen demands created during short high-speed pursuits.
