Central Park Is Bigger Than Monaco xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] New York City's Central Park covers more land than the entire country of Monaco despite sitting in the middle of Manhattan.

Central Park covers about 843 acres, making it larger than the entire Principality of Monaco, which occupies roughly 500 acres less land. Despite being surrounded by one of the world's busiest cities, the park contains forests, lakes, meadows, sports facilities, and miles of walking paths. Designed by Frederick Law Olmsted and Calvert Vaux, Central Park has become one of the world's most famous urban green spaces. It provides millions of visitors each year with recreation, wildlife habitats, and a welcome escape from city life.

Did you know?

Central Park covers approximately 843 acres, or 3.41 square kilometers, exceeding Monaco's land area of roughly 2.1 square kilometers despite being only one park within Manhattan.
