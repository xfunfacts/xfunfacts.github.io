Some Places in Antarctica Haven't Seen Snow for Millions of Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Parts of Antarctica have remained so dry that they have seen little or no snowfall for more than two million years.

Some regions of Antarctica are so dry that they have not received significant snowfall for more than two million years. These areas, known as the McMurdo Dry Valleys, experience extremely low humidity and powerful winds that remove snow before it can accumulate. The barren landscape resembles parts of Mars, making it an important location for testing equipment and studying environments similar to those on other planets. Microbial life survives in these extreme conditions, helping scientists understand how organisms adapt to severe climates. The Dry Valleys are among the most unusual places on Earth.

Did you know?

Antarctica's McMurdo Dry Valleys experience extremely little snowfall because surrounding mountains and powerful dry winds limit moisture, leaving some surfaces ice-free for extraordinarily long periods.
