Most Jeep Models Hide Secret Easter Eggs xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many Jeep vehicles hide playful Easter eggs, including tiny geckos, spiders, flip-flops, and other secret design details.

Many Jeep vehicles include small hidden decorative features known as Easter eggs. These playful designs may take the form of tiny geckos, spiders, flip-flops, historic Jeep silhouettes, or other symbols tucked into unexpected places throughout the vehicle. Engineers and designers include them as a fun surprise for owners to discover over time. The tradition has become a popular part of Jeep culture, encouraging enthusiasts to search for new hidden details whenever a model is released.

Did you know?

Jeep designers began hiding small motifs in vehicles as playful design details, but the type and number of Easter eggs vary greatly between models and model years.
