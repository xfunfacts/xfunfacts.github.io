Your Liver Can Regrow Itself xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The liver can regenerate much of its tissue, making it one of the few human organs with this ability.

The liver is one of the few human organs capable of remarkable regeneration. Even if as much as two-thirds of the liver is removed during surgery, the remaining healthy tissue can grow back to restore much of its original size and function. This process happens because liver cells divide rapidly when needed while carefully regulating growth to avoid unnecessary tissue formation. Although the liver can regenerate, repeated damage from heavy alcohol use, viral hepatitis, or chronic disease can eventually overwhelm this ability. Its unique healing power continues to inspire medical research into tissue regeneration and organ repair.

Did you know?

The liver regenerates mainly by existing mature cells dividing to restore lost tissue, rather than by simply regrowing an entirely new organ from a small fragment.
