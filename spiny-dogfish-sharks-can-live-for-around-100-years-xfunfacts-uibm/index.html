Spiny Dogfish Sharks Can Live for Around 100 Years xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Spiny dogfish sharks can live for around 100 years, making them among the longest-lived shark species ever studied.

Spiny dogfish are among the longest-lived sharks, with some individuals approaching or even exceeding 100 years of age. These slow-growing sharks mature much later than many other fish, and females require nearly two years to complete a pregnancy, making it one of the longest known gestation periods among vertebrates. Their slow reproduction means populations recover very gradually after overfishing, making conservation particularly important. Researchers continue studying spiny dogfish to better understand aging, marine ecosystems, and sustainable fisheries management.

Did you know?

Spiny dogfish mature very slowly and produce relatively few young, making their populations especially vulnerable to heavy fishing pressure.
