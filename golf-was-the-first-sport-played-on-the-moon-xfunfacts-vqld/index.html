Golf Was the First Sport Played on the Moon xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Alan Shepard made history by hitting golf balls on the Moon during the Apollo 14 mission in 1971.

During the Apollo 14 mission in 1971, astronaut Alan Shepard used a makeshift golf club attached to a lunar sample tool to hit two golf balls across the Moon's surface. The shots became the first golf strokes ever played beyond Earth. Because the Moon has only about one-sixth of Earth's gravity and almost no atmosphere, the balls traveled much farther than they would under similar conditions on Earth. The memorable moment added a touch of humor to an otherwise serious scientific mission and remains one of the most iconic events in space exploration history.

Did you know?

Alan Shepard used a modified six-iron head attached to a lunar sample-collection handle to hit golf balls during Apollo 14.
