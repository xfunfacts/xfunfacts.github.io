The First Metered Taxi Entered Service in 1907 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The first metered taxi entered service in London in 1907, making fares more accurate and transparent.

The first taxicabs equipped with taximeters entered service in London in 1907, changing urban transportation by allowing fares to be calculated based on distance traveled. Before taximeters became common, passengers often had to negotiate prices directly with drivers, which could lead to disputes. The introduction of mechanical meters brought greater fairness, transparency, and consistency to public transportation. Over time, taximeters became standard equipment in taxis around the world and later evolved into electronic systems linked with GPS technology. Today's ride-hailing services continue this tradition by automatically calculating fares using digital mapping and distance measurements.

Did you know?

Taximeters automatically calculated fares from distance and time, helping standardize taxi pricing as motor cabs replaced horse-drawn vehicles.
