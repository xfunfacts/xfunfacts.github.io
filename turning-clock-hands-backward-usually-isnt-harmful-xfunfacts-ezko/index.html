Turning Clock Hands Backward Usually Isn't Harmful xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Turning clock hands backward is usually safe on modern clocks, although some older mechanical chiming models can be affected.

Many people worry that turning a clock's hands backward will permanently damage the mechanism, but this is generally not true for most modern clocks. Quartz clocks and many contemporary mechanical designs can safely be adjusted in either direction without causing problems. However, some older mechanical clocks with chiming or striking mechanisms may become misaligned if their hands are repeatedly moved backward. For those clocks, manufacturers often recommend advancing the hands forward or following specific adjustment instructions. Understanding the type of clock you own helps ensure it is set correctly while avoiding unnecessary wear. This common household myth illustrates how advice that was once valid for older technology does not always apply to modern designs.

Did you know?
