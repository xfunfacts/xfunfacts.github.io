A Day on Venus Is Longer Than Its Year xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A single day on Venus lasts longer than its year because the planet rotates extremely slowly.

One of the strangest facts about Venus is that a single day lasts longer than an entire year. The planet rotates so slowly that it takes about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation, while it needs only about 225 Earth days to travel once around the Sun. Venus also rotates in the opposite direction of most planets, meaning the Sun would appear to rise in the west and set in the east if someone could stand on its surface. This unusual motion continues to puzzle astronomers, who are still investigating why Venus spins so slowly and in reverse. Its unique rotation makes Venus unlike any other major planet in the solar system.

Did you know?

Venus also rotates backward compared with most planets, so the Sun would appear to rise in the west and set in the east from its surface.
