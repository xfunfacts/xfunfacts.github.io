Earth Is About 4.5 Billion Years Old xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago, with its age determined through radiometric dating of ancient rocks and meteorites.

Scientists estimate that Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago by studying ancient rocks and meteorites using a technique called radiometric dating. This method measures the natural decay of radioactive elements to determine the age of minerals with remarkable precision. During its long history, Earth has experienced volcanic activity, shifting continents, ice ages, and multiple mass extinctions. These events shaped the planet into the world we know today. Earth's age provides important clues about the evolution of life and the solar system.

Did you know?

The oldest known minerals on Earth are tiny zircon crystals more than 4.3 billion years old, providing clues about conditions on the young planet.
