There's a Lake in Africa That Can Explode xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Lake Nyos in Cameroon once released a deadly cloud of carbon dioxide in one of history's rarest natural disasters.

Lake Nyos in Cameroon became internationally known after a rare limnic eruption in 1986 released a massive cloud of carbon dioxide from deep beneath the lake. Because carbon dioxide is heavier than air, it flowed across nearby valleys, displacing oxygen and causing the deaths of more than 1,700 people and thousands of animals. Scientists have since installed degassing pipes that gradually release trapped gas, greatly reducing the risk of another catastrophic eruption. The event remains one of the rarest natural disasters ever recorded.

Did you know?

Lake Nyos released a dense cloud of carbon dioxide in 1986 after gas-rich deep water suddenly overturned, killing people and animals by displacing breathable air near the ground.
