Three U.S. Presidents Died on July 4 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Three U.S. presidents died on July 4, creating one of the most remarkable coincidences in American history.

Three U.S. presidents share an unusual historical connection with Independence Day. John Adams and Thomas Jefferson both died on July 4, 1826, exactly fifty years after the Declaration of Independence was adopted. Five years later, James Monroe also died on July 4, 1831. The coincidence has fascinated historians for generations because all three men played major roles in the founding and early leadership of the United States.

Did you know?
