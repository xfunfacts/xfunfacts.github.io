Australia Is Surrounded by Two Oceans xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Australia is bordered by the Indian and Pacific Oceans, giving it an extensive coastline and rich marine ecosystems.

Australia is surrounded by both the Indian Ocean and the Pacific Ocean, giving the country more than 21,000 miles of coastline when its islands are included. This extensive coastline supports world-famous beaches, coral reefs, mangrove forests, and diverse marine ecosystems. Coastal environments also influence Australia's climate, economy, and recreational lifestyle. Fishing, tourism, shipping, and marine conservation all play important roles in the nation's development. Australia's oceans are central to both its natural heritage and modern economy.

Did you know?

Australia lies between the Indian and Pacific ocean regions, although modern geographic authorities also recognize surrounding marginal seas such as the Coral and Tasman Seas.
