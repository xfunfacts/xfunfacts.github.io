Blue Lobsters Are Extremely Rare xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Blue lobsters are incredibly rare, appearing in about one out of every two million because of a genetic mutation.

Blue lobsters are incredibly rare, with scientists estimating that only about one in two million has their striking bright blue shell. The unusual color is caused by a genetic mutation that makes the lobster produce an excess of a specific protein, altering how light reflects from its shell. Despite their dramatic appearance, blue lobsters are otherwise normal and can survive just as well as their more common brown relatives. When caught by fishermen, many are donated to aquariums instead of being sold for food because of their exceptional rarity.

Did you know?

Blue coloration in lobsters usually comes from an unusual interaction between pigments and proteins in the shell rather than from blue blood.
