It Takes About 364 Licks to Finish a Tootsie Pop xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Scientists have repeatedly tested how many licks it takes to finish a Tootsie Pop. One well-known experiment estimated an average of 364 licks.

The famous question "How many licks does it take to reach the center of a Tootsie Pop?" has fascinated people for decades. While the answer varies depending on the person, several scientific experiments have attempted to solve the mystery. Purdue University engineering students designed a licking machine that estimated around 364 licks would reach the center, while other studies produced results ranging from about 144 to more than 400 licks. Differences in tongue pressure, saliva production, licking speed, and candy size all influence the outcome. Even the Tootsie Roll company has never claimed a definitive answer, choosing instead to embrace the question as part of the candy's identity. The famous advertising campaign featuring Mr. Owl helped turn the mystery into one of the best-known marketing slogans in American history. Rather than finding one correct number, the experiment demonstrates how simple everyday questions can inspire scientific curiosity and become part of popular culture for generations.

Did you know?

Experiments estimating how many licks it takes to reach a Tootsie Pop's center have produced widely different results, because tongue size, licking technique, candy dimensions, and moisture affect the outcome.
