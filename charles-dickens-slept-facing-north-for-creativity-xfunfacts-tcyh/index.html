Charles Dickens Slept Facing North for Creativity xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Charles Dickens was so convinced that sleeping north improved creativity that he reportedly repositioned every bed he used.

Charles Dickens, one of the greatest novelists in English literature, held an unusual personal belief about sleep and creativity. According to accounts from friends and family, Dickens insisted on positioning every bed so that it faced north before going to sleep. He believed this orientation improved his creativity, sharpened his mental clarity, and contributed to better writing. Whenever he traveled, he reportedly checked the direction of the bed and rearranged it if necessary. Although there is no scientific evidence supporting a connection between sleeping direction and creative ability, the habit became one of the many fascinating eccentricities associated with the celebrated author. Dickens was known for maintaining disciplined daily routines, taking long walks, and paying close attention to his personal environment while working on his novels. His unusual sleeping preference continues to intrigue historians and readers, illustrating how even some of history's greatest creative minds embraced highly personal rituals they believed enhanced their productivity.

Did you know?

Dickens was deeply interested in mesmerism and other Victorian ideas about health and magnetism, which may help explain reports about his preferred sleeping direction.
