Pangolins Are the Only Mammals Covered in Scales xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Pangolins are the world's only mammals covered entirely in protective keratin scales.

Pangolins are the only mammals completely covered with large overlapping scales made of keratin, the same protein found in human fingernails and hair. When threatened, they curl into a tight ball, using their tough scales as armor against predators. Despite this impressive defense, pangolins are among the world's most heavily trafficked mammals because of illegal wildlife trade. Conservation organizations continue working to protect these unique animals and their rapidly declining populations.

Did you know?

Pangolin scales are made of keratin, the same tough protein found in human fingernails and hair, and they grow continuously throughout life.
