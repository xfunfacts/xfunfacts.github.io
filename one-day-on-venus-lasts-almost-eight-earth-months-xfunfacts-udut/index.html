One Day on Venus Lasts Almost Eight Earth Months xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A single day on Venus lasts almost eight Earth months because the planet rotates extremely slowly on its axis.

A single day on Venus lasts almost eight Earth months because the planet rotates extraordinarily slowly. In fact, Venus spins so slowly that one rotation takes longer than one complete orbit around the Sun. Even more unusual, Venus rotates in the opposite direction from most other planets, meaning the Sun appears to rise in the west and set in the east. These unusual characteristics make Venus one of the most fascinating planets in our solar system.

Did you know?

Venus rotates in the opposite direction from most planets, so an observer above its clouds would see the Sun rise in the west and set in the east.
