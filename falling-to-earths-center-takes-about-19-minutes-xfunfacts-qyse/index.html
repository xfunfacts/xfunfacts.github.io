Falling to Earth's Center Takes About 19 Minutes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A theoretical fall through Earth's center would take about 19 minutes without air resistance or other obstacles.

If a straight tunnel could somehow be built through the exact center of Earth and all air resistance were removed, falling through it would take only about 19 minutes from one side of the planet to the other. During the journey, gravity would continuously accelerate the traveler until reaching the center, then gradually slow the fall while climbing toward the opposite side. In reality, such a tunnel cannot exist because Earth's intense heat, pressure, and molten outer core make the scenario impossible with current technology. Even so, physicists use this thought experiment to explain gravity and orbital motion.

Did you know?

In the idealized gravity-tunnel calculation, the 19-minute fall assumes a simplified Earth model and ignores air resistance, rotation, temperature, and pressure.
