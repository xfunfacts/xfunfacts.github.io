Men Are Struck by Lightning More Often Than Women xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Men account for most lightning strike victims in many countries because they are more often exposed during outdoor activities.

In many countries, men account for the majority of lightning strike victims because they are statistically more likely to work or spend recreational time outdoors during thunderstorms. Occupations such as farming, construction, fishing, and forestry often increase exposure to lightning. Although anyone can be struck, following weather warnings and seeking shelter inside a substantial building or enclosed vehicle greatly reduces the risk. Lightning is extremely powerful, capable of reaching temperatures hotter than the surface of the Sun for a brief instant, making proper safety precautions essential.

Did you know?

Lightning fatalities are influenced more by exposure and behavior than biology, with outdoor occupations and recreation helping explain why male victims are more common.
