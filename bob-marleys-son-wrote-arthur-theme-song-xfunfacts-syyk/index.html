Bob Marley's Son Wrote Arthur Theme Song xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Ziggy Marley, the son of Bob Marley, performed the theme song for the children's cartoon Arthur. The catchy opening became familiar to generations of viewers.

Ziggy Marley, the eldest son of reggae legend Bob Marley, introduced a generation of children to his music by writing and performing the theme song for the animated television series Arthur. Created when he was 26 years old, the cheerful opening song became closely associated with the long-running educational program, which premiered in 1996 and aired for more than two decades. Although Ziggy inherited his father's musical talent, he developed his own successful career by blending reggae with pop and family-oriented music. His work on Arthur introduced his voice to millions of young viewers while expanding his audience beyond traditional reggae fans. The theme song remains one of the most recognizable children's television openings ever created.

Did you know?

Ziggy Marley and the Melody Makers performed “Believe in Yourself,” the opening theme for Arthur, helping connect reggae-influenced music with a generation of children.
