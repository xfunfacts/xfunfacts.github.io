A Fly Reacts in Just 30 Milliseconds xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A fly reacts in about 30 milliseconds, giving it incredibly fast reflexes that help it escape predators and swatting hands.

A fly can react to danger in about 30 milliseconds, allowing it to escape approaching predators with astonishing speed. Specialized eyes containing thousands of tiny lenses give flies a wide field of view and help them detect even slight movements. Their nervous system processes visual information rapidly, enabling quick changes in flight direction before a threat reaches them. These remarkable abilities have inspired research into high-speed robotics, drone navigation, and artificial vision systems.

Did you know?

Flies process visual information extremely quickly, which helps explain why a slowly approaching swatter may appear much easier for them to avoid.
