The Real Name of the Hashtag Is Octothorpe xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The # symbol is officially called an octothorpe, a name that predates the rise of social media hashtags.

Although most people call the "#" symbol a hashtag or pound sign, its official technical name is "octothorpe." The term became popular among telephone engineers during the twentieth century, though the exact origin of the word "thorpe" remains uncertain. Today the symbol has become one of the most recognizable characters in digital communication thanks to social media platforms, where hashtags organize conversations and make topics easier to search. Its history stretches far beyond the internet age.

Did you know?

The term “octothorpe” appears to have been coined at Bell Labs in the 1960s or early 1970s, but the exact story behind the unusual name remains disputed. Wiktionary
