You're More Likely to Be Stung by a Bee on a Windy Day xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Bees may become more defensive on windy days because strong winds interfere with their flight and normal foraging behavior.

Bees may become more defensive during windy conditions because strong air currents interfere with their ability to fly, navigate, and detect scents. Gusty weather can make it more difficult for bees to forage efficiently, increasing stress around the hive and causing them to react more aggressively when disturbed. Although most bees sting only to defend themselves or their colony, giving hives plenty of space and avoiding sudden movements remain the best ways to reduce the risk of being stung. Calm weather generally allows bees to forage more peacefully.

Did you know?

Wind can reduce bees' ability to forage efficiently, so more workers may remain near the hive, where disturbance can trigger defensive behavior.
