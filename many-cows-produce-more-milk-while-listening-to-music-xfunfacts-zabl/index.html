Many Cows Produce More Milk While Listening to Music xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Calm music may help dairy cows feel more relaxed, and studies suggest this can support increased milk production.

Research has suggested that calm, relaxing music may help some dairy cows produce slightly more milk by reducing stress. Comfortable animals generally eat better, rest more, and maintain healthier hormone levels that support milk production. Farmers often combine music with good nutrition, clean housing, and proper veterinary care to improve overall herd welfare. Although music alone is not responsible for higher yields, it can contribute to a calmer environment that benefits both the animals and farm productivity.

Did you know?

Some studies suggest slow-tempo music may reduce stress-related behavior in dairy cattle, although responses can vary between herds and environments.
