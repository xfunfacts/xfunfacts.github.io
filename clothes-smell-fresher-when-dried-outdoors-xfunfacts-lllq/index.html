Clothes Smell Fresher When Dried Outdoors xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Clothes dried outdoors often smell fresher because sunlight and fresh air help reduce odor-causing compounds naturally.

Clothes often smell fresher when dried outdoors because sunlight and fresh air help eliminate odor-causing compounds that can remain trapped in fabric after washing. Ultraviolet light contributes through a process called photolysis, which breaks down some organic molecules responsible for unpleasant smells. Outdoor drying also allows moving air to carry away moisture more effectively, reducing the chance for mildew to develop. In addition, sunlight has mild natural disinfecting properties that can reduce certain microorganisms. While prolonged sun exposure may gradually fade some fabrics, line drying remains an energy-efficient and environmentally friendly way to dry laundry.

Did you know?

Sunlight can trigger photochemical reactions in damp fabrics that create small amounts of volatile compounds associated with the characteristic smell of line-dried laundry.
