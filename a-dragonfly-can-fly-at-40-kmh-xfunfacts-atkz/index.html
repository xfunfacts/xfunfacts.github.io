A Dragonfly Can Fly at 40 km/h xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Dragonflies can fly at speeds of around 40 km/h while performing remarkable aerial maneuvers to catch prey.

Dragonflies can fly at speeds of around 40 kilometers per hour while maintaining exceptional agility and control. Their four independently moving wings allow them to hover, fly backward, change direction instantly, and capture prey with remarkable precision. Dragonflies have some of the best eyesight in the insect world, with large compound eyes providing nearly 360-degree vision. They feed mainly on mosquitoes, flies, and other small insects, making them beneficial predators in many ecosystems. Fossil evidence shows that ancient relatives of dragonflies existed more than 300 million years ago, demonstrating the long evolutionary history of these highly successful flying insects.

Did you know?

Dragonflies can fly forward, backward, sideways, and even hover because their four wings can be controlled with remarkable independence.
