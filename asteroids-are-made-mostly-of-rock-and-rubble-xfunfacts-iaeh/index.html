Asteroids Are Made Mostly of Rock and Rubble xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many asteroids are rocky bodies or loose rubble piles left over from the formation of the solar system.

Asteroids are primarily composed of rock, metal, and rubble left over from the formation of the solar system about 4.6 billion years ago. While some asteroids are solid bodies, others are loosely connected collections of rocks held together by their own weak gravity, often called "rubble piles." These ancient objects preserve materials that have changed very little since the planets formed. Space missions such as OSIRIS-REx and Hayabusa2 have collected samples from asteroids to help scientists study the earliest building blocks of the solar system. Their discoveries provide valuable insight into planetary formation.

Did you know?

Some asteroids are so loosely bound that gentle impacts could rearrange their surfaces rather than shatter them like solid rock.
