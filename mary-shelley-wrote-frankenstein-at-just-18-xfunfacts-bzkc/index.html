Mary Shelley Wrote Frankenstein at Just 18 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Mary Shelley began writing Frankenstein at just 18 years old, creating one of literature's most influential novels.

Mary Shelley began writing Frankenstein when she was only 18 years old during a stay near Lake Geneva in Switzerland with Percy Shelley and Lord Byron. The novel grew out of a friendly ghost story competition but evolved into one of the most influential works in literary history. Often regarded as one of the first science fiction novels, Frankenstein continues to raise thought-provoking questions about scientific responsibility, ethics, and the consequences of human ambition more than two centuries after it was written.

Did you know?

Mary Shelley conceived Frankenstein during a storytelling challenge near Lake Geneva in 1816, alongside Lord Byron and Percy Shelley; the novel was published anonymously in 1818 when she was 20.
