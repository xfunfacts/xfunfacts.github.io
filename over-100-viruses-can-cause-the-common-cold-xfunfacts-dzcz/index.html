Over 100 Viruses Can Cause the Common Cold xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] More than 100 different viruses can cause the common cold, making repeated infections common throughout a person's lifetime.

More than 100 different viruses can cause the common cold, which is one reason people can catch colds repeatedly throughout their lives. Rhinoviruses are the most common cause, but coronaviruses, adenoviruses, respiratory syncytial virus, and several other virus families can produce similar symptoms. Because so many different viruses are responsible, creating a single vaccine that prevents every common cold is extremely difficult. Most infections resolve on their own within a week or two as the immune system eliminates the virus. Good hygiene practices, including regular handwashing, avoiding close contact with sick individuals, and covering coughs and sneezes, remain among the most effective ways to reduce the spread of cold viruses.

Did you know?

Rhinoviruses cause many common colds and include numerous distinct types, while seasonal coronaviruses, adenoviruses, and other respiratory viruses can produce similar symptoms.
