Sea Turtles Return Home to Nest xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Female sea turtles return to the same beaches where they hatched to lay the next generation of eggs.

Female sea turtles display remarkable navigation skills by returning to the same beaches where they hatched decades earlier to lay their own eggs. Scientists believe they use Earth's magnetic field, along with other environmental cues, to locate these familiar nesting sites across thousands of miles of open ocean. After digging nests in the sand, females lay their eggs before returning to the sea. The hatchlings later emerge and begin the long journey back to the ocean on their own. This extraordinary migration is one of nature's greatest navigational achievements.

Did you know?

Female sea turtles show natal homing, often returning to the same region where they hatched, likely using Earth's magnetic field among other environmental cues.
