Mary Had a Little Lamb Was Inspired by a Real Girl xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The nursery rhyme Mary Had a Little Lamb was inspired by the real-life story of Mary Sawyer and her pet lamb.

The nursery rhyme Mary Had a Little Lamb was inspired by the real-life story of Mary Sawyer, a young girl from Massachusetts whose pet lamb famously followed her to school. The unusual event became well known in the local community before eventually inspiring one of the world's most recognizable children's poems. Over the years, the rhyme has been translated into many languages and taught to generations of children. It remains a lasting example of how an ordinary childhood moment can become part of popular culture.

Did you know?

Mary Sawyer of Massachusetts claimed that her childhood lamb inspired the nursery rhyme, while the earliest published verses appeared in Sarah Josepha Hale's 1830 collection, making the story's precise origins contested.
