Michelin Stars Come from a Tire Company xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Michelin Stars began as part of a travel guide created by the Michelin tire company to encourage people to drive farther.

The famous Michelin Stars awarded to outstanding restaurants originated with the Michelin tire company. In 1900, Michelin published a travel guide to encourage motorists to take longer trips, creating more demand for tires. The guide eventually began recommending restaurants, and its star rating system became one of the highest honors in fine dining. Today, earning even a single Michelin Star can transform a restaurant's reputation and attract visitors from around the world.

Did you know?

The Michelin Guide was first published in 1900, but restaurant stars appeared in 1926; the three-star system followed in 1931, with three stars eventually signifying cuisine worth a special journey.
