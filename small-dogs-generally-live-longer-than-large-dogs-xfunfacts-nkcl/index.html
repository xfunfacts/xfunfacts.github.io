Small Dogs Generally Live Longer Than Large Dogs xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Smaller dog breeds generally live longer than larger breeds, although genetics and overall care also affect lifespan.

Smaller dog breeds generally live longer than larger breeds, with many small dogs reaching 12 to 16 years of age or even more under good care. Scientists believe larger dogs tend to age more rapidly because their bodies grow faster and experience greater physical stress over time. Genetics, nutrition, exercise, veterinary care, and overall lifestyle also influence a dog's lifespan regardless of size. Regular health checkups and proper daily care can help dogs of all breeds enjoy longer, healthier lives.

Did you know?

Large dog breeds tend to age faster biologically, which may help explain why their average lifespans are often shorter than those of small breeds.
