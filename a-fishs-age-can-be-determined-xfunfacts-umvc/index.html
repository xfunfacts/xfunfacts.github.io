A Fish's Age Can Be Determined xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Fish age can be estimated by studying growth rings on scales or tiny ear bones called otoliths.

A fish's age can often be estimated by examining growth rings found on its scales, fin spines, or tiny ear bones called otoliths. Much like counting the rings of a tree, scientists analyze these growth patterns to estimate how many years the fish has lived. This information helps researchers understand population health, growth rates, and sustainable fishing practices. Accurate age estimates are important for managing fish stocks and protecting marine ecosystems. The technique has become a valuable tool in fisheries science.

Did you know?

Scientists often age fish by counting annual growth bands in otoliths, structures in the inner ear that also help fish maintain balance and detect sound.
