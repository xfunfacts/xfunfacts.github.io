It Takes About 5 kg of Potatoes to Gain 0.5 kg xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Eating about 5 kg of potatoes provides roughly enough calories to support a gain of about 0.5 kg of body weight.

Eating about 5 kilograms of potatoes provides roughly enough calories to support a gain of about 0.5 kilograms of body weight, although actual weight change depends on a person's metabolism, activity level, and overall diet. Potatoes are often misunderstood as unhealthy, but they are naturally rich in vitamin C, potassium, vitamin B6, and complex carbohydrates while containing very little fat. A plain potato has roughly the same number of calories as an apple of similar size, with most additional calories coming from toppings such as butter, cheese, cream, or frying oil. When prepared by boiling, baking, or steaming, potatoes can be part of a balanced diet and provide lasting energy because their starch is digested more slowly than many refined carbohydrates.

Did you know?

Five kilograms of raw potatoes provide approximately 3,850 calories, but gaining half a kilogram of body fat depends on sustained energy surplus, metabolism, and activity rather than eating a fixed potato quantity.
