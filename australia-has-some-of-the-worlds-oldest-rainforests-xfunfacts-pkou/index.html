Australia Has Some of the World's Oldest Rainforests xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Australia's Daintree Rainforest is among the oldest tropical rainforests on Earth, home to exceptional biodiversity.

Australia's Daintree Rainforest is considered one of the oldest tropical rainforests on Earth, with origins dating back more than 100 million years. This ancient forest contains thousands of plant species, many found nowhere else in the world, along with rare birds, reptiles, insects, and mammals. Scientists study the rainforest because it provides valuable insight into the evolution of plants that once covered much of the ancient supercontinent Gondwana. The Daintree also borders the Great Barrier Reef, creating one of the few places where two UNESCO World Heritage Sites meet. It remains one of Australia's greatest ecological treasures.

Did you know?

The Daintree region contains plant lineages with roots stretching back to the ancient Gondwanan forests, although the individual trees growing there today are not themselves millions of years old.
