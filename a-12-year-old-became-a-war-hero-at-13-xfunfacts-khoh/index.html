A 12-Year-Old Became a War Hero at 13 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A 12-year-old boy lied about his age to join the U.S. Navy during World War II. He became a decorated war hero at just 13 before the truth was discovered.

In 1942, Calvin Graham became one of the youngest American servicemen of World War II after lying about his age to enlist in the U.S. Navy at just 12 years old. After successfully passing as an older teenager, he served aboard the battleship USS South Dakota and experienced intense combat during the Naval Battle of Guadalcanal. His bravery earned him several military decorations while he was still only 13 years old. However, his remarkable military career came to an abrupt end when his mother discovered the truth and informed authorities, leading to his discharge. Years later, much of his military recognition was restored, and his story became one of the most extraordinary examples of youthful courage in wartime history. Calvin Graham's experience continues to inspire historians because it highlights both the sacrifices of wartime service and the importance of verifying military enlistment standards.
