Game of Thrones Used IKEA Rugs as Cloaks xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Game of Thrones costume designers transformed IKEA rugs into realistic Night's Watch cloaks for the series.

The rugged cloaks worn by the Night's Watch in Game of Thrones were created from ordinary IKEA rugs rather than expensive custom fabrics. Costume designers shaved the wool, dyed the material, weathered it by hand, and carefully stitched it into realistic garments that matched the harsh conditions beyond the Wall. This creative behind-the-scenes technique demonstrates how film and television productions often transform inexpensive everyday items into convincing costumes through skilled craftsmanship. Fans were amazed when the surprising production secret became public.

Did you know?

Game of Thrones costume designer Michele Clapton confirmed that IKEA rugs were cut, dyed, waxed, and distressed to create some Night's Watch cloaks.
