Tiny Mites Live on Human Eyelashes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Microscopic Demodex mites naturally live around the eyelashes of nearly all adults without causing noticeable problems.

Nearly all adults host microscopic Demodex mites around their eyelashes and hair follicles without ever noticing them. These tiny arachnids feed on dead skin cells and natural oils produced by the skin, forming part of the normal ecosystem that exists on the human body. In most people they cause no problems, although unusually high populations may contribute to certain eye or skin conditions. Their presence highlights the complex relationship humans share with countless microscopic organisms that naturally live on and inside the body.

Did you know?

Demodex mites inhabit human hair follicles and sebaceous glands, especially around the face and eyelashes, and usually cause no symptoms unless their populations become excessive.
