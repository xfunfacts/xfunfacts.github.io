Snowy Owls Live in the Arctic xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Snowy owls thrive in the Arctic with thick white feathers that provide camouflage and insulation in freezing conditions.

Snowy owls are perfectly adapted to life in the Arctic, where their white feathers provide excellent camouflage against snow and ice. Unlike many other owl species, snowy owls are active during both the day and night, especially during the Arctic summer when daylight lasts almost around the clock. Their thick plumage covers even their legs and feet, protecting them from freezing temperatures. Snowy owls mainly hunt lemmings but will also eat birds and other small mammals when necessary. Their striking appearance and remarkable cold-weather adaptations make them one of the world's most recognizable owls.

Did you know?

Snowy owls have heavily feathered feet and toes, creating natural insulation that helps protect them from extreme Arctic cold.
