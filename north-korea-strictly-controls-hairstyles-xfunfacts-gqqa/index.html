North Korea Strictly Controls Hairstyles xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] North Korea is widely reported to regulate approved hairstyles for men and women. The exact number of permitted styles varies across different sources.

North Korea is frequently reported to regulate hairstyles through officially approved styles for men and women, although details vary depending on the source and time period. Numerous reports from journalists, researchers, and defectors describe government guidance encouraging citizens to choose from specific haircuts considered appropriate under state standards. Exact numbers of approved hairstyles differ between publications, and independent verification remains difficult because of the country's limited access to outside observers. The reported regulations are often discussed alongside broader government controls over clothing, fashion, and public appearance. While some stories have been exaggerated in international media, restrictions on personal expression remain a widely studied aspect of North Korean society. The topic highlights the relationship between politics, culture, and individual identity in one of the world's most isolated nations.

Did you know?

North Korean authorities impose extensive restrictions on personal expression, but widely circulated claims that citizens may choose only from a fixed government-approved list of hairstyles lack strong independent verification.
