Your Brain Is Only 2% of Your Body Weight xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Although your brain is only about 2% of body weight, it consumes around one-fifth of your body's oxygen every day.

Although the human brain makes up only about two percent of total body weight, it consumes roughly one-fifth of the body's oxygen and a significant portion of its blood supply. This constant demand reflects the enormous amount of work performed by billions of neurons communicating through electrical and chemical signals every second. The brain controls movement, memory, emotions, decision-making, and countless automatic processes such as breathing and heartbeat. To maintain peak performance, it depends on a steady supply of oxygen, glucose, quality sleep, physical activity, and balanced nutrition. Even small interruptions in blood flow can quickly affect brain function, highlighting how metabolically active this remarkable organ truly is.

Did you know?

An adult brain typically weighs about 1.3–1.4 kilograms, yet it requires a continuous supply of oxygen and glucose because neurons have limited energy reserves.
