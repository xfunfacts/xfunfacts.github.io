Finland Has More Than Three Million Saunas xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Finland has more than three million saunas, making sauna bathing an everyday tradition for much of the population.

With more than three million saunas serving a population of about 5.6 million people, Finland has nearly one sauna for every two residents. Sauna bathing has been part of Finnish culture for centuries and is valued as a place to relax, socialize, and unwind after work or exercise. Saunas can be found in private homes, apartment buildings, businesses, and even government offices. While many people associate saunas with luxury, in Finland they are considered an ordinary part of everyday life and national identity.

Did you know?

Finnish sauna culture emphasizes relaxation, cleansing, and social connection; UNESCO added it to its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2020.
