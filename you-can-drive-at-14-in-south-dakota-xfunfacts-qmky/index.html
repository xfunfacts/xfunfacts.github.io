You Can Drive at 14 in South Dakota xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] In South Dakota, teenagers can legally begin learning to drive at just 14 years old with parental permission.

South Dakota is one of the few U.S. states that allows teenagers to begin driving at an unusually young age. With parental consent, young people can apply for a learner's permit at age 14, giving them the opportunity to gain driving experience well before reaching adulthood. After meeting state requirements, including supervised driving and passing the necessary tests, they may become eligible for a full driver's license at age 16. These rules reflect the practical transportation needs of a largely rural state, where long distances and limited public transportation make personal vehicles especially important. Early licensing helps teenagers travel to school, work, and family responsibilities while learning safe driving habits under supervision. Although the minimum driving age varies across the United States, South Dakota has consistently maintained one of the earliest licensing systems. The state's approach highlights how geography, population density, and local lifestyles can influence traffic laws and licensing policies.

Did you know?

South Dakota has historically allowed very young drivers under graduated licensing rules, but permit ages and restrictions can change with state law.
