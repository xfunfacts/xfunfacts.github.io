The Moon Is the Main Reason Earth Has Tides xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Without the Moon, Earth's tides would be dramatically different. Its gravity shapes the oceans every single day across the planet.

The Moon is the primary force behind Earth's ocean tides. Its gravity pulls on our planet, creating bulges of water that produce the high and low tides experienced along coastlines every day. The Sun also influences tides, but because it is much farther away, its effect is weaker despite its enormous size. When the Sun, Moon, and Earth align, their combined gravity creates especially high and low tides known as spring tides. When the Sun and Moon are at right angles, they produce milder neap tides. Tides play a vital role in coastal ecosystems, navigation, fishing, and marine life, making the Moon an essential part of Earth's natural environment.

Did you know?

The Sun also raises tides on Earth, but the Moon has the stronger tidal influence because it is far closer to our planet.
