One Day on Venus Lasts Almost an Earth Year xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] One day on Venus lasts nearly an Earth year because the planet rotates incredibly slowly.

A single day on Venus lasts almost as long as an Earth year because Venus rotates extraordinarily slowly on its axis. One complete rotation takes about 243 Earth days, while the planet completes one orbit around the Sun in about 225 Earth days. Venus also rotates in the opposite direction of most planets, causing the Sun to appear to rise in the west and set in the east. These unusual characteristics make Venus one of the most fascinating and mysterious planets in our solar system.

Did you know?

Venus takes about 243 Earth days to rotate once, yet its solar day is only about 117 Earth days because the planet rotates backward while orbiting the Sun.
