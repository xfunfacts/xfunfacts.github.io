Early Golf Balls Were Stuffed with Feathers xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Early golf balls, known as featheries, were handmade from leather and tightly packed with feathers before modern designs appeared.

Before the invention of modern rubber golf balls, many golf balls were handmade from leather pouches tightly packed with boiled goose feathers. Known as featheries, these balls were carefully stitched together while the leather was still wet so it would shrink tightly around the feathers as it dried. Producing a single ball required considerable skill and time, making featheries expensive and valuable. Although they eventually gave way to more durable designs, feather golf balls played a significant role in the early history of the sport.

Did you know?

Featherie golf balls were made by stuffing wet feathers into a wet leather pouch; as both dried, the expanding feathers created a hard, compact ball.
