Cashew Nuts Grow Attached to Cashew Apples xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Cashew nuts grow beneath colorful cashew apples, which are widely used for juice and other beverages in Brazil.

The edible cashew nut grows attached to the bottom of a colorful structure known as the cashew apple. While the nut is widely exported around the world, the juicy cashew apple is highly perishable and is usually consumed close to where it is grown. In countries such as Brazil, it is commonly made into juice, jams, and soft drinks. Before reaching grocery stores, raw cashew shells must be carefully processed because they contain irritating natural oils.

Did you know?

The cashew nut develops inside a hard, kidney-shaped shell attached beneath the swollen cashew apple, which is technically an accessory fruit; the shell contains irritating compounds that require careful processing.
