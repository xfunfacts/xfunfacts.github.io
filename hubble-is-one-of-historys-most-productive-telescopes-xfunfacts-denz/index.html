Hubble Is One of History's Most Productive Telescopes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hubble has powered more than 15,000 scientific studies since its launch. Few scientific instruments have transformed astronomy so dramatically.

The Hubble Space Telescope has transformed astronomy since its launch in 1990 by providing some of the clearest images ever captured of the universe. Orbiting above Earth's atmosphere, Hubble avoids the distortion that limits ground-based telescopes, allowing it to observe distant galaxies, nebulae, and planets with remarkable detail. Its discoveries have contributed to more than 15,000 scientific papers, making it one of the most productive scientific instruments in history. Hubble has helped measure the expansion rate of the universe, study black holes, investigate exoplanets, and capture breathtaking images that continue to inspire scientists and the public alike.

Did you know?

Hubble orbits above most of Earth's atmosphere, avoiding atmospheric distortion and allowing exceptionally sharp observations in visible, ultraviolet, and near-infrared light.
