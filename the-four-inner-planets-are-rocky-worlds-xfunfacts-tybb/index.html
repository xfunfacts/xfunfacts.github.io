The Four Inner Planets Are Rocky Worlds xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars are the solar system's four rocky inner planets with solid surfaces and unique landscapes.

Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars are known as the terrestrial or rocky planets because they possess solid surfaces made primarily of rock and metal. These four inner planets are relatively small compared with the giant planets farther from the Sun and have much higher densities. Each one has a unique environment, from Mercury's crater-covered landscape to Venus' scorching atmosphere, Earth's life-supporting conditions, and Mars' dusty deserts. Their rocky surfaces preserve evidence of volcanic activity, impact craters, and geological processes that help scientists understand planetary evolution. Because they formed closer to the young Sun, lighter gases were largely blown away, leaving behind heavier materials. The terrestrial planets provide important clues about how Earth itself formed and changed over billions of years.

Did you know?

The terrestrial planets are much denser than the giant planets because they are composed mainly of rock and metal rather than hydrogen and helium.
