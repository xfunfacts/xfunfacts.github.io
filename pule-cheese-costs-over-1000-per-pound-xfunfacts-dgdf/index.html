Pule Cheese Costs Over $1,000 Per Pound xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Pule cheese is made from rare donkey milk at a single Serbian farm. Its limited production makes it one of the world's most expensive cheeses.

Pule cheese is considered one of the rarest and most expensive cheeses in the world because it is made almost entirely from donkey milk. Production takes place at a single farm in Serbia, where donkeys produce only small quantities of milk compared with cows or goats. It can take many liters of milk to produce just one kilogram of cheese, making the process extremely labor-intensive and limiting supply. As a result, Pule cheese often sells for well over one thousand dollars per pound, although prices vary depending on availability. The cheese is known for its mild, slightly nutty flavor and crumbly texture. Its rarity has made it a luxury food sought after by collectors and gourmet enthusiasts. Pule demonstrates how limited production, specialized farming, and unique ingredients can dramatically increase the value of a traditional food product.

Did you know?

Pule cheese is produced in Serbia's Zasavica region using Balkan donkey milk, which is difficult to turn into cheese because of its low casein content and limited milk yield per animal.
