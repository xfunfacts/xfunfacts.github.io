This American Town Has Only One Resident xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] One American town has a mayor, librarian, bartender—and they're all the same person because only one resident lives there.

Hidden in the rural state of Nebraska is one of America's most unusual communities: the village of Monowi, which has an official population of just one person. The town's only resident, Elsie Eiler, serves as the mayor, librarian, bartender, tax collector, and virtually every other public official required to keep the village operating. Each year she even prepares the town's budget, pays local taxes, and renews Monowi's official liquor license for the tavern she runs herself. Once home to dozens of residents during the early twentieth century, Monowi gradually declined as younger generations moved away in search of jobs and opportunities elsewhere. After her husband passed away in 2004, Elsie became the village's sole remaining resident. Despite its tiny population, Monowi has become an international curiosity, attracting tourists who visit simply to experience one of the world's smallest functioning municipalities. The town serves as a fascinating reminder of how demographic and economic changes can reshape rural communities over time.

Did you know?

Monowi, Nebraska, became famous when Elsie Eiler served as its sole resident, mayor, and tavern owner, illustrating how population decline transformed many small American farming communities.
