Isaac Asimov Wrote Books in Every Dewey Decimal Category xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Isaac Asimov is widely credited with publishing books across every major Dewey Decimal category, from science and history to literature and humor.

Isaac Asimov was one of the most prolific authors in modern history, publishing or editing more than 500 books during his lifetime. He is widely credited as the only writer to have produced books covering every major category of the Dewey Decimal Classification system, ranging from science and mathematics to history, literature, religion, and humor. Although best known for his groundbreaking science fiction works, including the Foundation and Robot series, Asimov was equally passionate about explaining scientific concepts to general readers. His ability to communicate complex ideas with clarity made him one of the twentieth century's most influential science writers. His extraordinary range of knowledge and curiosity allowed him to cross disciplines in a way that few authors have ever matched.

Did you know?

The popular claim that Asimov wrote in every Dewey Decimal category is exaggerated, though his extraordinary bibliography spans science, history, literature, humor, and many other fields.
