Julius Caesar's Alleged Son Became Egypt's Last Pharaoh xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Cleopatra believed Caesarion was Julius Caesar's son, and he would go on to become the last pharaoh of ancient Egypt.

Caesarion occupies a unique and mysterious place in ancient Egyptian history as the final pharaoh of Egypt's long dynastic tradition. Officially named Ptolemy XV Caesar, he was the son of Queen Cleopatra VII, who insisted that Julius Caesar was his father. Although many historians consider this claim plausible, Julius Caesar never formally acknowledged Caesarion as his heir during his lifetime. After Caesar's assassination, Cleopatra ruled Egypt while presenting her young son as co-ruler and legitimate successor. Following Cleopatra's alliance with Mark Antony and their eventual defeat by Octavian, the future Emperor Augustus viewed Caesarion as a potential political rival because of his claimed connection to Julius Caesar. Shortly after Egypt fell under Roman control in 30 BC, Caesarion was captured and executed, bringing an end to the Ptolemaic dynasty and thousands of years of rule by Egyptian pharaohs. His brief reign marked the final chapter of ancient Egypt as an independent kingdom before it became a province of the expanding Roman Empire.

Did you know?

Caesarion, officially Ptolemy XV Caesar, ruled alongside Cleopatra VII and briefly after her death in 30 BC; his claimed paternity by Julius Caesar remains historically debated.
