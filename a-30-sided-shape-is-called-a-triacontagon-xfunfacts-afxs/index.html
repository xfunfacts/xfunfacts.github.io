A 30-Sided Shape Is Called a Triacontagon xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A polygon with 30 sides is called a triacontagon. The name comes from Ancient Greek words meaning thirty and angle.

In geometry, a polygon with 30 sides is called a triacontagon, a name derived from ancient Greek words meaning "thirty angles." Like all polygons, a triacontagon can be either regular, with all sides and angles equal, or irregular, with varying side lengths and angles. While rarely encountered in everyday life, these shapes appear in mathematics, computer graphics, architecture, and decorative design. Studying polygons with many sides helps mathematicians understand geometric patterns, symmetry, and the relationships between angles and perimeter. The triacontagon is one of many fascinating examples showing how geometry extends far beyond simple triangles and squares.

Did you know?

A regular triacontagon has 30 equal sides and 30 equal interior angles, each measuring 168 degrees; the sum of all its interior angles is 5,040 degrees.
