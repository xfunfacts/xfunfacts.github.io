BTS Was the First K-Pop Group With Twitter Emojis xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] BTS became the first K-pop group to receive custom Twitter emojis linked to hashtags. The milestone reflected the group's massive global popularity.

BTS became the first K-pop group to receive official custom Twitter emojis linked to their hashtags, marking an important milestone in the relationship between social media and global music fandoms. Whenever fans used designated BTS hashtags, special purple-themed emojis automatically appeared, increasing engagement across the platform. The feature reflected BTS's enormous online influence and the extraordinary dedication of their worldwide fan community known as ARMY. Social media played a major role in the group's international success, allowing them to connect directly with millions of fans across different countries and languages. The achievement demonstrated how digital platforms were becoming increasingly important for global entertainment and fan interaction.

Did you know?

BTS received Twitter’s first custom K-pop emoji campaign in 2016, illustrating how social-media fandom became an important force in the international spread of K-pop.
