Caesar Salad Was Invented in 1924 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Caesar salad wasn't named after Julius Caesar. It was created in 1924 by chef Caesar Cardini at his restaurant in Tijuana, Mexico.

The Caesar salad is named after its creator, Italian chef Caesar Cardini, not the Roman emperor Julius Caesar. Cardini invented the famous dish in 1924 at his restaurant in Tijuana, Mexico, where many Americans traveled during Prohibition. According to the popular story, he created the salad using ingredients that were still available during a busy holiday weekend. The original recipe included crisp romaine lettuce, olive oil, garlic, Parmesan cheese, egg, Worcestershire sauce, lemon juice, and croutons. Today, Caesar salad is enjoyed around the world and remains one of the most recognizable salads ever created.

Did you know?

Caesar salad became popular in Tijuana during American Prohibition, and early versions featured whole romaine leaves, Parmesan, croutons, and a dressing prepared at the table; its precise origin remains debated.
