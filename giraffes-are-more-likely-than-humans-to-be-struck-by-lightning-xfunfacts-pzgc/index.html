Giraffes Are More Likely Than Humans to Be Struck by Lightning xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Giraffes are more vulnerable to lightning than humans because their exceptional height makes them stand out across open landscapes.

Giraffes are struck by lightning more often than humans because their great height makes them one of the tallest objects across open African landscapes. Researchers have documented multiple cases showing that lightning poses a significant natural hazard to these animals. Like trees and tall structures, giraffes are more likely to become part of a lightning strike's path. Their vulnerability reminds scientists that weather can influence wildlife survival in unexpected ways.

Did you know?

Because giraffes tower above most animals, thunderstorms on open savannas may expose them to lightning risks that shorter animals are less likely to face.
