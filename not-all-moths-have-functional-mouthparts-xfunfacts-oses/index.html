Not All Moths Have Functional Mouthparts xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Many adult moths have reduced or nonfunctional mouthparts and survive on energy stored during their caterpillar stage.

Many adult moth species have reduced or completely nonfunctional mouthparts and do not eat during the final stage of their lives. Instead, they rely on energy stored during their caterpillar stage, when feeding is their primary activity. Adult moths with nonfunctional mouths usually live only long enough to mate and reproduce before dying. Other moth species, however, retain fully functional mouthparts and feed on nectar or other food sources. This remarkable variation reflects the incredible diversity found within the moth family.

Did you know?

Adult moth species without functional mouthparts depend entirely on energy reserves stored during the caterpillar stage to survive long enough to reproduce.
