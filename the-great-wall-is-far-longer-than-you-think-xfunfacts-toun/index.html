The Great Wall Is Far Longer Than You Think xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Great Wall of China is far longer than the commonly quoted 3,728 miles. Modern surveys measure its total length at about 13,171 miles across all sections.

The Great Wall of China is far longer than many people realize. Older estimates commonly stated that the wall measured about 3,728 miles, but comprehensive archaeological surveys have shown that the total length is approximately 13,171 miles when all connected walls, trenches, natural barriers, and branches built by different dynasties are included. Rather than being a single continuous wall, it is a vast defensive network constructed over many centuries. Different sections vary greatly in age, building materials, and condition depending on the region and historical period. The wall served military, transportation, and communication purposes while helping protect northern Chinese territories from invasions. Today it remains one of the world's most recognizable landmarks and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting millions of visitors while continuing to reveal new archaeological discoveries.

Did you know?

The Great Wall is not one continuous wall but a vast system of walls, trenches, fortifications, and natural barriers built by different dynasties.
