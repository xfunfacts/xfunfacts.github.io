Sound Cannot Travel Through Space xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Sound cannot travel through space because the vacuum contains almost no particles to carry vibrations.

Sound cannot travel through the vacuum of space because there are almost no particles available to carry sound waves. On Earth, sound moves by vibrating molecules in air, water, or solid materials, but the near-empty conditions of space prevent these vibrations from spreading. This is why explosions in space would appear silent to an outside observer. Communication between astronauts instead relies on radio waves, which are electromagnetic signals capable of traveling through the vacuum.

Did you know?

Sound requires a medium such as gas, liquid, or solid, so astronauts outside a spacecraft could not hear an explosion through the surrounding vacuum.
