Earth's Core Is as Hot as the Sun's Surface xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Earth's core is nearly as hot as the Sun's surface, generating the energy that helps power our planet's magnetic field.

Earth's core reaches temperatures comparable to the surface of the Sun, exceeding 9,000°F (about 5,000°C) in some regions. This intense heat comes from leftover energy from Earth's formation, radioactive decay, and pressure deep within the planet. Although extremely hot, the immense pressure keeps much of the inner core solid while the outer core remains molten. Movements within the liquid outer core generate Earth's magnetic field, which protects the planet from harmful solar radiation. The core is essential to Earth's long-term habitability.

Did you know?

Earth's inner core stays solid despite extreme heat because immense pressure raises the melting point of iron under deep planetary conditions.
