The World Record for Holding Breath Underwater Is Nearly 24.5 Minutes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Budimir Šobat set the world record for holding his breath underwater at nearly 24.5 minutes under carefully controlled conditions.

Croatian freediver Budimir Šobat set the world record for holding his breath underwater for nearly 24 and a half minutes after breathing pure oxygen beforehand. Competitive static apnea requires exceptional lung capacity, mental focus, and careful training to safely slow the body's oxygen consumption. Such records are performed under strict medical supervision because prolonged breath-holding can be extremely dangerous. The achievement highlights the remarkable limits of human physiology when combined with years of specialized preparation.

Did you know?

Budimir Šobat achieved a 24-minute, 37-second underwater breath hold in 2021 after breathing pure oxygen beforehand, making it fundamentally different from ordinary unaided breath-holding.
