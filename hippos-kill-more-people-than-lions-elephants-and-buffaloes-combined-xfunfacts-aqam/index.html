Hippos Kill More People Than Lions, Elephants, and Buffaloes Combined xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hippos cause more human deaths each year in parts of Africa than lions, elephants, and buffaloes combined because of their territorial behavior.

Hippos are responsible for more human deaths each year in parts of Africa than lions, elephants, and African buffaloes combined. Although they spend much of the day resting in rivers and lakes, hippos are highly territorial and can become extremely aggressive when people or boats approach too closely. Despite their heavy bodies, they can run surprisingly fast over short distances and possess powerful jaws capable of inflicting severe injuries. Wildlife experts recommend maintaining a safe distance from hippos at all times, especially near waterways where unexpected encounters are most likely.

Did you know?

Many serious hippo encounters occur near water, where the animals may aggressively defend access routes, calves, or areas they use during the day.
