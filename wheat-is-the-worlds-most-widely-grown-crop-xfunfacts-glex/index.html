Wheat Is the World's Most Widely Grown Crop xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Wheat is one of the world's most widely grown crops, providing a major source of calories and essential nutrients for billions of people.

Wheat is one of the world's most widely cultivated crops and provides a major source of calories for billions of people. It is used to make bread, pasta, noodles, cereals, pastries, and countless other foods. Different varieties of wheat thrive in diverse climates, allowing it to be grown across much of the globe. Beyond human consumption, wheat is also used as livestock feed and in industrial products. Advances in farming and plant breeding have significantly increased wheat yields, making it one of the foundations of global food security.

Did you know?

Wheat is among the world's most extensively cultivated crops and supplies a major share of human dietary calories, but "most widely grown" depends on the metric used.
