Sprite Became Coca-Cola's Third Major Soft Drink in 1961 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Introduced in 1961, Sprite became one of Coca-Cola's earliest global soft drink brands and remains popular worldwide.

Sprite was introduced by The Coca-Cola Company in 1961 as a clear lemon-lime soft drink designed to offer a crisp, caffeine-free alternative to cola beverages. It quickly became one of the company's most successful global brands and is now sold in countries around the world. Over the decades, Sprite has been marketed through memorable advertising campaigns and regional flavor variations while maintaining its refreshing citrus identity. Its popularity demonstrates how a simple product can become an internationally recognized brand through consistent marketing and widespread distribution.

Did you know?

Sprite debuted in the United States in 1961 as a lemon-lime soft drink, entering a market already shaped by competitors such as 7UP and eventually becoming one of Coca-Cola's major international brands.
