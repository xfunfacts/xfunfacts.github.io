China Has 3,000 Miles of Underground Missile Tunnels xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] China is reported to operate about 3,000 miles of underground missile tunnels. The vast network is believed to store and transport strategic missiles.

China is widely believed to operate an enormous underground tunnel network stretching roughly 3,000 miles to support its strategic missile forces. Often referred to as the "Underground Great Wall," the system is designed to conceal, transport, and protect mobile intercontinental ballistic missiles from surveillance and potential attacks. Built deep beneath mountains and reinforced with extensive infrastructure, the tunnels provide flexibility for moving military equipment while making it far more difficult for adversaries to determine missile locations. Although many details remain classified, military analysts consider the network an important component of China's nuclear deterrence strategy and one of the largest underground military construction projects ever undertaken.

Did you know?

A 2011 Georgetown University research project described an estimated 5,000-kilometer Chinese underground missile tunnel network, sometimes called the Underground Great Wall, but its precise extent and capabilities remain independently unverified.
