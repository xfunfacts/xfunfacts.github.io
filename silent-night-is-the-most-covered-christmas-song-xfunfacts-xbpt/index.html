"Silent Night" Is the Most Covered Christmas Song xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] "Silent Night" has been recorded more than 26,000 times since 1818, making it the most covered Christmas song in history.

Since its first performance in 1818, "Silent Night" has become the most recorded Christmas song in history, with more than 26,000 known versions in numerous languages. Originally written in Austria by Joseph Mohr and Franz Xaver Gruber, the simple melody and peaceful message helped it spread around the world. The song has been performed by artists from nearly every musical genre, ranging from classical choirs to modern pop stars. Its universal themes continue to make it one of the most beloved holiday songs ever written.

Did you know?

“Silent Night” was first performed in Austria in 1818, with lyrics by Joseph Mohr and music by Franz Xaver Gruber, before spreading through translations worldwide.
