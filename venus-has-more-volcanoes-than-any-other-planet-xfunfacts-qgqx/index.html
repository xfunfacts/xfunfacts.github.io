Venus Has More Volcanoes Than Any Other Planet xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Venus has more volcanoes than any other planet in the solar system. Scientists estimate the planet contains tens of thousands of volcanic structures.

Venus contains more volcanoes than any other planet in the Solar System, with scientists estimating that its surface includes tens of thousands of volcanic structures ranging from small volcanic domes to enormous shield volcanoes. Thick clouds hide the planet's landscape from visible light, so researchers rely on radar mapping from spacecraft to study its geology. Evidence suggests that volcanic activity has played a major role in shaping Venus's surface over billions of years, and recent observations even hint that some volcanoes may still be active today. Understanding Venusian volcanism helps scientists compare the geological evolution of rocky planets, including Earth. The planet's immense volcanic landscape makes it one of the most fascinating worlds for planetary geologists studying the forces that shape terrestrial planets.

Did you know?

Radar mapping has revealed tens of thousands of volcanic features on Venus, and recent studies suggest at least some of them may still be active today.
