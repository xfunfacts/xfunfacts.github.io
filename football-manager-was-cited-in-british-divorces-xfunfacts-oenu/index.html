Football Manager Was Cited in British Divorces xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Football Manager was cited as a contributing factor in more than 35 British divorce cases during 2012. The game's addictive nature sometimes affected real relationships.

The football management simulation Football Manager has earned a reputation for being so immersive that it can consume hundreds of hours of players' time. In 2012, reports from legal professionals in the United Kingdom suggested that the game had been mentioned in more than 35 divorce cases as a contributing factor to relationship problems. Although the game itself was not the direct cause of the divorces, excessive play was reportedly linked to neglect of family responsibilities and reduced communication between partners. Like many highly engaging games, Football Manager can encourage long gaming sessions through its detailed strategy, realistic management systems, and constant progression. The story serves as an example of how entertainment can become problematic when it begins interfering with personal relationships and daily life.

Did you know?

Football Manager is famous for its detailed simulation of football management, but the widely repeated claim that it contributed to more than 35 British divorces in 2012 lacks sufficiently documented court records.
