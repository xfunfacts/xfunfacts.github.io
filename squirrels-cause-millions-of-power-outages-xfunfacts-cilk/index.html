Squirrels Cause Millions of Power Outages xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Squirrels are responsible for an estimated 10 to 20 percent of U.S. power outages. Most incidents are small and much easier to repair than storm-related damage.

Squirrels are responsible for an estimated 10 to 20 percent of power outages across the United States. These curious animals often chew through electrical cables or accidentally create short circuits while climbing on transformers and utility equipment. Fortunately, squirrel-related outages are usually limited to small areas and can often be repaired more quickly than damage caused by severe storms. Utility companies have developed protective covers, barriers, and wildlife-friendly equipment to reduce these incidents. The surprising statistic shows how even small animals can have a noticeable effect on large electrical systems.

Did you know?

Squirrels can cause electrical outages by bridging energized equipment with their bodies, though national percentages vary greatly by utility, region, and reporting method.
