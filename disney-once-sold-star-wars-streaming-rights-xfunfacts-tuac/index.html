Disney Once Sold Star Wars Streaming Rights xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Disney once sold the streaming rights to the original Star Wars films, only to later discover it couldn't easily get them back.

Disney's acquisition of Lucasfilm gave the company ownership of the Star Wars franchise, but one important obstacle remained before Disney+ launched. Years earlier, Disney had licensed the streaming rights for the original Star Wars films to Turner Broadcasting under a long-term agreement extending until 2024. At the time, the deal made business sense because Disney had not yet entered the streaming market. However, when the company decided to build Disney+ as its own subscription platform, it wanted the iconic films available exclusively on its new service. Reports indicated that Disney repeatedly attempted to negotiate an early return of the rights, but Turner declined because the agreement remained legally binding. As a result, Disney temporarily owned the Star Wars franchise without controlling all of its streaming distribution. The situation highlighted how media licensing contracts signed years earlier can continue shaping business decisions long after market conditions change. It also demonstrated the growing importance of exclusive content in the competition between major streaming platforms during the rise of online entertainment.

Did you know?

Disney acquired Lucasfilm in 2012, but preexisting distribution agreements complicated control over certain Star Wars films; 20th Century Fox retained distribution rights to the original 1977 film until Disney acquired Fox's entertainment assets in 2019.
