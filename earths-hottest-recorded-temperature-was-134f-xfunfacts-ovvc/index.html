Earth's Hottest Recorded Temperature Was 134°F xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Death Valley recorded Earth's highest official air temperature at 134°F during July 1913.

The highest officially recorded air temperature on Earth reached 134°F (56.7°C) at Furnace Creek in Death Valley, California, in July 1913. Death Valley's unique geography, low elevation, dry climate, and surrounding mountain ranges trap intense heat, making it one of the hottest places on the planet. Despite these extreme conditions, specialized plants and animals have adapted to survive in the harsh desert environment. Scientists continue studying Death Valley to better understand climate, desert ecosystems, and the effects of extreme heat on living organisms.

Did you know?

The 56.7°C reading at Furnace Creek in 1913 remains the official world record, although some scientists continue to debate aspects of the measurement.
