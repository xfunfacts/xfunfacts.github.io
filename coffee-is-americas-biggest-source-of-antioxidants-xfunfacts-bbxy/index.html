Coffee Is America’s Biggest Source of Antioxidants xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Coffee is the largest source of antioxidants in the average American diet. Its ranking comes from how much Americans drink, not because it beats fruits or vegetables.

Although fruits and vegetables contain many powerful antioxidants, coffee is often cited as the largest single source of antioxidants in the average American diet. This is not because coffee contains more antioxidants than berries or leafy greens, but because Americans consume coffee in such large quantities every day. Coffee contains natural compounds called polyphenols, which have antioxidant properties that help protect cells from damage caused by unstable molecules known as free radicals. Researchers continue studying how these compounds may contribute to overall health, although coffee should not replace a balanced diet rich in fruits and vegetables. Drinking moderate amounts of coffee can be part of a healthy lifestyle for many adults, provided excessive sugar and high-calorie additives are limited. The statistic illustrates how eating habits can influence the major sources of nutrients within an entire population.

Did you know?

Coffee has ranked among the largest contributors to dietary antioxidants in some American surveys because it is consumed frequently, although antioxidant intake does not directly measure health benefits.
