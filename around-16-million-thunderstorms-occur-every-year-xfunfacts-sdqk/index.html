Around 16 Million Thunderstorms Occur Every Year xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Around 16 million thunderstorms occur worldwide every year, with about 2,000 active somewhere on Earth at any moment.

Scientists estimate that about 16 million thunderstorms occur worldwide every year, with roughly 2,000 happening at any given moment. These storms form when warm, moist air rises rapidly into cooler parts of the atmosphere, creating towering thunderclouds. Besides producing lightning and heavy rain, thunderstorms play an important role in Earth's water cycle and atmospheric balance. They can also generate hail, tornadoes, and damaging winds under the right conditions.

Did you know?

Thunderstorms are most common in warm, humid regions where rising air can build tall cumulonimbus clouds capable of producing lightning, hail, and heavy rain.
