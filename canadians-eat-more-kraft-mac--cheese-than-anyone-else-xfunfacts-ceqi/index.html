Canadians Eat More Kraft Mac & Cheese Than Anyone Else xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Canadians consume more Kraft Mac & Cheese per person than any other country, making it a national favorite.

Canadians consume more Kraft Mac & Cheese per person than people in any other country, making it one of Canada's best-known packaged foods. The product became especially popular because it is affordable, quick to prepare, and easy to store. Many Canadian families have grown up with it as a familiar comfort food, and it is often affectionately referred to as "KD." Its popularity demonstrates how certain convenience foods can become part of a country's everyday culture and eating habits.

Did you know?

Kraft Dinner became a Canadian household staple partly because its boxed macaroni and cheese offered an inexpensive, shelf-stable meal; the claim of world-leading per-capita consumption depends on brand sales data.
