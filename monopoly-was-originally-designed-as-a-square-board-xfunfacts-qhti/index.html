Monopoly Was Originally Designed as a Square Board xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Monopoly evolved from The Landlord's Game, which originally featured a square board long before the modern version.

The earliest version of Monopoly was based on The Landlord's Game, created by Elizabeth Magie in 1904 to demonstrate ideas about land ownership and economic inequality. Like the modern game, it used a square board with players moving around the edges to buy properties and collect payments. Charles Darrow later adapted and popularized the game before Parker Brothers released Monopoly commercially in the 1930s. Today, Monopoly has become one of the world's best-known board games, translated into dozens of languages and enjoyed by hundreds of millions of players across generations.

Did you know?

Elizabeth Magie patented The Landlord's Game in 1904, decades before Monopoly became commercially successful; her original design included rules intended to demonstrate how different approaches to property ownership affected players.
