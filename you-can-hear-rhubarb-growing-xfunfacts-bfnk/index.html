You Can Hear Rhubarb Growing xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Rhubarb grows so rapidly that its expanding stalks can produce faint creaking and popping sounds under ideal conditions.

Rhubarb can grow so rapidly under ideal conditions that its expanding stalks sometimes produce faint creaking or popping sounds. In specially designed forcing sheds, where warmth and darkness encourage accelerated growth, these subtle noises can occasionally be heard in otherwise quiet surroundings. The sounds result from plant cells rapidly expanding rather than from the stalks rubbing together. Rhubarb's unusual growth rate has fascinated gardeners and scientists alike, making it one of the few plants known for producing audible sounds while growing.

Did you know?

Forced rhubarb can grow rapidly enough in dark, warm sheds to produce audible creaks and pops as expanding stalks and leaves move against one another.
