Earth Has Experienced Multiple Ice Ages xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Earth has experienced at least five major ice ages that dramatically reshaped continents, oceans, and global climates.

Earth has experienced at least five major ice ages during its 4.5-billion-year history, each lasting millions of years and dramatically reshaping the planet. During these periods, vast ice sheets covered large portions of the continents, lowering sea levels and altering global ecosystems. The current geological era is technically part of an ice age because permanent ice still exists at both poles, although humanity is living in a relatively warm interval known as an interglacial period. Studying past ice ages helps scientists better understand long-term climate change and Earth's natural climate cycles.

Did you know?

Earth is currently within an ice age because permanent ice sheets still exist; the warmer interval we live in today is an interglacial period within that larger ice age.
