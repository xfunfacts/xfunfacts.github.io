Coca-Cola Won't Melt a Nail in 4 Days xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Coca-Cola is fairly acidic, but it cannot dissolve a metal nail in four days as internet myths claim. Scientific tests have repeatedly disproved the story.

Coca-Cola is an acidic beverage with a pH generally ranging between about 2.5 and 2.8, largely because of phosphoric acid and dissolved carbon dioxide. This acidity has inspired many internet myths claiming the drink can dissolve a metal nail within just a few days. In reality, controlled experiments have shown that while Coca-Cola can slowly contribute to corrosion over extended periods, it does not rapidly dissolve steel nails as the popular claim suggests. Many acidic household substances, including fruit juices and vinegar, also have relatively low pH values without possessing extraordinary dissolving abilities. The myth illustrates how isolated scientific facts can be exaggerated when removed from proper context.

Did you know?

Coca-Cola contains phosphoric acid and has a pH around 2.5, but acidity alone does not mean it can rapidly dissolve an iron nail; corrosion depends on exposure time, metal composition, and chemical conditions.
