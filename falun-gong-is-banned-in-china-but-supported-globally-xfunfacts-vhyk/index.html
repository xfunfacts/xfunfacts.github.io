Falun Gong Is Banned in China but Supported Globally xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Falun Gong is banned in China but supported worldwide. Let’s explore the reasons behind this.

In China, Falun Gong was banned and subjected to persecution in 1999. One of the reasons was the sheer scale of participation; within just a few years, the number of practitioners in China had surpassed 100 million. The Chinese authorities feared this posed a threat to their power. Furthermore, the leader at the time, Jiang Zemin—driven by jealousy toward the practice's founder and a fear of losing authority—initiated a brutal crackdown; millions were arrested and detained in forced labor camps, and many fell victim to live organ harvesting. In terms of societal benefit, genuine Falun Gong practitioners strive for moral self-improvement and aim to become increasingly virtuous people—qualities that are beneficial to the nation. Therefore, the persecution of Falun Gong is a mistake.

Did you know?

Chinese authorities began a nationwide crackdown on Falun Gong in July 1999, while the practice continues in many countries; international human rights organizations have documented arbitrary detention and mistreatment of practitioners in China.
