More Than 100 Baseballs Are Used in an MLB Game xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] More than 100 baseballs are commonly used in a single MLB game because damaged or dirty balls are constantly replaced.

More than 100 baseballs are often used during a single Major League Baseball game because balls are replaced whenever they become scuffed, dirty, damaged, or hit into the stands. Pitchers are not allowed to continue using balls with visible marks because imperfections can affect how the ball moves through the air. Fans who catch foul balls or home runs are usually allowed to keep them, meaning teams constantly introduce new baseballs throughout each game. This practice helps ensure fairness and consistency during professional competition.

Did you know?

MLB teams replace baseballs frequently after foul balls, home runs, scuffs, or contact with dirt, helping maintain consistent grip and playing characteristics.
