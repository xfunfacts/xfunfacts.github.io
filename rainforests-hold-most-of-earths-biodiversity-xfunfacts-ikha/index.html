Rainforests Hold Most of Earth's Biodiversity xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Rainforests contain more than half of Earth's plant and animal species, making them the planet's richest ecosystems.

Rainforests contain more than half of Earth's plant and animal species despite covering only a small percentage of the planet's land surface. Their warm temperatures, abundant rainfall, and layered vegetation create countless habitats for insects, birds, mammals, reptiles, amphibians, and plants. Many rainforest species remain unknown to science and are still being discovered. These ecosystems also store enormous amounts of carbon, helping regulate Earth's climate. Protecting rainforests safeguards both biodiversity and global environmental health.

Did you know?

Tropical rainforests occupy only a small portion of Earth's land yet contain an exceptionally high proportion of terrestrial species, though the often-quoted "more than half" figure is an estimate.
