Taste Buds Decline with Age xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Taste buds gradually become less sensitive with age, making some foods seem milder than they once did.

As people grow older, both the number and sensitivity of their taste buds gradually decline, making certain flavors less intense than they were during childhood or early adulthood. Changes in smell, which contributes significantly to the perception of flavor, also play an important role in this process. As a result, older adults may notice that foods seem blander and may prefer stronger seasonings or sweeter flavors. Certain medications, illnesses, and smoking can further reduce taste sensitivity. Maintaining good oral health and eating a varied diet can help preserve the enjoyment of food while supporting proper nutrition throughout life.

Did you know?

Taste perception can weaken with age because of changes in taste receptors, smell, saliva, medications, and health, rather than simply because taste buds disappear.
