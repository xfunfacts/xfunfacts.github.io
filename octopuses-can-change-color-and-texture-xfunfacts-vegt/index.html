Octopuses Can Change Color and Texture xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Octopuses rapidly change both color and skin texture to blend into coral reefs, rocks, and the ocean floor.

Octopuses can rapidly change both the color and texture of their skin to blend almost perfectly into their surroundings. Specialized pigment cells called chromatophores work together with reflective cells and tiny muscles to create astonishing camouflage in just seconds. They can imitate rocks, coral, sand, and seaweed while also changing skin texture to match nearby surfaces. This ability helps octopuses avoid predators and surprise prey. Their camouflage is considered one of the most advanced examples found anywhere in the animal kingdom.

Did you know?

Octopuses change color with chromatophores and alter skin texture using muscular structures called papillae, allowing rapid visual and structural camouflage.
