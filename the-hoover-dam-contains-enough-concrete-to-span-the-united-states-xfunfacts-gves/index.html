The Hoover Dam Contains Enough Concrete to Span the United States xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Hoover Dam contains enough concrete to build a two-lane highway stretching across the United States.

The Hoover Dam required an extraordinary amount of concrete during construction in the 1930s. Engineers estimated that the material used could build a two-lane highway stretching from San Francisco to New York City. At the time, it was one of the largest engineering projects ever attempted, providing hydroelectric power, flood control, and water storage for millions of people. The dam remains a landmark of modern engineering and an important part of the American Southwest's infrastructure.

Did you know?

Hoover Dam's concrete was poured in many interlocking blocks rather than as one giant mass, allowing heat from curing concrete to dissipate more safely.
