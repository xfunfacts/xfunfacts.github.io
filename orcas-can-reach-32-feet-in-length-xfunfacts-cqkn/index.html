Orcas Can Reach 32 Feet in Length xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Adult orcas can grow to nearly 32 feet in length, making them the largest dolphins on Earth.

Adult orcas can grow to impressive lengths of up to 32 feet, or nearly 10 meters, making them the largest dolphins on Earth. Males are generally much larger than females and can weigh several tons. Their powerful bodies allow them to hunt a wide variety of prey, including fish, seals, and even large whales. Orcas are found in every ocean and rank among the world's most successful marine predators.

Did you know?

Male orcas are considerably larger than females and can approach 9 to 10 meters in exceptional cases, making orcas the largest members of the dolphin family.
