Octopuses Have No Bones xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Without bones, octopuses can squeeze through openings barely larger than their hard beaks.

Because octopuses have no bones or rigid skeleton, they can squeeze through openings barely larger than their beak, which is the only hard part of their body. This flexibility allows them to escape predators, hide in narrow crevices, and enter places that seem impossibly small. Their soft bodies can change shape dramatically without injury. Combined with exceptional camouflage, this ability makes octopuses extremely difficult to capture. It is one of their most effective survival adaptations.

Did you know?

Because almost all of an octopus is soft tissue, the rigid beak is usually the main structure limiting how small an opening the animal can squeeze through.
