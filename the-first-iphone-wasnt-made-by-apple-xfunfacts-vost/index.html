The First iPhone Wasn't Made by Apple xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Cisco released a product named iPhone in 2006, months before Apple introduced its iconic smartphone under the same name.

Many people assume Apple created the first iPhone, but the name was actually used earlier by Cisco. In 2006, Cisco released an internet phone called the iPhone, months before Apple introduced its revolutionary smartphone in 2007. After Apple's announcement, the two companies reached a trademark agreement allowing both to use the name under specific conditions. Today, Apple's iPhone has become one of the world's most influential consumer technology products despite not being the first device to carry the name.

Did you know?

Cisco's Linksys division sold a VoIP handset called iPhone before Apple's launch, and the two companies later reached an agreement over use of the trademark.
