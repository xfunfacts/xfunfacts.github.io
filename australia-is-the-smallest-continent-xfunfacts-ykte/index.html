Australia Is the Smallest Continent xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Australia is the world's smallest continent and the sixth-largest country, famous for its unique wildlife and landscapes.

Australia is the world's smallest continent while also ranking as the sixth-largest country by land area. Despite its relatively small continental size, Australia covers a vast landscape of deserts, mountains, rainforests, grasslands, and tropical coastlines. Its long geographic isolation allowed many unique plants and animals to evolve independently, including kangaroos, koalas, wombats, and platypuses. The continent is also home to vibrant modern cities alongside enormous protected wilderness areas. Australia's remarkable biodiversity makes it unlike any other continent on Earth.

Did you know?

The Australian continent covers approximately 7.7 million square kilometers, while Antarctica is nearly twice as large despite having no permanent resident population.
