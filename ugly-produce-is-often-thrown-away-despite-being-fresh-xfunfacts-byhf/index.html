Ugly Produce Is Often Thrown Away Despite Being Fresh xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Perfectly edible fruits and vegetables are often thrown away for one simple reason. The scale of this food waste may surprise you.

An astonishing amount of perfectly edible produce never reaches grocery stores simply because it fails to meet cosmetic standards. Fruits and vegetables with unusual shapes, scars, blemishes, or irregular sizes are often rejected during sorting even though they remain just as nutritious and safe to eat as visually perfect produce. Estimates suggest that roughly forty percent of harvested produce is discarded before sale for appearance alone, contributing significantly to global food waste. This unnecessary loss wastes water, farmland, fertilizer, labor, transportation, and energy that were invested in growing the crops. In recent years, some supermarkets and food companies have introduced "ugly produce" programs that sell imperfect fruits and vegetables at lower prices instead of throwing them away. Consumers have also become more aware that natural variation does not affect quality or taste. Reducing cosmetic food waste can improve food security, lower environmental impacts, and help farmers recover more value from every harvest while encouraging shoppers to focus on freshness rather than appearance.

Did you know?

Cosmetic standards imposed by retailers can cause edible fruits and vegetables to be rejected before sale, even when minor blemishes or unusual shapes have little effect on their nutritional value or safety.
