Cats Have 32 Muscles in Each Ear xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Cats have 32 muscles in each ear, allowing them to rotate their ears with remarkable precision toward sounds.

Each of a cat's ears contains 32 muscles, allowing the animal to rotate its ears through nearly 180 degrees with remarkable precision. These muscles help cats pinpoint the exact location of faint sounds, an ability that makes them highly effective hunters even in darkness. Cats can also move each ear independently, enabling them to monitor multiple sound sources at the same time. This extraordinary hearing system is one of the key adaptations that has contributed to the success of domestic and wild cats alike.

Did you know?

Cats can rotate each ear independently, helping them pinpoint sounds without turning their entire head toward the source.
