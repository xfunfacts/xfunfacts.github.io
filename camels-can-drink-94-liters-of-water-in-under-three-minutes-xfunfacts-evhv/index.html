Camels Can Drink 94 Liters of Water in Under Three Minutes xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A thirsty camel can drink about 94 liters of water in under three minutes after long periods without hydration.

A thirsty camel can drink around 94 liters of water in less than three minutes, an extraordinary ability that allows it to recover quickly after traveling long distances without access to water. Contrary to popular belief, camels do not store water in their humps. Instead, the humps store fat that can be converted into energy when food is scarce. Camels survive dehydration because their bodies tolerate water loss that would be fatal to most mammals, and their uniquely shaped red blood cells continue flowing efficiently even when blood becomes highly concentrated. After reaching a water source, they rapidly replace lost fluids without suffering the dangerous effects that sudden rehydration would cause in many other animals. Combined with efficient kidneys, reduced sweating, and the ability to fluctuate body temperature, this remarkable drinking capacity makes camels perfectly suited for survival in arid deserts.

Did you know?

Camels can tolerate losing a much greater percentage of their body water than most mammals, an adaptation that helps them survive prolonged desert journeys.
