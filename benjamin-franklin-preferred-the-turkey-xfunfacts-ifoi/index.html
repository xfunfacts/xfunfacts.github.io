Benjamin Franklin Preferred the Turkey xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Benjamin Franklin believed the turkey was a better national symbol for America than the bald eagle.

Benjamin Franklin once expressed his opinion that the wild turkey would have made a more respectable national symbol for the United States than the bald eagle. In a famous letter, he criticized the eagle as a bird that often stole food from others, while describing the turkey as courageous and native to North America. Franklin was never officially involved in choosing the national emblem, but his humorous comments became one of the best-known stories in American history. The bald eagle ultimately remained the nation's enduring symbol.

Did you know?

Franklin criticized the bald eagle's character in a private letter and praised the turkey, but he never formally proposed replacing the eagle as America's national emblem.
