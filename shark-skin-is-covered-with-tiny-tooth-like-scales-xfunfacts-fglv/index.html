Shark Skin Is Covered with Tiny Tooth-Like Scales xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Shark skin is covered with microscopic tooth-like scales that reduce drag and improve swimming efficiency.

Shark skin is covered with millions of microscopic structures called dermal denticles, which resemble tiny teeth rather than ordinary fish scales. These hard, tooth-like scales reduce water resistance, allowing sharks to swim more efficiently while also protecting the skin from injury and parasites. Engineers have even studied dermal denticles to inspire more efficient swimsuits, aircraft surfaces, and ship coatings. This remarkable skin structure is one of the many adaptations that contribute to the shark's success as a marine predator.

Did you know?

Shark skin is covered with dermal denticles, tiny tooth-like structures that can reduce turbulence and protect the body from abrasion.
