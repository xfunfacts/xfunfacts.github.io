Humans Have Far More Than Five Senses xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Humans have many more than five senses, including balance, body position, temperature, pain, hunger, and thirst.

Humans possess far more than the traditional five senses taught in elementary school. In addition to sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch, the body can detect balance, body position, pain, temperature, hunger, thirst, and many other internal signals. Specialized receptors continuously provide the brain with information that helps maintain posture, regulate body functions, and respond to changing conditions. Modern neuroscience continues discovering new sensory systems, revealing that human perception is far more complex than once believed.

Did you know?

Beyond sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch, humans also sense balance, body position, temperature, pain, internal organ states, hunger, and thirst.
