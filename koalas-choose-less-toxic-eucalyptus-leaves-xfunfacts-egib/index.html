Koalas Choose Less Toxic Eucalyptus Leaves xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Koalas use their powerful sense of smell to choose eucalyptus leaves with fewer toxins and better nutrition.

Koalas use an excellent sense of smell to select eucalyptus leaves with lower concentrations of toxins and higher nutritional value. Even among the same tree species, leaf chemistry can vary depending on age, season, and growing conditions. By carefully choosing the safest and most nutritious leaves, koalas reduce the harmful effects of toxic compounds while maximizing the limited energy available from their diet. This selective feeding behavior is essential for their survival. Their powerful nose is one of their most valuable adaptations.

Did you know?

Koalas often sniff leaves before eating them, using chemical cues to judge which eucalyptus leaves are safer and more nutritious.
