A Group of Unicorns Is Called a Blessing xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] A group of unicorns is traditionally called a blessing. The whimsical collective noun matches the mythical creature's magical reputation.

Although unicorns exist only in mythology, English gives them a wonderfully fitting collective noun. A group of unicorns is traditionally called a "blessing," reflecting the magical, pure, and enchanting image these legendary creatures have carried for centuries. Collective nouns have long been part of English folklore, producing imaginative names such as a murder of crows, a parliament of owls, and an exaltation of larks. While a blessing of unicorns has no scientific basis, it continues to appear in fantasy literature, games, and popular culture. The phrase captures the sense of wonder associated with one of mythology's most beloved creatures and remains a delightful example of the creativity found within the English language.

Did you know?

A blessing of unicorns is a playful collective noun associated with modern fantasy vocabulary, not a formally established zoological term, because unicorns are mythical creatures rather than a recognized animal species.
