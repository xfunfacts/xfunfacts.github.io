Female Kangaroos Carry Joeys in a Pouch xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Female kangaroos carry developing joeys in a pouch that provides warmth, protection, and nourishment.

Female kangaroos carry their developing young, called joeys, inside a protective pouch located on the front of their bodies. After birth, the tiny joey crawls into the pouch, where it continues growing while feeding on its mother's milk. The pouch provides warmth, protection, and easy access to nourishment during the early stages of development. As the joey grows stronger, it begins leaving the pouch for short periods before eventually becoming independent. This remarkable reproductive strategy is a defining feature of marsupials.

Did you know?

A kangaroo pouch contains several teats, and a mother can produce different types of milk at the same time for joeys of different ages.
