Tomatoes Have More Genes Than Humans xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Tomatoes contain more genes than humans, showing that gene count does not determine an organism's complexity.

Tomatoes contain around 31,000 genes, significantly more than the roughly 20,000 protein-coding genes found in humans. This surprising fact does not mean tomatoes are more complex organisms, since biological complexity depends on how genes function and interact rather than their total number. Plants often possess large gene families that help them adapt to changing environments, resist disease, and produce fruits. The tomato genome has become an important resource for scientists working to improve crop quality, nutrition, and agricultural sustainability.

Did you know?

Tomatoes have roughly 35,000 protein-coding genes, while humans have about 20,000, showing that gene count alone does not determine biological complexity
