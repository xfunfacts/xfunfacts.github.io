Earth's Circumference Is About 24,901 Miles xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Earth's circumference measures about 24,901 miles around the equator, a distance first estimated over 2,000 years ago.

Earth's circumference measures about 24,901 miles, or roughly 40,075 kilometers, around the equator. Ancient Greek scholar Eratosthenes calculated this distance more than 2,200 years ago using shadows, geometry, and simple observations, producing an impressively accurate estimate. Today, satellites provide extremely precise measurements, but his achievement remains one of history's greatest scientific accomplishments. Earth's circumference also forms the basis for navigation, mapping, and many modern geographic calculations.

Did you know?

Earth's equatorial circumference is approximately 40,075 kilometers, while its polar circumference is slightly shorter because the planet bulges at the equator due to rotation.
