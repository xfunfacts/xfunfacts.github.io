Starfish Don't Have Brains xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Starfish survive without brains by using a simple nervous system that controls movement and senses.

Although starfish do not have brains, they are far from simple creatures. Instead of a centralized brain, they rely on a network of nerves connected to a nerve ring surrounding their mouth, allowing them to coordinate movement and respond to their environment. Hundreds of tiny tube feet help them crawl, grip surfaces, and even pry open shellfish. Some species can regenerate lost arms, and in certain cases an entirely new starfish can grow from a remaining arm if part of the central body survives. Their unusual biology continues to fascinate marine scientists.

Did you know?

Sea stars lack a centralized brain but possess a nerve ring and radial nerves that coordinate movement, feeding, and responses to light and touch.
