Our Solar System Has a Boundary xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The heliopause marks the outer boundary of our Solar System, where the Sun's influence gives way to interstellar space.

Our Solar System does not simply fade into empty space. Instead, it has a distant boundary called the heliopause, where the outward flow of charged particles from the Sun, known as the solar wind, is finally balanced by particles drifting through interstellar space. Beyond this point begins the environment between the stars. NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft crossed the heliopause in 2012, becoming the first human-made object to enter interstellar space. Studying this boundary helps scientists better understand how the Sun interacts with the rest of the galaxy and how cosmic radiation reaches our Solar System.

Did you know?

The heliopause is not a rigid shell; its shape changes as solar activity varies and as the Solar System moves through the surrounding interstellar medium.
