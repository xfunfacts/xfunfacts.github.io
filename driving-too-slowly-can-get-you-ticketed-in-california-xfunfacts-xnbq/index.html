Driving Too Slowly Can Get You Ticketed in California xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Driving far below the speed of traffic can result in a ticket in California. Motorists may be cited if they unnecessarily block the normal flow of vehicles.

Driving significantly below the speed of surrounding traffic can sometimes be just as dangerous as speeding. California law allows police officers to issue citations when drivers move so slowly that they obstruct the normal flow of traffic without a valid reason. The rule is designed to reduce congestion, prevent sudden lane changes, and minimize the risk of rear-end collisions caused by unexpected slow-moving vehicles. There are, of course, exceptions for poor weather, road hazards, heavy traffic, or mechanical problems. The law reminds drivers that safe driving is not only about obeying maximum speed limits but also about maintaining a reasonable speed that matches road conditions and surrounding traffic.

Did you know?

California law focuses on impeding normal traffic rather than simply being below a posted speed, so context matters when unusually slow driving becomes an offense.
