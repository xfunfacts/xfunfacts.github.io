The Record for Pancakes Flipped in One Minute Is 140 xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The world record for pancake flips in one minute is an astonishing 140 successful flips.

The current world record for the most pancake flips in one minute stands at an astonishing 140. Achieving this feat requires remarkable coordination, speed, and precision, as every pancake must be successfully tossed and caught according to official record rules. Competitive pancake flipping has become a popular attraction at charity events, festivals, and culinary competitions around the world. While it may seem like a lighthearted challenge, mastering the technique takes practice, timing, and consistent control over both the pan and the pancake.

Did you know?

Australian chef Brad Jolly achieved 140 complete pancake tosses in one minute in Sydney in 2012; ten additional attempts were disqualified because the pancake failed to complete the required rotation.
