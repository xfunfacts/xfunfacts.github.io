Your Brain Uses About 20% of Your Body's Oxygen xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Although it makes up only about 2% of body weight, your brain uses around 20% of the body's oxygen at rest.

Although the brain represents only about 2 percent of total body weight, it consumes roughly 20 percent of the body's oxygen while at rest. This high demand reflects the constant activity of billions of nerve cells that communicate through electrical and chemical signals every second. Oxygen is essential for producing the energy needed to support memory, movement, sensation, and countless other brain functions. Even brief interruptions in oxygen supply can quickly affect brain performance, demonstrating why healthy circulation and adequate breathing are vital for normal cognitive function throughout life.

Did you know?

The brain receives approximately 15% of resting cardiac output and consumes about 20% of the body's oxygen, making interrupted blood flow especially dangerous to brain tissue.
