An Ostrich Egg Takes Much Longer to Boil Than a Chicken Egg xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] An ostrich egg can take up to two hours to hard-boil because of its enormous size and thick shell.

Because of its enormous size, an ostrich egg takes much longer to boil than a chicken egg. A soft-boiled ostrich egg may require about 50 minutes, while a fully hard-boiled one can take close to two hours. Weighing around 1.4 kilograms on average, a single ostrich egg is roughly equivalent to two dozen chicken eggs. Its thick shell, which can be several millimeters thick, is strong enough to support the weight of an adult person under the right conditions. Beyond cooking, emptied ostrich eggshells have also been used throughout history as decorative objects, containers, and works of art in various cultures.

Did you know?

An ostrich egg can weigh more than 1.4 kilograms, equivalent to roughly two dozen chicken eggs, and its thick shell and large volume require considerably longer cooking times.
