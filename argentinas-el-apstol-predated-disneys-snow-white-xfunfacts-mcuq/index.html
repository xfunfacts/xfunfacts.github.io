Argentina's El Apóstol Predated Disney's Snow White xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Argentina's El Apóstol became the first feature-length animated film years before Disney released Snow White.

Released in 1917, Argentina's El Apóstol was a full-length animated feature created years before Disney's Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs. Produced using approximately 58,000 hand-drawn illustrations, the political satire demonstrated that feature-length animation was possible long before it became commercially popular. Unfortunately, no known copies survive because the film was destroyed in a fire, making it one of cinema's greatest lost works. Historians still recognize its important place in animation history.

Did you know?

Argentina's El Apóstol premiered in 1917 and is generally regarded as the first feature-length animated film, though no complete copy is known to survive today.
