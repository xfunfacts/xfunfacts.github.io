The Moon Orbits Earth Every 27.32 Days xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] The Moon completes one orbit around Earth every 27.32 days relative to the distant stars.

Relative to the distant stars, the Moon completes one orbit around Earth approximately every 27.32 days, a period known as the sidereal month. However, because Earth is also moving around the Sun, the familiar cycle from one full Moon to the next takes about 29.5 days, known as the synodic month. The Moon's orbit influences tides, stabilizes Earth's axial tilt, and has shaped calendars and cultures for thousands of years. Its gravitational relationship with Earth continues to be one of the most important interactions within our solar system.

Did you know?

The Moon takes about 29.5 days to return to the same phase because Earth and the Moon are both moving around the Sun during each lunar orbit.
