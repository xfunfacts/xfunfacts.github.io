Nutella Sells Enough to Cover the Great Wall xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] Nutella is consumed on an astonishing scale every year. The numbers behind the world's favorite chocolate spread are far bigger than most people expect.

Nutella is one of the world's most popular chocolate-hazelnut spreads, selling in such enormous quantities each year that the total production reaches astonishing proportions. Estimates suggest the annual volume could cover the length of the Great Wall of China multiple times if spread out, while the total weight is often compared to that of the Empire State Building. The product's popularity also explains why the company purchases roughly one-quarter of the world's hazelnut supply each year. This massive demand has encouraged agricultural research into improving hazelnut production and protecting crops from disease and climate challenges. Since its introduction in the 1960s, Nutella has become a household staple in many countries, enjoyed on bread, pancakes, pastries, and desserts. Its global success demonstrates how a single food product can influence international agriculture, manufacturing, and consumer markets on an extraordinary scale.

Did you know?

Nutella's enormous annual sales have inspired comparisons with the Great Wall of China, but such claims depend on package dimensions and sales figures, so they should not be presented as verified physical measurements.
