Octopuses Have Nine Brains xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] An octopus has one central brain and eight arm nerve centers, creating its remarkable coordination and flexibility.

An octopus has one central brain along with large clusters of nerve cells in each of its eight arms, leading many people to describe it as having nine brains. While only one true brain controls the animal, the arm nerve centers can process information and coordinate movement independently. This allows each arm to explore and manipulate objects with remarkable flexibility while remaining connected to the central nervous system. The arrangement contributes to the octopus's extraordinary dexterity and problem-solving abilities. It is one of the most unusual nervous systems in the animal kingdom.

Did you know?

An octopus does not literally have nine separate brains; it has one central brain plus large neural networks in each arm that can process sensory information locally.
