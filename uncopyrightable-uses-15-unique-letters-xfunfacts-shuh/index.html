Uncopyrightable Uses 15 Unique Letters xFunFacts

The fact

[xFunFacts] "Uncopyrightable" is famous as the only common 15-letter English word with no repeated letters. Every letter appears exactly once.

The English word "uncopyrightable" is famous among language enthusiasts because it contains 15 letters without repeating a single one. Every letter appears exactly once, making it one of the longest commonly recognized English words with this unusual property. The word itself refers to something that cannot be protected by copyright law, but its spelling has attracted far more attention than its legal meaning. Linguists and puzzle lovers often use it as an example when discussing unusual vocabulary patterns and letter distributions in English. Although a few obscure technical words share similar characteristics, "uncopyrightable" remains the best-known example because it appears in standard dictionaries and is familiar to many English speakers.

Did you know?

“Uncopyrightable” has 15 letters with no repeated letter, a rare isogram pattern, although it is not the only English word that can achieve long nonrepeating sequences.
